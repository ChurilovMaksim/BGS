3. Frozen generator: Jacobi-chain multiplicity and exact kick-rank boundary (`BGS-FM-005`); nonlinear classical bracket pressure and its normalization boundary (`BGS-FM-006`).
4. Seven-cut generator and full-propagator local-law transfer at the exact fixed coupling (`BGS-FM-007`); algebraic spectral simplicity away from seven (`BGS-FM-008`) and the common antiunitary certificate (`BGS-FM-009`). Keep every quantitative/qualitative boundary explicit.

6. Exact moment constraints, transfer bounds, and loop-test falsifiers (`BGS-FM-003`).
7. Mixed-resolvent identities and three-orientation barrier (`BGS-FM-004`).
8. Exact trace-to-local-statistics bridge (`BGS-TRACE-001`–`004`).
9. External loop-characterization input and its exact hypothesis map (`BGS-LOOP-001`).
10. Clustered Abel transform and the positive-time counterexample (`BGS-TRACE-006`).
11. Frontier: the missing model estimate `BGS-TRACE-005`.
12. Explicit statement that `BGS-BGS-001` remains open.

The paper should be framed as a rigorous frontier/obstruction paper, not as a
positive universality theorem until the excluded open claims are closed.
