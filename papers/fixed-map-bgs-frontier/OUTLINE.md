1. Frozen quantization and raw-to-midpoint Weyl dictionary.
2. Classical Anosov dynamics and symmetry elimination (`BGS-FM-001`).
3. Frozen generator: Jacobi-chain multiplicity and exact kick-rank boundary (`BGS-FM-005`); nonlinear classical bracket pressure and its normalization boundary (`BGS-FM-006`).
4. Pell crossover and the weak-kick obstruction (`BGS-FM-002`).
5. Exact moment constraints, transfer bounds, and loop-test falsifiers (`BGS-FM-003`).
6. Mixed-resolvent identities and three-orientation barrier (`BGS-FM-004`).
7. Exact trace-to-local-statistics bridge (`BGS-TRACE-001`–`004`).
8. External loop-characterization input and its exact hypothesis map (`BGS-LOOP-001`).
9. Clustered Abel transform and the positive-time counterexample (`BGS-TRACE-006`).
10. Frontier: the missing model estimate `BGS-TRACE-005`.
11. Explicit statement that `BGS-BGS-001` remains open.

The paper should be framed as a rigorous frontier/obstruction paper, not as a
positive universality theorem until the excluded open claims are closed.
