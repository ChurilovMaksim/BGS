In the one-exponent case needed here: a nonzero algebraic beta, exponent-field degree d, conjugate bound c, and integer q>0 with q beta integral give

|P(exp(beta))| >= H^(-3dS) exp(-(cqS)^(18S)), S=6dD,

for a nonzero integer polynomial P of degree at most D and coefficient absolute values at most H, with positive integer D,H. The candidate uses P=Z+1 and D=H=1, degree d<=2L, c=2h_L and the actual q=abs(a_d)<=h_L. This yields the asserted exp(-(24L h_L^2)^(216L)) bound by monotone enlargement. Integrality is proved for q, not for its numerical upper bound h_L.
