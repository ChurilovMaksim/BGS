## 1. Exact derivative parameter

Let `E=E21`, `kappa=tau/(2*pi)`, and `Phi=Phi_g^kappa`. The independently verified Hessian gives

    D Phi(P)=I+xE,
    x=-(kappa)*g_qq(P)=(9803/125)*tau*pi,
    P=(0,0).

The sign is positive at the specified positive tau. Since `E^2=0`, the derivative formula is exact for every real flow time; no small-coupling error is present. S and Phi both fix P.

At the literal nonzero rational tau, x is transcendental. The registered Hermite–Lindemann implication suffices: if pi were algebraic, the nonzero algebraic number `i*pi` would have transcendental exponential, contradicting `exp(i*pi)=-1`. Multiplying pi by a nonzero rational preserves transcendence. No Diophantine approximation or lower bound on a transcendental expression is required.

This extra arithmetic step is important: unlike the kick-only K1 argument, the fixed-word proof should not be announced for *every* nonzero real tau on the basis of this reasoning. It works at the exact target and, equally, at every nonzero algebraic tau.

## 2. All-word leading coefficient, including negative exponents

In the abstract free product of the two infinite cyclic groups, cyclic reduction makes a nontrivial word conjugate to a pure nonzero power or to

    C(x)=S^(a_1) (I+xE)^(b_1) ... S^(a_k) (I+xE)^(b_k),

where all a_j and b_j are nonzero integers. There is no omitted alternating-word case: an alternating cyclic word can be cyclically rotated to begin with an S block and end with a T block.

Nilpotence gives `(I+xE)^b=I+b*x*E` for every integer b, including negative b. Also `S^-1=[[3,-4],[-2,3]]` has integral entries, so the trace is an integer-coefficient polynomial of degree at most k. To obtain degree k one must select the x term from every T block; there is only this one selection. Its coefficient is

    (product_j b_j) Tr(S^(a_1)E ... S^(a_k)E).

For every 2-by-2 A, `E A E=A_12 E` and `Tr(AE)=A_12`. Applying these identities successively yields

    [x^k] Tr C(x) = (product_j b_j) (product_j (S^(a_j))_12).

For `lambda=3+2*sqrt(2)`,

    (S^a)_12=(lambda^a-lambda^-a)/sqrt(2),

which is nonzero for every nonzero integer a. This also verifies the factor `1/sqrt(2)` in the written candidate. Thus the leading coefficient cannot cancel, and the degree is exactly k.

A nonconstant polynomial with algebraic coefficients cannot have an algebraic value at a transcendental input: otherwise that input would satisfy a nonzero polynomial over the algebraic numbers and hence be algebraic. Therefore `Tr C(x)` is transcendental, in particular unequal to the identity trace 2. A pure S power is nonidentity because S is hyperbolic; a pure T power is `I+b*x*E != I`. Conjugation preserves nonidentity.

This proves the derivative representation is faithful for *every* abstract word, not only a finite list or words with positive exponents. Derivatives at the shared fixed point form a genuine representation of the group of maps fixing P, so the same faithfulness holds for the real-analytic torus maps generated by S and Phi.

## 3. Composition order in F8–F9

The candidate uses the convention

    K* Op(a) K = Op(a composed with F_K) + o_op(1).

With this convention,

    (KL)* Op(a) (KL)
      = L* [K* Op(a) K] L
      = Op(a composed with F_K composed with F_L) + o_op(1).

Thus `F_(KL)=F_K composed with F_L`, exactly as F9 states. The actual generator maps are `F_Q=Phi^-1` and `F_M=S^-1`, exactly as F8 states. Inverse letters quantize the inverse maps, obtained by conjugating the original Egorov identity and composing the observable with the inverse classical map.

It follows that a quantum word is evaluated in the same written order at the inverse generator maps. Inverting both abstract generators is an automorphism of the free group, so a nontrivial word remains nontrivial. The F2 faithfulness proof therefore applies directly.

For comparison only: with the *other* convention `K Op(a) K*=Op(a composed with H_K)`, one has `H_(KL)=H_L composed with H_K`. This reversal was a potential ambiguity in an informal handoff, but is **not an error in the written F8–F9**, which consistently uses inverse conjugation.

The fixed-word composition argument asks for finitely many applications of Egorov to fixed smooth symbols. No control of word length growing with N has been used or obtained.

## 4. Trace, free Haar moments, and relative spectra

Every nontrivial resulting classical word is a nonidentity analytic torus map. Its fixed set has zero area by the analytic embedding-coordinate zero-set argument. Applying the audited overlap lemma against the identity gives

    Tr W_N(w)/N -> 0

for every fixed nonidentity abstract word. An identity word evaluates to I exactly because Q_N and M_N are unitary and inverse pairs cancel. This is precisely the canonical free-group trace, hence precisely convergence in normalized *-moments to two free Haar unitaries. No assertion about operator norms of general noncommutative polynomials follows.

Every nontrivial free-group word has infinite order: cyclically reducing it gives a nonempty cyclic word whose positive powers cannot fully cancel, and negative powers follow by inversion. Thus the theorem applies to every nonzero fixed power of a fixed nontrivial word, proving its macroscopic Haar eigenphase law.

If v and w are distinct elements of the abstract free group, `v w^-1` is nontrivial. Right multiplication gives the exact identity

    [W_N(v)-z W_N(w)] W_N(w)* = W_N(v w^-1)-zI.

Uniform circular-arc discrepancy, the chord change of variables, and the exact singular-value rank variational principle now yield F2–F4 with precisely the formulas in the candidate. Uniformity is in the scalar phase z, for each fixed pair of words. It is not uniform over all words.

The isolated N=7 scalar Q_N and all other small-dimensional coincidences are compatible with the theorem. The same all-odd-N Fourier calculus covers seven-divisible subsequences; no simplicity assumption is needed.

## 5. Independent echo-power check

Let `V_(N,m)=U_N^m M_N^-m` for fixed nonzero m. It is the nontrivial free word `(qm)^m m^-m`, so the fixed-word theorem immediately supplies its circular law and the chord/rank limits for `U_N^m-z M_N^m`.

There is also a valid standalone derivative calculation. Using the positive-conjugation convention just for this calculation gives

    E_m = S^-m composed with G^m,
    G = S composed with Phi,
    D E_m(P) = S^-m [S(I+xE)]^m.

For m>=2, the degree-m coefficient of the derivative trace is

    Tr(S^-m (SE)^m)
      = 4^(m-1) Tr(S^(1-m)E)
      = 4^(m-1) (S^(1-m))_12 != 0,

because `(SE)^m=4^(m-1)SE`. Its trace is therefore transcendental and its derivative has infinite order: a finite-order matrix has roots of unity as eigenvalues and therefore algebraic trace. Since P is fixed, `D(E_m^ell)(P)=(D E_m(P))^ell`, excluding identity maps at every nonzero iterate. For m=1, `E_1=Phi` and the nilpotent argument handles all iterates. Negative m follows from the exact unitary identity

    V_(N,-k)=M_N^-k V_(N,k)* M_N^k.

This check agrees with, and is subsumed by, the general freeness proof.

## 6. Safe endpoint of the result

The strongest established statement here is qualitative fixed-word normalized *-moment convergence, with macroscopic eigenphase and uniform scalar-comparison singular/rank laws for any specified fixed nontrivial relative word. It rules out fixed-word relations and fixed-word scalar returns on infinite odd-N subsequences, including rank-o(N) approximate scalar returns in the stated norms.

It supplies no effective startup N, no growth window for word length or moments, no uniformity in tau, no strong freeness, and no microscopic BGS conclusion. In particular a relation or near-relation with word length depending on N lies outside the theorem. TRACE-005 remains open.
