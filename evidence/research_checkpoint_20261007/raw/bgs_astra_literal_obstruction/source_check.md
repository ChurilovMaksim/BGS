The checked corollary states that a nonzero algebraic complex exponent has a transcendental exponential. If pi were algebraic, nonzero i*pi would be algebraic, while exp(i*pi)=-1 is algebraic, a contradiction. Multiplication by the nonzero rational (9803/125)10^(-80) preserves transcendence. A nonconstant rational polynomial evaluated at this x cannot be algebraic, since otherwise x would solve a polynomial over the algebraic numbers and hence be algebraic.
