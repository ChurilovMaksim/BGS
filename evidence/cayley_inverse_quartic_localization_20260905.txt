4. Universal pair-sum discriminant identity regression

Before promotion, the exact identity
  Disc(S_F)=Disc(F)^(m-2) Q_F^2
was independently expanded with exact SymPy integer arithmetic on test root sets of sizes m=4,5,6.  In each case the symbolic/integer difference was exactly zero.

This finite regression is not the proof.  The proof in mathematics/cayley_inverse_quartic_localization.md partitions pairs of pair-sum roots into intersecting and disjoint graph edges.

PERFORMANCE NOTE

Repeated exact SymPy resultants/factorizations become the dominant cost near K~100 when all O(K^2) reduced edges are swept.  An attempted larger brute-force batch was stopped rather than silently truncating.  The scalable route should use a specialized modular or fraction-free resultant/norm kernel in compiled code, while preserving the same-embedding promotion gate.

NON-CLAIMS

- No asymptotic largest-prime theorem is claimed.
- No claim that rational gcd(N(w_e),N(w_f)) equals an ideal contraction is made.
- No data from interrupted computations are used.
- The K=89 witness does not disprove K^(4+o(1)); it only disproves a strict finite K^4 ceiling.
- No BGS/GOE/COE conclusion follows from these finite arithmetic certificates.
