## 2. The x-sequence and its reflection

x_{u+3}=−x_u (α³≡−1 mod 5) and x_2=4x_0+x_1 mod 5
(from α²=2+2√2 mod 5: Tr(Aα²)=4Tr(A)+Tr(Aα)).
Lemma 1 (fibres ≤2). For A≠0 every U_v has size ≤2.
Proof: |U_v|=n_v+n_{−v}, n_v=#{u∈{0,1,2}: x_u=v}. |U_v|≥3 forces
(n_v,n_{−v})∈{(2,1),(1,2)}; each of the six subcases contradicts
x_2=4x_0+x_1 unless v=0, and n_0=2 forces x_0=x_1=x_2=0 hence A=0
((Tr A,Tr Aα) determines A).
Lemma 2 (ρ₆). For every A there is a reflection ρ₆(u)=−u+c₆ mod 6
with x∘ρ₆=x; it swaps within each pair fibre and fixes each singleton.
Proof: x_{−u+c}=Tr(Aα^cᾱ^u)=Tr(Āᾱ^cα^u), so stabilisation ⟺
Āᾱ^c=A ⟺ ᾱ^c=A/Ā; the norm-1 subgroup of (R/5)^× is cyclic of order
6, generated by α, so A/Ā∈⟨α⟩ and c₆ exists. If u is fixed
(c₆=2u) and x_u=0, then Aα^u=w√2, so A/Ā=−ᾱ^{2u}=−ᾱ^{c₆}≠ᾱ^{c₆}
in characteristic 5, contradiction; hence fixed points have x_u≠0,
and a pair fibre cannot be pointwise fixed (that would force
f₂=f₁+3, but x_{f₁+3}=−x_{f₁}≠x_{f₁}). So fixed points = singletons.
Lemma 3. c₆ even ⟺ M≠N, where M=S_0, N=S_1.
Proof: c₆ odd makes ρ₆ swap parity, so {x_u: u even}={x_u: u odd},
i.e. M=N; contrapositive gives the claim.
Henceforth assume M≠N (else Stab(S)=D_4 and Π_1∩Π_2≠∅ trivially)
and B≠0 (else Π_2=D_4). Then Stab(S)=K={(1,0),(1,2),(−1,0),(−1,2)},
the k-even elements of D_4, and c₆ is even.

## 3. D₆ assembly

Lemma 4. (I) ⟹ Y∘φ=Y or Y∘φ=Y∘ρ₆.
Proof by finite case analysis: (I) and the conclusion depend only on
the equality pattern of Y, i.e. a set partition of ℤ/6 (203 of them),
together with A (12 non-degenerate) and φ∈D₆ (12). All 6,024
(I)-satisfying cases satisfy the conclusion; 0 failures.
Remark: non-degeneracy is essential. For arbitrary Y-tuples the lemma
is false at degenerate A, e.g. A=(0,1), φ=shift by 3, fibres
{0,3},{1,2},{4,5}, Y=[1,2,1,0,2,1] satisfies (I) but Y∘φ is neither

## 4. Bridge: from Y-invariance to π_e ∈ Stab(T)·K

π_e=(ε,k); k even ⟹ π_e∈K, done. Assume k odd. Then need a k-odd
element of Stab(T), which holds ⟺ T_0=T_1 or T_0=−T_1:
ρ₁=(−1,1)∈Stab(T) ⟺ T_0=T_1; ρ₃=(−1,3)∈Stab(T) ⟺ T_0=−T_1;
rotations (1,1),(1,3) force T_0=T_1 resp. T_0=−T_1 as well.
Write z_w=Tr(Bα^w) mod 11 (z_{w+1}=6z_w−z_{w−1}, z_{w+6}=−z_w).
Case 1 (Y∘φ=Y), ε=1 (φ a shift by odd k): k=±1 makes φ generate ℤ/6,
so all Y_u equal {c,−c}; then z_1=6z_0+z_5∈{±7c,±5c}∉{±c} unless

5y_u=±2x_u for w_u=Bα^u, i.e. w_u∈L_+∪L_− (5y=2x resp. 5y=−2x);

Case 2 (Y∘φ=Y∘ρ₆): Y is invariant under ψ′=φ∘ρ₆ (compose with ρ₆).

Lemma 5 (reflection Möbius). Let Ψ(w)=−w+K′ on ℤ/12, K′ odd, with
z_{Ψ(w)}=σ_w z_w, σ_w∈{±1}, for all w with z_w≠0, B≠0. Then σ_w≡σ
constant, and T_0=±T_1.
Proof: t_w=Bα^w, λ=B/B̄ (norm 1), μ=t_w/t̄_w=λα^{2w}. Expanding
z_{Ψ(w)}=λ⁻¹ᾱ^{K′}t_w+λα^{K′}t̄_w=σ_w(t_w+t̄_w) gives, for μ≠−1,
σ_w=f(μ)=(aμ+b)/(μ+1), a=ᾱ^{K′}λ⁻¹, b=α^{K′}λ. μ takes 6 distinct
values (α² has order 6 mod 11); μ=−1 ⟺ z_w=0, at most one w mod 6.
So f(μ)∈{±1} at ≥5 points; a non-constant Möbius function takes each
value at most once, so f is constant, a=b, λ²=ᾱ^{2K′}, σ_w≡σ.
Ψ sends T_0-positions {0,4,8} to T_1-positions {1,5,9} if K′≡1 mod 4
