# A necessary discriminator for future Work proposals

**Use this as a proof-admission test, not as a claimed solution or a mandated proof technique.**

For every fixed nonzero nonnegative smooth weight w in a positive compact time band, there is a changed unitary sequence at O_w(1/N) operator distance from the actual U_N whose genuine occupation-mismatched, h>floor(.49m), s>floor(kappa N/log N) signed N^-2 aggregate has positive liminf, although the matching damped endpoint tends to zero. The fixed kappa must be chosen within the inherited gap's admissible range. An additional O(N^-3) rational phase split makes the spectrum simple and every distinct-eigenvalue ratio non-root-of-unity without changing the conclusion.

The changed sequence retains:

- the same fixed-symbol principal classical dynamics and first-order Egorov scale;
- entrywise O(N^-1/2) flatness, the strong position gap, reverse-kernel decay, and a uniform one-step nonzero-charge gap;
- the same ordinary-Weyl compact charge-pinch limit, within its untwisted scope;
- all fixed normalized kick/cat word limits and the reviewed uniform window floor(log log log N/(4 log 8));
- any **separately given** uniform Schatten S_p bound on the charge pinching, for fixed p>2;
- exact occupation projection, generic generating-transfer identities, checksum-free low-switch removal, and the all-switch damped contraction;
- after the small split, simple spectrum and absence of exact positive scalar powers.

Therefore a proposed endpoint proof based only on those retained statements cannot work. It must identify an additional hypothesis or estimate that the changed sequence fails, and show exactly where that information enters the signed boundary calculation. Suitable candidates could involve the prescribed raw kick, more precise symbol/transport information, or quantitatively stronger long-word/link information; the barrier does **not** prove that one particular exact-quantization route is logically necessary.

Ask each proposal to supply three concrete items:

1. The first new estimate, with its N scale, norm, occupation/Hamming/switch masks, trace normalization, and placement of the modulus written explicitly.
2. An argument that this estimate is actually available for the literal fixed-tau quantizer and does not follow merely from the preserved data above.
3. Its value or failure mechanism on the rounded-and-split control. If the argument also proves the desired zero endpoint for that control, it contains a missing assumption or an invalid implication.

A finite computation can test the proposed estimate or implementation at specified dimensions. It cannot replace the uniform-in-N separating estimate. Exact recurrence, multiplicity, or root-of-unity ratios alone are insufficient separators because the split control excludes all three.
