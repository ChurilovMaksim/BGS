# Literature checked during this pass

These sources provide context. None supplies a missing hypothesis in the internal cancellation proof.

1. Gregory Berkolaiko, *Form factor expansion for large graphs: a diagrammatic approach*, arXiv:nlin/0604025v1, 2006. Primary full PDF read: https://arxiv.org/pdf/nlin/0604025v1 . The introduction excludes certain short stretches; the conclusion explains why such stretches require cancellations and cannot be bounded by their absolute values. The paper is not used as a uniform all-orders Heisenberg-time estimate.

2. Bruno Bertini, Pavel Kos, Tomaž Prosen, *Random Matrix Spectral Form Factor of Dual-Unitary Quantum Circuits*, arXiv:2012.12254v3, 16 July 2021. Primary full PDF read: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.12254 . Equation (18) gives the CUE reference min(t,N). The thermodynamic conclusion in Theorem 1 / Corollary 1 concerns the specified dual-unitary circuits with the stated on-site disorder and fixed time as system length tends to infinity. A two-site gate with a growing local dimension and deterministic Fourier rotations does not satisfy those hypotheses automatically. The higher-moment equality in the discussion following equation (196) is not treated here as an established general theorem.

3. Gregory Berkolaiko, *Diagonal approximation of the form factor of the unitary group*, arXiv:nlin/0509037. Primary PDF read: https://arxiv.org/pdf/nlin/0509037 . Haar unitary averaging is distinct from the fixed Fourier matrix multiplied by independent diagonal phases.

Recent dual-unitary/static-Hamiltonian and boundary-chaos leads were inspected at abstract level where full primary access failed. No full theorem from those leads was verified or used. Search results and model similarities are not proof transfers.

The present calculations expand the fixed-power answer in N^(-1). This is distinct from a uniform expansion in scaled time t/N. No priority claim for the cancellations is justified by this limited literature comparison.
