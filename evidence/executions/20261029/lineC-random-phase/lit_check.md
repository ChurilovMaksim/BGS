# Literature re-check (2026-10-29, ~16:00 UTC+1). Full texts in lit/*.txt (arXiv PDFs via pdftotext).
- Tanner 2001, J. Phys. A 34 8485, arXiv nlin/0104014. Unitary-stochastic ensemble U = D1 Ũ D2, 2N-1 uniform phases; spectral functions need only N phases (D·Ũ).
  Sec. IV: "uniformly connected star-graphs" with Ũ = symmetric Fourier matrix ũ_nm = N^{-1/2} e^{2πi(n-1)(m-1)/N}. COE observed NUMERICALLY (Fig. 2), deviation σ(N) ≈ 4/3·N^{-4/3}. Off-diagonal terms attributed to "phase correlations imposed by unitarity" (no computation). lit/nlin_0104014.txt lines 225-300, 330-360, 525-560.
- Berkolaiko–Schanz–Whitney 2002, PRL 88 104101, arXiv nlin/0107056. B×B bond-scattering matrix S_{m'l,lm} = σ^{(l)}_{m'm} e^{iφ_ml}, independent BOND phases (φ_ml = φ_lm under TR). Computes only pairs differing by the orientation of one loop; −2τ² in the limit B→∞ at fixed τ under a mixing condition (their Eq. 4). Examples: complete graphs, B = N² bonds, DFT or Neumann vertex matrices. Text: "we have not discussed whether other correlated orbits could produce contributions of the same or lower order". lit/nlin_0107056.txt ~l.300-330.
- Berkolaiko–Schanz–Whitney 2003, J. Phys. A 36 8373, arXiv nlin/0205014. Same bond-phase ensemble. Diagrams with ≤ 2 self-intersections and ≤ 4 arcs; "explain why all other diagrams are expected to give higher-order corrections only". +2τ³ for uniformly hyperbolic TR graphs. Eq. (75): the fully connected Fourier graph agrees with GOE to third order in τ (B→∞). lit/nlin_0205014.txt l.100-130, 185-232.
- Berkolaiko 2004, Waves Random Media 14 S7, arXiv nlin/0305009: an algorithm for TR orbit pairs, third order for general graphs (abstract only checked).
- Gnutzmann–Altland 2004, PRL 93 194101, arXiv nlin/0402029: bond-phase average → supersymmetric σ-model; WD statistics for graphs with a spectral gap (field-theoretic, not a fixed-m statement; abstract checked).
- Gutkin–Osipov, Nonlinearity 26 (2013) 177, and J. Stat. Phys. (2013), arXiv 1305.0059: clusters of periodic orbits of the baker map / de Bruijn graphs, cluster sizes; a truncated-unitary form factor ⟨|Tr(QΛ(φ))^n|²⟩ with random diagonal phases (abstracts + ar5iv excerpt checked). They do not treat the DFT/cat-map ensemble or Gauss sums.
