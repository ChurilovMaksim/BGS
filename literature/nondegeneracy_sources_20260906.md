# Primary-source and priority boundary

Date: 6 September 2026.

1. Guy Cohen and Jean-Pierre Conze, *Central limit theorem for commutative
   semigroups of toral endomorphisms*, arXiv:1304.4556v2.
   Primary HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/1304.4556v2 . The sections on the
   Fourier-orbit spectral-density formula and degeneracy were inspected.
   This is prior work for the orbit-sum variance formalism, not evidence that
   the particular quadratic map studied in this packet is everywhere positive.

2. Tomaz Prosen and Marko Znidaric, *Quantum freeze of fidelity decay for
   chaotic dynamics*, Physical Review Letters 94 (2005), 044101.
   Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0401142 . The abstract was
   retrieved anew. Residual perturbations and their second-order mechanism
   are prior physics. The present classical variance statements are not
   conclusions quoted from that abstract.

Specific searches for Poisson brackets that are Fourier coboundaries of
hyperbolic toral automorphisms did not establish an exact predecessor or an
exhaustive absence of one. General Poisson-groupoid and Lie-bialgebra matches
are unrelated and were not used. The finite radius-eight theorem and invariant
Hessian criterion are project-level results with a supplied proof, not certified
world-priority claims. No external referee review was obtained.
