# Pell Floquet continuation: source and priority boundary

Checked 5 September 2026. This is a focused source audit, not a complete
priority search. The new note proves its finite inequalities and its global
limit directly. No external universality assertion is assumed.

Greg Kuperberg, *A tracial quantum central limit theorem*, Transactions of
the AMS 357 (2005), 459--471; arXiv:math-ph/0202035 (submitted 2002).
Primary abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0202035 . It concerns
noncommutative polynomials of averages of i.i.d. samples with a tracial
state. This is relevant precedent for classical limits in a tracial
noncommutative setting, not a plug-in theorem for the deterministic
Pell-period triangular array. No claim of inventing tracial Gaussian
limits is made here.

*Theory of Trotter Error with Commutator Scaling*, Physical Review X 11
(2021), 011020, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevX.11.011020. Primary institutional record:
https://quics.umd.edu/publications/theory-trotter-error-commutator-scaling .
The paper explicitly uses commutator information without relying on a
truncated Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff expansion. Our elementary two-factor
Duhamel bound is standard; it is proved in the note to fix constants and
avoid importing stronger hypotheses or claiming the general method as new.

Martin Horvat and Mirko Degli Esposti, *Egorov property in perturbed cat
map*, Journal of Physics A 40 (2007), DOI 10.1088/1751-8113/40/32/004;
arXiv:0706.4397v2. Primary abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.4397 .
It studies semiclassically small perturbations and quantum--classical
fidelity defined by a phase-space-density/Wigner overlap. That is not the
normalized Floquet trace or folded eigenphase distribution studied here.
The common small-perturbation setting is not evidence of independent
priority for either the new formulation or any underlying mechanism.

Only abstracts and primary bibliographic records were used for this
comparison. Full-text priority review of these papers and their citation
networks remains to be done. Absence of a matching theorem in these records
is not a claim that no such theorem exists.

## Claimed project-specific content

The centered cyclic inverse has a weighted Pell commutator phase sum of
order 1/K. Four-letter lifting makes distinct-time signed pair supports
orthogonal in the full Weyl basis. Their combination gives a normalized
Hilbert--Schmidt normal form with explicit error 12 epsilon^2/sqrt(K),
without epsilon times the generator norm tending to zero. A self-contained
bounded-cluster word count then yields a wrapped normal law for the actual
K-th power at epsilon=tau/sqrt(2K). These statements, including constants,
are the precise candidates for further independent review, not a general
claim to have proved BGS or discovered a new form of quantum mechanics.
