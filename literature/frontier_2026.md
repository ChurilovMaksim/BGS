# Frontier check — September 2026

The literature was rechecked during the reconstruction to avoid confusing quantum ergodicity with local spectral statistics.

Kurlberg--Ostafe--Rudnick--Shparlinski (2025) strengthen higher-dimensional quantum-ergodicity results for cat maps. Bhakta--Shparlinski (2026) treat prime-power sequences with quantitative discrepancy estimates. These are genuine advances on eigenfunction equidistribution, but they do not supply a GOE/GUE local eigenphase law.

Grove--Saad (2025) provide a useful modern template for joint families of elliptic curves: mixed trace moments are controlled only after nonconstant `j`, reduction conditions, and non-isogeny/non-isomorphism conditions are established. This supports the architecture of the current off/off route while also showing why those hypotheses must be proved rather than hidden under “standard sheaf theory”.

Sawin--Forey--Fresan--Kowalski provide quantitative sheaf-complexity technology that can make constants uniform once the actual sheaves, singularities and geometric isomorphism classes are under control.

The defensible frontier position for this repository is therefore:

1. exact finite-field reduction and mixed-energy structure are real results;
2. the off/off elliptic-family route is strong enough to justify a focused proof audit;
3. the restricted M1 theorem is still conditional;
4. the bridge from M1 to microscopic spectral statistics remains open;
5. no literature found in the 2026 check makes the old “general BGS solved” or “M1 proved by Möbius fibers” claims defensible.
