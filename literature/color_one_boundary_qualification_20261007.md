## Exact correction

For one independent U(1) phase, with one retarded/advanced bosonic and fermionic
flavor, the specified ordinary disk/plane body-coordinate integral of the
locally flat color-one bulk density does not equal the determinant-ratio phase
average. At a=b=c=d=0 the phase average is 1 and the absolutely convergent
bulk integral is 0; for c=d=0 it misses the whole 1/(1-ab) source dependence.
The correct audited prescription is the paired bulk-plus-surface cutoff limit,
with one common fermionic radial cutoff. For generic cd the two summands need
not exist separately. This correction concerns that explicitly specified
ordinary bulk-only coordinate formula. It does not invalidate every invariant
superintegral prescription and does not itself refute graph universality.

Zirnbauer, *Color-Flavor Transformation Revisited*, arXiv:2109.10272v1,
§2.4, (2.55)--(2.58), printed pp.12--13, gives the square-flavor stable range
N_color>=2n_0; one independent phase has N_color=1. Table 1 and
(4.36)--(4.40), printed pp.31--32, plus the surface operator (4.29), supply
the exactly checked coherent sections/completion. Physical site count is
not color count. Separate coordinate phases require separate color-one
transforms; a single common phase with physical-N flavors is different again.
The original 1998 chaotic-map source retains unspecified global-anomaly
terms and does not justify dropping them.

The compact finite-pair prescription in
[TRACE-023](../mathematics/phase_trace_checkpoint.md#bgs-trace-023-corrected-pair-source-and-uniform-source-bounds)
reorganizes the complete projector into four bounded-domain coefficient
functionals. It avoids separately divergent summands and is independently
reviewed on its stated coherent-section domain.
