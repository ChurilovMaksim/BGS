The Forrester HTML has apparent transcription/typographical issues in
some indices and parentheses. The project fixes time constants through
the angle generator and verifies an independent finite heat/Gram formula;
it does not use those transcriptions as axioms.

The covariance, commuting correction, coupling estimates and fixed-trace
martingale arguments are project derivations. Their methods use standard
Itô calculus, Girsanov, Pinsker, Gronwall and finite-dimensional martingale
convergence. Targeted searches did not establish priority for the precise
quadratic noise. Absence of a match is not evidence of novelty.
