# Exact-source and overlap ledger for the consolidated manuscript

Checked 6 September 2026. This is a bounded source audit, not a world-priority
certificate. The new return-word proof is elementary and does not invoke a
matrix Livsic theorem. Sources whose exact form was not recovered remain
explicit inherited inputs rather than being authenticated by their title.

## Rechecked primary inputs

**Vyugin--Makarychev, arXiv:1504.01354v2, 24 September 2015.**
Title: *Solutions of polynomial equation over F_p and new bounds of additive
energy*. Theorem 2, PDF page 2, was read in primary HTML and visually checked
in the primary PDF. Its hypotheses include an absolutely irreducible
bidegree-(m,n) polynomial, nonzero P(0,0), deg P(x,0)>=1, and
100(mn)^(3/2)<|G|<p^(3/4)/3. It bounds the number of points in two cosets by
16*m*n^2*(m+n)*|G|^(2/3). The Cayley polynomial is
(2-t)xy+t(x+y)-(2+t). At t not in {0,-2}, the needed constant and slice
conditions hold. Its bilinear coefficient determinant is -4, with the t=2
case an irreducible line. Thus the specialization m=n=1 gives 32*K^(2/3).
The two excluded fibres are bounded separately, giving the stated <34*K^(8/3)
energy estimate. This checks the exact used theorem, not every assertion or
proof in that external article. Primary locator:
https://arxiv.org/html/1504.01354v2

**Dritschel--Rovnyak, arXiv:0903.3639v1, Section 1.**
The scalar Fejer--Riesz statement and root-pair argument were read and visually
checked on PDF page 2. A nonnegative Laurent polynomial on the unit circle is
the squared modulus of a polynomial of the same nonnegative degree. The project
establishes strict positivity before applying it, and real coefficients follow
by the conjugation-invariant root choice. No multivariable/operator
factorization is imported. Primary locator: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0903.3639

**Cohen--Conze, arXiv:1304.4556v2.**
Primary HTML was read around the orbit variance and coboundary discussion,
including Theorem 2.31. Its regularity and action assumptions are not omitted:
the stated Fourier decay exponent exceeds dimension, and its coboundary
conclusion is classical. The project also gives the needed one-automorphism
orbit-sum argument directly. No classical CLT is used as a quantum CLT.
Primary locator: https://arxiv.org/html/1304.4556v2

**Prosen--Znidaric, arXiv:quant-ph/0401142.**
The primary five-page preprint and page 2 image were inspected. Equations
(4), (6), (7) and the effective second-order discussion contain direct
precedents for residual perturbations, endpoint telescoping and commutator
mechanisms. Their semiclassical fidelity approximations are not a theorem
proving this project's fixed-strength eigenphase limits. The overlap is
substantial at the mechanism level, not evidence of priority for the new
specific construction. Publication: PRL 94 (2005), 044101.

**Keating--Mezzadri, Nonlinearity 13 (2000), 747--775.**
DOI 10.1088/0951-7715/13/3/313. The Oxford record and primary HPL-BRIMS-1999-09
report were consulted. Its nonlinear perturbations and arithmetic

unrelated cellular-billiard paper is explicitly excluded. The search does not

## Other dependencies and scope limits

Mityagin's analytic zero-set theorem (arXiv:1512.07276, Proposition 0), the
standard two-factor Duhamel estimate, Weyl orthogonality, finite matrix-unit
innerness and spectral Riesz-rank continuity have their exact roles described
in the manuscript. Several of these elementary statements are also proved
there directly. Kuperberg's tracial i.i.d. CLT is background, not a theorem
applied to the deterministic Pell array. Kalinin's matrix Livsic theorem
(arXiv:0808.0350) is contextual; no unchecked nonabelian Livsic implication is
used in the new proof.

The older prime-conductor cotangent and cyclotomic-unit independence inputs,
Deligne/Igusa monodromy inputs for the elliptic correlation layer, and detailed
prime-counting uniformity in the density statements were not all reacquired
and rederived in this pass. Their original source statements and dependencies
are preserved, with no new blanket certification. Finite reruns of the related
resultants and discriminants do not authenticate these external theorems.
A successful computation is not substituted for an unverified external leaf.

## Search boundary and recent literature

Targeted searches used English terms for quantum cat maps, Poisson coboundaries,
noncommuting conjugacies, residual/fidelity-freeze mechanisms and BGS, including
a 2026 query. Some results were irrelevant surname matches or distance-matrix
BBS papers; they were not used. A recent PRX record for Kumar--Prosen--Roy,
*Leading and Beyond Leading-Order Spectral Form Factor in Chaotic Quantum
Many-Body Systems Across All Dyson Symmetry Classes* (2026), was found; only
its publisher record/abstract was reviewed, so no theorem is imported from it.
The limited search is insufficient to establish priority, or to classify the
entire modern spectral-universality literature.

Overlap classification of the new route: standard group-valued coboundary
algebra plus standard semiclassical locality; an explicit noncommuting smooth
example and its finite remainder are proved within the project. Whether the
same or a stronger construction is already known remains unverified.
