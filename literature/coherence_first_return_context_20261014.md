# Prior first-return mechanism and the project's scope

The primary source is J. Bourgain, F. A. Grünbaum, L. Velázquez and
J. Wilkening, *Quantum recurrence of a subspace and operator-valued
Schur functions*, arXiv:1302.7286v1 (2013).
[Author-hosted full text](https://math.berkeley.edu/~wilken/papers/Subspace_Recurrence_CMP.pdf)
was actually read at printed pages 4 and 6.

Equation (1), printed page 4, defines first-return and unmeasured-return
amplitude operators. The renewal equations on printed page 6 give both
ordered coefficient sums despite noncommutation of the individual
coefficients. Under the dictionary H=M_N(C), unitary step Ad_U and
absorbing subspace the diagonal matrices, these are the mechanisms
used in [TRACE-083](../mathematics/spectral-obstructions/coherent_first_return_pulse.md).

The mechanism is prior mathematics. The project explicitly proves its
own recurrence and uses its own tensor/chirp example; no external
recurrence theorem is a load-bearing hypothesis. The four-coefficient
formula, perturbation estimates and cyclic compositions are scoped
project results, without established mathematical priority. The
Hilbert--Schmidt amplitude protocol is not asserted to model a literal
density-matrix measurement or the specified physical cavity source.
