# Sources for the Schur continuation, 11 October 2026

## Scalar concentration input

Tropp, *Freedman's inequality for matrix martingales*, Electronic
Communications in Probability 16 (2011), 262–270,
[author-hosted PDF](https://tropp.caltech.edu/papers/Tro11-Freedmans-Inequality.pdf).
The load-bearing input for TRACE-060 is **Theorem 1.1, printed page 263**:
the scalar maximal Freedman inequality, applied to each sign of a stopped
real diagonal martingale. Its bounded-increment and predictable-variation
hypotheses are checked before each crossing. A matrix/block concentration
theorem is not used. The all-time flatness event is not inferred from this
inequality.

The 11 October source mapping was corrected accordingly. The preceding
metadata cache remains preserved; the new cache explicitly records this
review and the updated claim impact. Metadata matching is not an independent
mathematical applicability test.

## Stochastic complementation

The stochastic complement is a standard construction, associated in
particular with C. D. Meyer, *Stochastic complementation, uncoupling Markov
chains, and the theory of nearly reducible systems*, SIAM Review 31 (1989).
The attempted retrieval of the author-hosted PDF timed out in this review,
so no theorem in that paper is claimed to have been checked or imported.
TRACE-073 gives its own harmonic-lift and positive-inertia proof of the
ordered centered singular-value inequality. Neither this elementary
construction nor the new scoped bound has established world priority here.

## Quantum graph universality: transfer not established

Pluhar and Weidenmüller, *Universal Quantum Graphs*,
[arXiv:1407.1148](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.1148), was inspected at the primary
PDF, including Section 3.4.2's loop expansion. Its quantum-graph setup uses
incommensurate bond lengths, directed bonds, specified vertex scattering,
and restrictions excluding bound states. A classical singular gap in the
maintained torus map does not by itself verify this graph setup or control
the full graded-integral remainder needed for a microscopic local law.
No graph universality theorem is added as a discharged BGS dependency.

## Project proof boundaries

The even-p Schatten-potential estimate in TRACE-073 and the squared-word
estimate in the strengthened TRACE-060 are reconstructed in their maintained
proofs. Finite symbolic and rational executions support finite algebra only.
Internal independent review is distinct from external peer review, formal
verification and a literature novelty search. Flatness probability, the
scalar cavity law and deterministic BGS remain open.

The [separate reversal continuation](../record/reversal_channel_continuation_20261011.md) imports no new external theorem. Its initial FM-022 leaf is a maintained project statement; world priority of the general contraction observation has not been searched exhaustively or certified.
