## Scalar concentration input

Tropp, *Freedman's inequality for matrix martingales*, Electronic
Communications in Probability 16 (2011), 262–270,
[author-hosted PDF](https://tropp.caltech.edu/papers/Tro11-Freedmans-Inequality.pdf).
The load-bearing input for TRACE-060 is **Theorem 1.1, printed page 263**:
the scalar maximal Freedman inequality, applied to each sign of a stopped
real diagonal martingale. Its bounded-increment and predictable-variation
hypotheses are checked before each crossing. A matrix/block concentration
theorem is not used. The all-time flatness event is not inferred from this
inequality.

The 11 October source mapping was corrected accordingly. The preceding
metadata cache remains preserved; the new cache explicitly records this
review and the updated claim impact. Metadata matching is not an independent

## Quantum graph universality: transfer not established

Pluhar and Weidenmüller, *Universal Quantum Graphs*,
[arXiv:1407.1148](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.1148), was inspected at the primary
PDF, including Section 3.4.2's loop expansion. Its quantum-graph setup uses
incommensurate bond lengths, directed bonds, specified vertex scattering,
and restrictions excluding bound states. A classical singular gap in the
maintained torus map does not by itself verify this graph setup or control
the full graded-integral remainder needed for a microscopic local law.
No graph universality theorem is added as a discharged BGS dependency.

The separate primary-source check of H. A. Weidenmüller,
*Massive Modes for Quantum Graphs*, [arXiv:1812.00655](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.00655),
15-page v1 PDF, inspected Section 4.3 (printed pp. 10–11) and the conclusion
(printed pp. 14–15). The argument estimates matrix-element products by
averages and assumes the resulting small-fluctuation approximations.
Strict estimates are explicitly left to further work. This is not an
available rigorous discharge of our all-time flatness or full remainder.

## Proper-delay moments: prior random-matrix reference

Cunden, Mezzadri, Simm and Vivo, *Large-N expansion for the time-delay matrix
of ballistic chaotic cavities*, Journal of Mathematical Physics 57, 111901
(2016), [primary arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.00250), was inspected
on 11 October 2026. The retrieved 26-page document identifies itself as
arXiv:1607.00250v3, 27 October 2016; its separate title-page date reads
26 April 2018. We do not infer a version history from that discrepancy.
Equation (3), printed p. 3, normalizes the random-matrix proper-delay
moments as N^(k−1) E Tr Q^k. Equation (6), printed p. 4, gives the leading
Schröder sequence; equation (21), printed p. 7, gives its generating
function including the zeroth moment. Table I, printed p. 21, includes
1, 2, 6, 22, 90 for orders one through five.

Those formulas concern the ideal-coupling inverse-Wishart/Laguerre model,
whose ensemble hypothesis is stated before (2). The number 22 and the
candidate Schröder endpoint law are therefore prior reference objects.
The [conditional fourth-delay theorem](../mathematics/fourth_delay_law_20261011.md)
does not import that ensemble hypothesis as a proved property of the
deterministic starting unitary. Its random-Schur conclusion has its own
proof. The source is credited for comparison, not registered as a new
load-bearing theorem. Our finite-depth polynomial and any all-order
conditional-process priority remain unestablished.

## Full-depth reference and the prior free-Jacobi family

Hamdi, *Spectral distribution of the free Jacobi process, revisited*,
[primary arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.07382), retrieved 11 October
2026, identifies itself as arXiv:1711.07382v1, 20 November 2017, eleven pages.
Its introduction distinguishes the full trace from the normalized P corner.
Theorem 1.1 (printed p. 2) gives the full-trace atoms. Equation (3.1)
(printed p. 4) gives the symmetry/Jacobi variable change; (4.1) (printed
p. 6) gives the equilibrium symmetry density. With alpha=2p-1, beta=2q-1
and y=(1+cos(theta))/2, these yield the standard free-projection reference.
For q>=p, subtract the outside-P mass (1-p)delta_0 and divide by p;
the corner has no zero atom and has atom (p+q-1)_+/p at one.

The project parameters p=x/(1+x), q=2x/(1+x) and h=x/y map that
corner law exactly to the full-depth clock reference. Its measure is also
proved directly from the algebraic Cauchy transform, so this comparison
is not a load-bearing external theorem. The dictionary supplies no
freeness of actual Schur matrices. Priority of the conditional process
theorem is not established by identifying its prior reference family.
