Theorem 3.3 (p.187) supplies uniqueness and the lower Bowen-ball Gibbs bound
for a Hölder potential on a basic hyperbolic flow set. Lemmas 4.1--4.2
(p.189) give Hölder geometric potential and the volume bound. Proposition
4.4(b) (p.190) gives zero geometric pressure on an attractor; Remark 5.2
(p.192) identifies invariant absolutely continuous volume.

Application: the actual analytic area-preserving near-cat map has a smooth
constant-roof mapping torus. Its whole manifold is hyperbolic, transitive,
locally maximal, with dense periodic orbits and a nonvanishing flow. It is
the required basic attractor. Constant-roof invariant measures correspond
to map measures times roof length; entropy and integrated potentials agree.
Mixing is not a hypothesis of the cited results. The fixed-point multiplier
and the strict-pressure contradiction are proved in TRACE-113.
