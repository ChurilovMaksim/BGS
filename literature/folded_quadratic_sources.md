# Sources and priority boundary: folded-square resonance control

Checked 1 October 2026. No world-first claim. Positive projections,
Cauchy--Schwarz, Young, convex counts, Gram determinants and divided
differences are prior tools. TRACE-012 proves its elementary assertions
directly; no external theorem is substituted for an actual-map estimate.

## Primary literature and limited search

Michael S. Floater, *Bounds on the determinant of an exponential matrix*,
[arXiv:2603.19900v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19900v2),
3 September 2026, introduction and Sections 1--2,6. Its stated matrix
uses ordered real exponent coordinates; total positivity cannot simply
be applied to the imaginary exponentials here. HCIZ and divided
differences are related established methods. TRACE-012 does not import
that paper's inequalities or claim to improve them.

Searches with both available engines covered folded spectral density,
resonance, determinant repulsion and recent BGS/cat-map papers, including
primary arXiv records. They do not establish priority of the exact
projected-square criterion; absence of a hit is not evidence of novelty.
General spectral-chaos discussions are not fixed-map proofs.

The Sine and finite CUE targets are already defined in the maintained
[trace bridge](../mathematics/trace-bridge/trace_to_local_statistics_bridge.md).
Their uniform cubic mass bound is proved from probability-frequency
Gram representations, with a nonoptimal constant. SymPy/Wolfram check
series and finite algebra, not the analytic proof.
