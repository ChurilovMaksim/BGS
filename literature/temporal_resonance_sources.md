# Literature boundary for temporal resonance results

G. Cohen and J.-P. Conze, Central limit theorem for commutative semigroups of
toral endomorphisms, arXiv:1304.4556. Fourier-orbit variance and dynamical
coboundary criteria are prior theory. The finite criterion used here is also
proved directly; the general CLT machinery is not claimed as new.

A. Boettcher and H. Widom, From Toeplitz Eigenvalues through Green's Kernels
to Higher-Order Wirtinger-Sobolev Inequalities, arXiv:math/0412269. The relation
between a symbol's zero order and finite-section eigenvalue decay is prior
Toeplitz theory. The continuation supplies a direct elementary rate proof and
an exact representation/comparison for the particular dynamical covariance.

S. Makarychev and I. Vyugin, Solutions of Polynomial Equations in Subgroups
of F_p*, Arnold Mathematical Journal 5 (2019), 105-121,
DOI 10.1007/s40598-019-00112-z. The averaging route considered here involves
rescalings/cosets of a fixed polynomial. The Cayley equivalence theorem blocks
one direct application, not all possible averaging methods.

S. Macourt, I. D. Shkredov and I. E. Shparlinski, Multiplicative Energy of
Shifted Subgroups and Bounds On Exponential Sums with Trinomials in Finite
Fields, arXiv:1701.06192. Multiplicative shifted-subgroup energy is not
silently converted into additive Cayley-image energy.

Identifiers were checked against primary records. Broad searches of quantum-
cat-map and Weyl-commutator literature did not establish priority for the
specialized module, common-divisor, crossover or phase-rank results. Absence
of a matching search hit is not a proof of novelty. No unverified search
abstract or purported spectral-universality theorem is used as proof input.
