# Prior structures in the Green-coordinate argument

Primary publisher pages checked on 13 October 2026:

- Carl D. Meyer, *The Role of the Group Generalized Inverse in the Theory
  of Finite Markov Chains*, SIAM Review (1975),
  [DOI 10.1137/1017044](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/1017044).
  The publisher identifies the group-generalized-inverse approach to
  finite Markov chains. This establishes prior subject matter, not the
  exact centered compression formula used here.
- Carl D. Meyer, *Stochastic Complementation, Uncoupling Markov Chains,
  and the Theory of Nearly Reducible Systems*, SIAM Review 31(2),
  240–272 (June 1989),
  [DOI 10.1137/1031050](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/1031050).
  The publisher abstract explicitly develops stochastic complementation
  and its applications to uncoupling finite Markov chains.

Only publisher metadata and abstracts were checked in this comparison;
the full papers and an exact theorem locator were not verified. Neither
paper is used as a load-bearing external theorem. TRACE-077 proves its
block inverse identity directly. Group inverses, stochastic complements,
Schur elimination and smooth Schatten potentials are prior structures.
No novelty claim for these objects, or world-priority claim for their
combination, follows from this limited search.

The mathematical contribution evaluated internally is the adapted,
fixed-ambient-space martingale in centered Green coordinates, together
with its depth-decaying initial compression and full first-exit bound.
Independent internal reconstruction is not external referee certification.
