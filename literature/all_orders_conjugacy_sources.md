# Primary-source audit: fixed strength, rigidity and sharpness

Date: 5 September 2026. This is a targeted comparison, not a world-priority
certificate. The new project claims concern the specific constructions and
proofs, not invention of locality, spectral factorization, or ergodicity.

## Cat-map spectral anomalies

J. P. Keating and F. Mezzadri, *Pseudo-symmetries of Anosov maps and spectral
statistics*, Nonlinearity 13 (2000), 747--775,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/0951-7715/13/3/313).
The title and publication were checked against the Oxford Quantum Institute
record. The primary HP technical report HPL-BRIMS-1999-09 was accessed through
[a preserved mirror](https://shiftleft.com/mirrors/www.hpl.hp.com/techreports/1999/HPL-BRIMS-1999-09.pdf).
The introduction, perturbation definitions and symmetry discussion were
inspected. They treat nonlinear shears and quantum-lattice pseudo-symmetries.
A screenshot request for its page 5 failed in the web renderer; the parsed text
of those passages was readable. No claim of an exhaustive full-paper or
literature priority review is made.

A previous title-only alphaXiv request had returned a different paper,
Gutkin's *Spectral statistics of cellular billiards*. That response is not a
source for the contents of the Keating--Mezzadri paper. The mismatch is
explicitly excluded from the present inference chain.

## Residual perturbations and commutator bounds

T. Prosen and M. Znidaric, *Quantum freeze of fidelity decay for chaotic
dynamics*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 94 (2005), 044101,
[primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0401142).
Coboundary cancellation and second-order commutators are prior mechanisms.
The fixed-strength flat branch uses a direct all-orders Fourier remainder,
not an assumption that a semiclassical fidelity approximation proves a spectral
correlation theorem.

A. M. Childs, Y. Su, M. C. Tran, N. Wiebe and S. Zhu,
*A Theory of Trotter Error*, [arXiv:1912.08854](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.08854),
published as *Theory of Trotter Error with Commutator Scaling*, Phys. Rev. X 11
(2021), 011020. Commutator-sensitive product estimates are established tools.
The elementary two-factor estimate used here is proved within the project note.

## Analytic zero sets

B. S. Mityagin, *The Zero Set of a Real Analytic Function*,
[arXiv:1512.07276](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.07276), Proposition 0;
Math. Notes 107 (2020), 529--530.
The primary PDF and its first-page statement were inspected. This supplies the
measure-zero property of a nontrivial analytic derivative's zero set. The
projective-line reduction and Fourier argument in the new rigidity note are
written out separately. No claim that analytic rigidity implies positivity of
the Green--Kubo variance of a nonzero bracket is made.

## Attainment of the sharp extremum

M. A. Dritschel and J. Rovnyak, *The Operator Fejer--Riesz Theorem*,
[arXiv:0903.3639](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.3639), Section 1;
book chapter, *A Glimpse at Hilbert Space Operators* (2010), 223--254.
The scalar factorization theorem and root-pair explanation on PDF page 2 were
read and visually inspected. Only the scalar, one-variable result is used.
The project's finite minimization requires realizability of autocorrelations;
it is not solved merely by an unconstrained weighted Cauchy--Schwarz bound.

Targeted searches did not determine whether the exact sharp functional or the
stated Poisson-rigidity corollary already appears in the literature. Absence of
a search hit is not evidence sufficient to claim a first-ever result.
