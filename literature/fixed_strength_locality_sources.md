# Fixed-strength locality: primary-source boundary

Checked 2026-09-05. This is a targeted source audit, not a priority certificate.

M. Hitrik, R. Lascar, J. Sjoestrand, M. Zerzeri, *Semiclassical Gevrey operators
in the complex domain*, Ann. Inst. Fourier 73 (2023), 1269-1318,
DOI 10.5802/aif.3546, arXiv:2009.09125. The primary preprint discusses the
nonoptimal factorial power (r!)^(2s-1) in the ordinary Weyl expansion and
stronger bounds available through other methods; see equation (3.151).
Our conservative exponent 1/(2s-1) is not presented as a new optimal
Gevrey theorem. Parsed primary text was available; screenshot calls failed.

T. Prosen and M. Znidaric, *Quantum Freeze of Fidelity Decay for Chaotic
Dynamics*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 94 (2005), 044101, arXiv:quant-ph/0401142.
Residual perturbations, discrete coboundaries and second-order effective
commutators are direct precedent. This paper must not be presented as if
our project discovered the residual-perturbation mechanism.

J. P. Keating and F. Mezzadri, *Pseudo-symmetries of Anosov maps and spectral
statistics*, Nonlinearity 13 (2000), 747-775,
DOI 10.1088/0951-7715/13/3/313. Author-institution records at Oxford and
Bristol confirm this citation. Full text was not obtained, so this audit
cannot establish whether the precise disjoint-support construction has
appeared there. A title-based alphaXiv retrieval returned the unrelated
arXiv:1010.0276 cellular-billiard paper; it was not used as this source.

The proposed contribution is the explicit natural single-exponential
fixed-strength family, its all-orders comparison, and the detailed
microscopic consequences under polynomially bounded unfolding. Every
ingredient has established antecedents, and the maps remain smoothly
conjugate to an arithmetic model. No world-priority or generic-BGS claim
is justified by this audit.
