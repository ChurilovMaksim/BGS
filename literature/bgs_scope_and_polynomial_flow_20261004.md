# Bounded primary-source check: BGS scope and polynomial coboundary limits

Checked 4 October 2026. This is a scoped check, not an exhaustive novelty
search or proof that no unlocated result exists. No external priority is
asserted for FLOW-011.

| Primary source and version | Material inspected | Use and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| O. Bohigas, M. J. Giannoni, C. Schmit, *Characterization of Chaotic Quantum Spectra and Universality of Level Fluctuation Laws*, PRL 52, 1 (1984), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.52.1) | Publisher abstract and bibliography | Numerical spectral evidence for Sinai billiards and a universality conjecture; the inspected material is not a rigorous all-system proof. |
| P. Kurlberg, Z. Rudnick, *Hecke theory and equidistribution for the quantization of linear maps of the torus*, [arXiv:chao-dyn/9901031v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/chao-dyn/9901031v2), 30 March 2000 | Abstract and bibliography | Large degeneracies and quantum symmetries in hyperbolic torus quantizations. Classical chaos alone does not identify an unrestricted symmetry-free universality contract. |
| F. Faure, S. Nonnenmacher, S. De Bièvre, *Scarred eigenstates for quantum cat maps of minimal periods*, [arXiv:nlin/0207060v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/nlin/0207060v2), 6 June 2003 | Abstract and bibliography | Short-period quantizations admit scarred states. This eigenfunction warning is not a proof of failure of every spectral BGS formulation. |
| Z. Pluhař, H. A. Weidenmüller, *Quantum Graphs and Random-Matrix Theory*, [arXiv:1407.1148](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.1148) | Abstract and bibliography | The graph result states model hypotheses: mixing, incommensurate lengths and absence of bound states. That scope cannot be imported into the nonlinear torus model. |
| P. J. Forrester, *Some exact correlations in the Dyson Brownian motion model for transitions to the CUE*, [arXiv:chao-dyn/9603010v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/chao-dyn/9603010), 25 March 1996 | PDF: circular heat construction and equispaced-start subsection 5.2 | Prior clock correlation kernel, credited in FLOW-007 and inherited by FLOW-011. Project normalization and alias estimates are separately checked. |
| H. A. Weidenmüller, *Eigenstate thermalization hypothesis versus Bohigas–Giannoni–Schmit conjecture: a comparison*, [arXiv:2509.17634](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.17634) | Abstract and bibliography | Recent comparison of statistical hypotheses. It supplies no model-specific deterministic Heisenberg estimate for this continuation. |
