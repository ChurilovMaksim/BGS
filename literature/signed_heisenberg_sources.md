# Sources and priority boundary for signed Heisenberg comparison

Checked 30 September 2026. Sources below distinguish a prior reference law
from the new project obstruction. There is no claim of an exhaustive
priority search or a world-first result.

## Load-bearing CUE moment formula

Persi Diaconis and Steven N. Evans, *Linear functionals of eigenvalues of
random matrices*, Transactions of the AMS 353 (2001), 2615--2633,
DOI [10.1090/S0002-9947-01-02800-8](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-01-02800-8).
The checked primary source is the author-hosted
[Berkeley 19-page PDF](https://statistics.berkeley.edu/sites/default/files/tech-reports/577.pdf).
Locator: Theorem 2.1(a), printed p. 3; its proof concludes on p. 4.

The theorem matches joint trace moments with independent complex Gaussians
only when both weighted degrees are at most the matrix dimension. The
calculation in [TRACE-010](../mathematics/signed_heisenberg_comparison.md)
has maximum degree $4\max I\le N$, including the cubic/pair cross terms.
No distributional independence at arbitrary Heisenberg powers is inferred.
The source explicitly corrects the earlier Diaconis--Shahshahani range.
The publisher PDF returned HTTP 403 in this session; the author-hosted
complete theorem and proof were read instead. Registered as EXT-DE-2001.

## Related prior warning, not a load-bearing theorem

R. E. Prange, *The spectral form factor is not self-averaging*,
[arXiv:chao-dyn/9606010v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/chao-dyn/9606010v1), 1996,
four-page primary preprint. The discussion around equations (5)--(7)
describes fluctuations of the unsmoothed form factor under its statistical
model and the role of time smoothing. This credits the prior conceptual
warning; it does not prove the deterministic pair-to-cubic lower bound
in TRACE-010. No novelty inference follows from the limited search.

## Prior project calibration

The exact finite CUE factorial cycle formula is maintained in
[heisenberg_trace_support.md](../mathematics/heisenberg_trace_support.md).
The 19 September [CUE counterterm](../supplement/fixed_map/report-audit-20260919/cue_reference.md)
and [high-band cubic variance](../supplement/fixed_map/report-audit-20260919/cubic_variance.md)
already establish nonzero raw cubic targets above the CUE ramp and an
$O(N^{-1})$ variance for specified factorized weights in that band.
TRACE-010's low-band complex-weight formula is a separate calibration,
not the discovery of cubic CUE concentration or of the need for signed sums.
