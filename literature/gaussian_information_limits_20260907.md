# Gaussian information-limit literature scope — checked 2026-09-07

Primary/official source metadata used for attribution and scope:

- Ivan, Sabapathy and Simon, *Operator-sum Representation for Bosonic Gaussian Channels*, Phys. Rev. A 84, 042311 (2011), DOI `10.1103/PhysRevA.84.042311`, arXiv:1012.4266. Standard Gaussian-channel/Kraus machinery is prior work.
- Butucea, Guta and Artiles, *Minimax and adaptive estimation of the Wigner function in quantum homodyne tomography with noisy data*, Ann. Statist. 35 (2007), 465-494, DOI `10.1214/009053606000001488`, arXiv:math/0504058. Noisy homodyne deconvolution and Wigner minimax estimation are prior work under different regularity classes and loss.
- Lami, *Quantum data hiding with continuous variable systems*, Phys. Rev. A 104, 052428 (2021), DOI `10.1103/PhysRevA.104.052428`, arXiv:2102.01100; Sabapathy and Winter, arXiv:2102.01622. Restricted-measurement bosonic data hiding is prior work.
- Mele et al., *Learning quantum states of continuous-variable systems*, Nature Physics 21 (2025), 2002-2008, DOI `10.1038/s41567-025-03086-2`, arXiv:2405.01431. General continuous-variable state learning under energy/moment conditions is prior work.

The BGS claims are narrower/different: a fixed-moment rare-phase family with a sharp heterodyne radial/aperture law, and a matching input trace-distance minimax order after a fixed strictly noisy phase-insensitive Gaussian channel even against collective output POVMs. This bounded source check establishes attribution and scope, not absence of every earlier theorem or global priority.
