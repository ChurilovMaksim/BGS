# M1 — current analytic bottleneck

## Target

Prove an asymptotic fluctuation estimate strong enough to connect the exact/twisted-Jacobi reduction to the desired spectral law, in an explicitly stated scaling regime. The historical statement `|delta| <= 16/K^2 = o(1)` is not active.

## Exact object to start from

Write the fluctuation contribution as an exact sum over ordered twist-pair pieces. If `F_tau` denotes the convolution contribution of a twist pair, then before any inequality expand

`E = ||sum_tau F_tau||^2`

into diagonal and cross terms. No cross term may be dropped unless an exact orthogonality or a quantitative covariance/cancellation theorem is proved.

The 2026-09-03 audit also rules out using the R86/R87 trace-fluctuation draft as a shortcut: its first moment expansion applies the ordinary binomial theorem to `C_q` and `C_q^*` without proving commutation, and its subsequent adjacent-pair factorisation is unproved.

## First repaired-M1 cycle: new exact obstruction

The historical Möbius-fiber assembly has now been audited independently in Python and C++20; see `research/M1_cross_terms_2026-09-03.md`, `code/experiments/m1_cross_terms.py`, `code/cpp/m1_cross_terms.cpp`, and `results/experimental/m1_cross_terms.json`.

Two concrete facts are established for that implemented historical assembly:

1. the full energy has nonzero cross-twist terms — the diagonal-only energy identity is false;
2. the stated uniform `|A_tau(d)| <= 4 sqrt(N)` bound for every nontrivial twist pair is false without exceptional-fiber exclusions.

The second failure is algebraically explicit. At `d=0`, the historical Möbius map

`v_d(u) = (u(d-1)+1)/(d-u+1)`

reduces to `v_0(u)=1` for every admissible `u != 1`. Therefore, for `psi1=1` and any nontrivial `psi2`,

`A_(1,psi2)(0) = N-2`,

which is much larger than `4 sqrt(N)` for the tested designs. The exact finite observations are also encoded in `formal/Bgs/M1DegenerateFiber.lean`; that Lean source is currently `UNVERIFIED BUILD` because Lean/Lake was unavailable in the execution runtime.

This means the repaired proof must split exceptional fibers/twist pairs from the genuinely generic character-sum layer before applying any Weil-type estimate.

## Required proof checkpoints

1. Fix the additive/multiplicative Fourier normalisation once.
2. Derive the exact ordered twist-pair formula from the current character convention and prove that it equals the full current `k=4` coupled-energy object.
3. Classify all degenerate Möbius fibers: degree drops, constant maps, common zero/pole cancellations, and perfect-power divisor cases.
4. Classify diagonal, trivial/nontrivial cross, and nontrivial/nontrivial cross correlations without discarding order-dependent terms.
5. Prove the divisor/character statement for the generic fibers and state every exceptional congruence separately.
6. Apply a finite-field character-sum estimate only after its hypotheses are verified.
7. Sum exceptional and generic contributions with the true number of twist pairs and the true normalization.
8. State the asymptotic regime `(N,K,m)` and verify that the final remainder genuinely tends to zero.
9. Use finite-N computations only as falsifiers/calibration unless an exact identity is separately proved.

## Active subproblems

### M1-A — exact degeneracy classification

Classify the divisor of `u^r v_d(u)^s` for all relevant character exponents `(r,s)` and all `d`. The `d=0` constant fiber is the first explicit member of the exceptional set, not the whole classification.

### M1-B — cross-Gram formula

For `F_tau=A_tau*A_tau`, derive an exact Fourier/character-sum representation of

`G_(tau,sigma) = <F_tau,F_sigma>`.

The goal is a structure theorem or summed cancellation estimate for the Gram matrix, not an entrywise triangle-inequality bound.

### M1-C — scalable falsifiers

Use Python for transparent small-design verification and C++ for larger finite-field sweeps. Do not run the naive `O(m^2 N^2)` construction at `N=3571`; exploit FFT, character symmetries, orbit structure, or sampling only after the exact formula is fixed.

### M1-D — formal layer

Write Lean only for exact finite identities and symbolic lemmas whose statements are already mathematically settled. Until Lean/Lake is available, every new Lean source must be marked `UNVERIFIED BUILD`; no theorem status may depend on an unbuilt file.

## Falsifier set

At minimum use `N=53`, `199`, and `3571`; add split-prime designs varying `K` and sector dimension enough to distinguish true `N`-decay from a merely small `K`-dependent constant. The independent `N=461` / `19489` matrix-element regression is a useful implementation check, not an asymptotic estimate.

## Alternative routes

The higher-moment, shape-automaton, word, and rank-2/Sp4 corpora may suggest a route that bypasses M1. Such a route is welcome only if it proves its own load-bearing estimate and does not import either the failed Möbius-fiber promotion or the invalid noncommutative-binomial R86 reduction.

## Promotion rule

M1 moves from `OPEN GAP` only when the exact cross-twist assembly, the complete exceptional-fiber classification, and the asymptotic limit are proved. A good finite-N envelope, a passing statistical experiment, or a formal file containing an admitted/opaque/unbuilt bound is not sufficient.
