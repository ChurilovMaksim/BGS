# M1 — current analytic bottleneck

## Target

Prove an asymptotic fluctuation estimate strong enough to connect the exact/twisted-Jacobi reduction to the desired spectral law, in an explicitly stated scaling regime.

The previous statement `|delta| <= 16/K^2 = o(1)` is not active.

## Exact object to start from

Write the fluctuation contribution as an exact sum over twist-pair pieces `A_tau`. Before any inequality, expand

`E = ||sum_tau A_tau||^2`

into diagonal and cross terms. No cross term may be dropped unless an exact orthogonality or a quantitative covariance/cancellation theorem is proved.

## Required proof checkpoints

1. Fix the additive/multiplicative Fourier normalisation once.
2. Derive the exact twist-pair formula from the current character convention.
3. Classify diagonal versus cross-twist correlations.
4. Prove the divisor/character statement excluding perfect-power degeneracies for the nontrivial character sums where it is needed.
5. Apply the appropriate finite-field character-sum estimate only after its hypotheses are verified.
6. Sum the bounds with the true number of twist pairs and the true normalisation.
7. State the asymptotic regime `(N,K,m)` and verify that the final remainder tends to zero in that regime.

## Falsifier set

At minimum use the existing designs `N=53`, `199`, `3571`; add new split-prime designs that vary `K` and `m` separately enough to distinguish an `N`-decay from a merely small `K`-dependent constant.

The old M1 finite-N test is retained under `results/historical-numerical/` because its character-sum observations may still be useful. Its old conclusion that M1 was essentially proved is explicitly superseded.

## Promotion rule

M1 moves from `OPEN GAP` only when the exact cross-twist assembly and asymptotic limit are both proved. A good numerical envelope is not sufficient.
