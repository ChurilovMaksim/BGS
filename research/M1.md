## Exact object now fixed

For ordered `x,y in F_N \ {0,1}` define

`u=x/y`, `v=(x-1)/(y-1)`, `d=x-y`.

The key table is **weighted**: for `x!=y` the key is regular and has multiplicity one, while all `N-2` diagonal pairs `x=y` collapse to `(u,v,d)=(1,1,0)`.

Let `H=<a>` have order `K`, `m=(N-1)/K`, and let `W[c_u,c_v,d]` count keys by the two quotient cosets in `(F_N^*)/H`. The subgroup constraints are exactly `c_2=-c_1`, hence

`B(t)=sum_c (W_c * W_{-c})(t)`.

The quotient-character DFT gives twists `A_tau(d)` and the exact projector identity

`B(t)=m^{-2} sum_tau (A_tau*A_tau)(t)`.

Writing `F_tau=A_tau*A_tau`, the energy is

`E=m^{-4} ||sum_tau F_tau||_2^2`,

so every diagonal and cross-twist term is part of the exact object. No cross term may be dropped without an exact orthogonality theorem or a quantitative assembled-covariance bound.

## Cycle 002 — regular single-twist Weil bound closed

For `d!=0`, choose a primitive annihilator character `omega` of order `m` and write the regular twist as

`A_{r,s}(d) = sum_{y admissible} omega(((y+d)/y)^r ((y+d-1)/(y-1))^s)`.

Define

`Q_d(Y)=Y(Y-1)(Y+d)(Y+d-1)`

and, with `r^-=(-r) mod m`, `s^-=(-s) mod m`,

`P_{r,s,d}(Y)=Q_d(Y)^m (Y+d)^r Y^{r^-}(Y+d-1)^s(Y-1)^{s^-}`.

The `Q_d^m` factor is a domain mask: on admissible points its character is one, while every excluded point contributes zero. Hence the restricted rational sum equals the complete polynomial sum

`A_{r,s}(d)=sum_{y in F_N} omega(P_{r,s,d}(y))`.

For generic `d notin {0,±1}`, the four root-multiplicity residues modulo `m` are `r,-r,s,-s`; for `d=1` they are `r,s-r,-s`; for `d=-1` they are `-r,r-s,s`. In every case, all residues vanish only for the trivial pair `(r,s)=(0,0)`. Thus every nontrivial regular twist produces a polynomial that is not an `m`-th power.

Applying Lidl--Niederreiter, *Finite Fields*, Theorem 5.41 gives

`|A_{r,s}(d)| <= 3 sqrt(N)` for every `d!=0` and nontrivial `(r,s)`,

with the sharper `2 sqrt(N)` bound at `d=±1`.

This closes the historical perfect-power / individual-fiber issue. It does **not** bound the sum of cross-twist covariances. Python and independent C++17 residue classifiers exhaustively agree at `m=4,18,20,210`; those computations are regression checks for the proof, not its logical basis.

## Exact raw-energy obstruction

Since `B(t)` is a nonnegative count and the singular pair contributes `(N-2)^2` to `B(0)`,

`E = sum_t B(t)^2 >= (N-2)^4`.

Therefore a raw bound `E=O(N^3 K^4)` cannot hold uniformly unless at least `K^4=Omega(N)`, or unless the deterministic singular contribution is removed by an exact centering identity upstream. M1 has an explicit fork:

1. prove that the spectral fluctuation observable is the regular/centered object after exact removal of the singular term; or
2. retain raw energy and derive/state an arithmetic growth regime strong enough to absorb the lower bound.
