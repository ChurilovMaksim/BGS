## Exact object now fixed

For ordered `x,y in F_N \ {0,1}` define

`u=x/y`, `v=(x-1)/(y-1)`, `d=x-y`.

The key table is **weighted**: for `x!=y` the key is regular and has multiplicity one, while all `N-2` diagonal pairs `x=y` collapse to `(u,v,d)=(1,1,0)`.

Let `H=<a>` have order `K`, `m=(N-1)/K`, and let `W[c_u,c_v,d]` count keys by the two quotient cosets in `(F_N^*)/H`. The subgroup constraints are exactly `c_2=-c_1`, hence

`B(t)=sum_c (W_c * W_{-c})(t)`.

The quotient-character DFT gives twists `A_tau(d)` and the exact projector identity

`B(t)=m^{-2} sum_tau (A_tau*A_tau)(t)`.

Writing `F_tau=A_tau*A_tau`, the energy is

`E=m^{-4} ||sum_tau F_tau||_2^2`,

so every diagonal and cross-twist term is part of the exact object. No cross term may be dropped without an exact orthogonality theorem or a quantitative assembled-covariance bound.

## Next analytic character-sum object

For `d!=0`, the regular twist is

`A_{r,s}(d) = sum_y chi_r((y+d)/y) chi_s((y+d-1)/(y-1))`.

Equivalently it is a multiplicative character sum of

`f_{r,s,d}(y)=(y+d)^r y^{-r}(y+d-1)^s(y-1)^{-s}`.

The divisor has four generic support points, with collisions at `d=±1`. The historical phrase “not a K-th power” is not the correct hypothesis: the relevant exponent is the **order of the character**, which divides the annihilator-group order `m`. The next proof must classify divisor valuations modulo that character order, including one-trivial-coordinate and collision cases, before invoking any Weil-type estimate.

The current 14-case suite observes `max_{tau nontrivial,d!=0}|A_tau(d)| < 3 sqrt(N)` in every sampled case. This is falsifier evidence only.

## Exact raw-energy obstruction

Since `B(t)` is a nonnegative count and the singular pair contributes `(N-2)^2` to `B(0)`,

`E = sum_t B(t)^2 >= (N-2)^4`.

Therefore a raw bound `E=O(N^3 K^4)` cannot hold uniformly unless at least `K^4=Omega(N)`, or unless the deterministic singular contribution is removed by an exact centering identity upstream. M1 now has an explicit fork:

1. prove that the spectral fluctuation observable is the regular/centered object after exact removal of the singular term; or
2. retain raw energy and derive/state an arithmetic growth regime strong enough to absorb the lower bound.

## Correct trace-3 arithmetic regime

For the fixed trace-3 cat map, a split eigenvalue satisfies `a+a^{-1}=3 mod N`. Let

`L_0=2`, `L_1=3`, `L_{n+1}=3L_n-L_{n-1}`.

If `ord_N(a)=K`, then `N | (L_K-2)`. Hence fixed `K` permits only finitely many primes `N`; along any infinite split-prime sequence for the fixed map, `K(N)->infinity`. With `lambda=(3+sqrt(5))/2`, the elementary inequality `L_K-2<lambda^K` gives `K>log(N)/log(lambda)`.

This repairs the historical asymptotic regime statement but does not by itself bound the cross-twist energy.

`N=53,a=10,K=13` is only a historical subgroup calibration: `a=10` is not a trace-3 eigenvalue modulo 53. It must not be used as a trace-3 split-prime example.
