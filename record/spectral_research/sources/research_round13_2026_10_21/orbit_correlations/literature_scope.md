# Round 13: source applicability for the orbit-correlation route

21 October 2026. No external result from this search is used as an
unproved premise in the new mathematical note.

## Primary sources actually opened

1. Frédéric Faure, *Can Quantum Dynamics Emerge from Classical Chaos?*,
   ICM 2026, volume 5, pp. 307–327,
   [DOI 10.1137/25M1805783](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/25M1805783),
   author manuscript [arXiv:2510.25307v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25307v1).
   The publisher HTML and selected raw manuscript pages were opened.
   The directly checked Theorems 2.9–2.10 concern resonance bands and
   approximation by spectral projectors for Anosov geodesic/contact
   flows. The resolvent estimate (2.13) is in gaps between bands; it
   supplies neither microscopic spacing within the outer band nor an
   identification with the exact prescribed matrix family. Those
   theorems therefore do not prove the needed original H_diag estimate.
   This is a scope check of the stated theorems, not a claim that the
   complete paper has been audited.

2. N. Argaman, F.-M. Dittes, E. Doron, J. P. Keating, A. Yu. Kitaev,
   M. Sieber and U. Smilansky, *Correlations in the actions of periodic
   orbits derived from quantum chaos*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 71 (1993),
   4326–4329,
   [DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.71.4326](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.71.4326).
   The publisher abstract was read. Its stated implication assumes
   random-matrix spectral statistics to infer action correlations.
   This direction cannot supply those statistics for the original U_N.
   The full article was not read in this pass.

## Search scope and finite conclusion

Heisenberg-time form factors, nonlinear quantum Anosov maps, periodic
action correlations, and high-frequency transfer estimates. The
results also pointed to old semiclassical encounter expansions, quantum
graphs, linear/prequantum cat maps, and random or interacting models.
No such result was imported without a proved comparison.
