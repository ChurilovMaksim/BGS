# Retractions and permanent quarantines

These routes are preserved for research history but must not be used as current
theorem inputs.

1. **Historical Möbius-fiber / M4D proof of M1.** Unsaturated elimination
   inserted a phantom `(1,1)` key and mishandled the diagonal multiplicity. The
   resulting perfect-power/cross-term object is not the current pair-key object.
2. **“Moment/Catalan data + an ESY citation implies BGS.”** False. The
   arithmetic matrix family is not automatically a Wigner ensemble, and the
   microscopic comparison/resolvent mechanism remains open.
3. **Run 93.2 third calibration.** The old producer omitted one ordered
   diagonal/off-diagonal coupling class.
4. **R86/R87 noncommutative trace shortcut.** Invalid as stated.
5. **Historical `N=95419` spacing verdict.** It compared different
   statistics (gap ratio versus ordinary spacing CDF).
6. **Old word-trace “certificate”.** A higher-word rewrite case remained
   unresolved.
7. **Hecke-centering Julia series.** Failed line; later red-team work exposed
   basis-selection and centering/noise artifacts.
8. **`cdense_transfer.jl` Perron bracket.** Falsified: the zero digit creates
   a zero-cost self-loop of weight one.
9. **`sol6_closed_form.jl` closed-form claim.** Falsified by preserved exact
   finite rows; the count depends on the prime in cases where the filename
   suggested otherwise.
10. **General `Sp(2g)` conclusion from diagonal/coset flatness.** Not
    established.
11. **Finite GOE fits as proof of a limiting BGS law.** Numerical evidence only.

The files themselves remain recoverable through `record/corpus/` and Git
history. Retraction means “do not infer from it”, not “delete the scientific
trail”.
