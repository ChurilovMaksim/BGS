## New mathematical conclusion of this pass

For any finite Hermitian B, real E, iid standard Cauchy diagonal T and nonzero A>=0, the scalar Tr(A(B-T-E)^-1) is exactly Cauchy with parameter Tr(A(B-E+i)^-1). The proof uses a bounded holomorphic transform before contour averaging, so it never assumes the undefined raw Cauchy mean.

For a Hermitian involution S this parameter is Tr(AS)/2-i Tr(A)/2. Nevertheless all positive scalar projections do not determine a matrix law: the explicit 2x2 inverse ensemble and affine diagonal-Cauchy ensemble in [TRACE-105](../mathematics/cauchy_positive_source_boundary_20261019.md) agree on every such projection and disagree on a signed one. The signed tail constants in the explicit two-dimensional example are 4/pi^2 and 2/pi. A stronger supplement proves that, for every non-diagonal Hermitian involution including the exact Fourier seed, H inverse and H/2 have identical all positive scalar projections but full matrix laws at total variation distance one. One rank-two signed source gives the separation. This rules out the particular closure from all positive projections, not BGS.
