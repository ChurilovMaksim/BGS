## Mathematical result

The locality proof controls a natural single-exponential kick at fixed
classical strength, in operator norm, modulo every inverse power of N.
It gives all-polynomial-time conjugacy, arithmetic spectral clusters and an
empty random-origin microscopic process. The maps are smoothly conjugate to
a cat map. This inherited arithmetic structure is essential; the theorem
is not generic BGS universality or a priority certificate.

The robustness addendum proves finite covering estimates for long gaps,
ordered close pairs and random-origin occupancy. All three collapse limits
survive arbitrary unitary norm errors eta_N=o(1/K), even when the error is
larger than the mean spacing 1/D. A nonempty limit after unfolding requires
nonvanishing image-cover length; the explicit Gevrey-2 example forces a
stretched-exponential lower bound on the unfolding's Lipschitz constant.
