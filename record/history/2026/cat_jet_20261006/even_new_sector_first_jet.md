# Uniform first-order splitting on the even-depth new sector

Status: candidate proof, pending independent audit. Let N=7*5^(2l), l>=1. The claim is that every full M_N eigenspace in the orthogonal complement of the first oldform layer H7 tensor J_1 H_(5^(2l-2)) has a simple Hermitian A_N compression. All these eigenspaces have dimension7. This gives local first-order splitting of this sector, not a global or literal-coupling U-simplicity theorem.

The new-sector dimension is7(5^(2l)-5^(2l-2)), or24/25 of N. The remaining old sector is deliberately not included. Complete-eigenspace isolation follows from the separately reviewed oldform_tower_compression.md, including its m=1 endpoint.

## 1. Exact coherent basis

Set h=5^l and n=h². In the CRT prime-power factor use its induced primitive root q of order n. Let psi(x)=h^(-1/2) on h|x and zero otherwise. Then M_n psi=psi and

    <psi,T_n(v)psi>=1_(v in h Z²).

For representatives v in (Z/h)^2, put phi_v=T_n(v)psi. These n vectors are an orthonormal basis: their Gram entries are zero for different labels, and their norms are one. Changing representatives only changes a phase of5-power order.

Exact Weyl covariance gives

    M_n phi_v = theta_v phi_(S v modulo h),
    T_n(u) phi_v = theta_(u,v) phi_(u+v modulo h),    (1)

where every theta is a root of5-power order. In particular every such phase reduces to one at the chosen prime over5. No archimedean approximation is involved.

The first oldform subspace is spanned by phi_v whose two coordinates are divisible by5: apply J_1 to the smaller coherent basis and use T_n(5u)J_1=J_1T_(n/25)(u). Its orthogonal complement is therefore the union of primitive S-orbits.

For Q0(a,b)=2b²-a², primitive vectors have unit norm modulo5. The norm-one torus is generated by S and has order

    L=6*5^(l-1).

It acts freely and transitively on every primitive norm circle

    O_c={v modulo h:Q0(v)=c}, c in (Z/h)^×.

This follows by the same elementary norm count and invertible two-by-two linear system used in the reviewed return classification. Thus each new-sector orbit has exactly L coherent basis vectors.

## 2. Eigenvectors and the exact common denominator

On an orbit O_c, M_n is a monomial cycle with all edge phases of5-power order. Its eigenvectors have coefficients u_k of modulus one, with a normalization factor L^(-1/2). If the eigenvalue reduces to gamma, then gamma is a sixth root and

    u_k reduces to gamma^(-k),

when the orbit is numbered S^k v0. The dual coefficient reduces to gamma^k/L. More precisely, after multiplication by L the exact matrix element of T_n(u) is a sum of integral root-of-unity terms indexed by

    y in O_c such that y+u is also in O_c.            (2)

Its residue is the corresponding sum of gamma_i^(k_(y+u)) gamma_j^(-k_y). This is an explicit eigenvector computation. It does not require the ramified cat spectral projector itself to be integral.

The cat M_n is simple by the separately reviewed uniform cat theorem. Within one monomial cycle the eigenvalue set is closed under multiplication by a cube root, because3 divides L. Therefore all three prime-power eigenvalues used by a fixed full product-cat eigenvalue lie in the SAME orbit O_c. Its complete M_N eigenspace has dimension3+2+2=7.

Choose an integral eigenbasis for the seven-dimensional cat normalized as H7=G7/Tr(G7), with eigenvalues z_i among the three cube roots. If the common full eigenvalue, divided by Tr(M7), has residue xi^t for a primitive sixth root xi, then the associated prime-power eigenvalue residues are

    gamma_i=xi^t/z_i, t=0,...,5.                    (3)

All primitive seventh-root choices are covered by Frobenius conjugation in F_(5^6).

## 3. Reduction of the actual symbol

Write A=4000^(-1)sum_v d_v T7(v) tensor Tn(v). Multiply a seven-dimensional compression by4000L. Formula(2) makes every entry integral in the chosen eigenvector coordinates. At the Gaussian prime i→2, only upper modes a=1 survive: the lower Gaussian coefficient has valuation2 and every diagonal coefficient has positive valuation. The upper b values are0,±1,±6,±7,±8. Their exact coefficients are retained individually, including all aliases.

For a primitive u, simultaneous membership of y and y+u in O_c is equivalent to one linear equation and one quadratic equation. Writing y=-u/2+s Ju, J=[[0,2],[1,0]], gives

    s²=(1-4c/Q0(u))/8 modulo h.                     (4)

If the right side is a nonzero square modulo5, there are exactly two roots, each lifting uniquely from its residue. If it is a nonsquare, there are no roots. If it is zero modulo5 and l>=2, the number of roots is either zero or divisible by5. In that last case all roots reduce to the same s=0, hence to the same pair of orbit labels. Their indices modulo6, and therefore their weights from(3), are identical after reduction. Their total residue contribution is zero.

Consequently, for l>=2 the reduction is computed on the six-point orbit O_(c mod5) in F5², with the degenerate class 4c=Q0(u) modulo5 OMITTED. For l=1 it is computed on that orbit with its one degenerate point retained. These are the only two depth regimes. This reduction preserves the individual actual mode coefficients and the exact seven-factor Weyl matrices.

## 4. The fixed finite matrices

For each c=1,2,3,4 and t=0,...,5, and for each of the two regimes, form the seven-by-seven matrix as follows.

- Take the fixed eigenbasis V7 of H7 and the exact matrices B7(u)=V7^(-1)T7(u)V7.
- Number O_c in F5² by S^k v0, k=0,...,5.
- Put gamma_i=xi^t/z_i.
- For each actual upper mode u=(1,b), define

  Bp_(i,j)(u)=sum_(y,y+u in O_c) gamma_i^(k_(y+u)) gamma_j^(-k_y),

  omitting the whole mode when l>=2 and4c=Q0(u) modulo5.
- Sum d_u B7_(i,j)(u) Bp_(i,j)(u) over the nine upper modes.

This is exactly the residue of4000L times the actual compression. It is not a generic-unitarity surrogate.

## 5. Lift and boundary

The seven-dimensional actual compression, multiplied by the nonzero scalar4000L, has an integral characteristic polynomial in the explicitly constructed algebraic eigenvector basis. Its residue is one of the48 certified squarefree polynomials. Hence its characteristic-zero discriminant is nonzero. Removing the scalar proves the Hermitian compression simple.

Thus every complete new-sector M_N eigenspace splits to first order in exp(itA_N)M_N near t=0, for every even depth2l>=2.
