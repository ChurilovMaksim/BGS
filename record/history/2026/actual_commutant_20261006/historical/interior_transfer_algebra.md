## Uniform raw transfer-block theorem

Let N be odd and 49|N, L=N/7, h=2^{-1} modulo N, and a=1-h modulo N. The actual seven Jacobi paths have starts a+jL and ends a+L-1+jL. Use the actual scalar-normalized metaplectic matrix

G(x,y)=exp(2 pi i [-8^{-1}(3x^2-2xy+3y^2)]/N).

For r=0,1 and t=0,...,6 define a seven-by-seven block

E_(r,t)(j,k)=G(a+L-1-r+jL, a+t+kL).

Thus r=0 is the last row of each path, r=1 is the penultimate row, and t is distance from its start. L>=7 makes all these layers genuine interior/path endpoints.

**Theorem.** Every E_(r,t) has rank one. The unital complex algebra generated by E_(0,0),...,E_(0,6) is isomorphic to C direct-sum M_6(C), and has dimension 37. Adding E_(1,0),...,E_(1,6) gives M_7(C). These statements are independent of v_7(N), and remain valid if 5 divides N.

**Proof.** Set w=exp(2 pi i/7), c=4^{-1}=2 modulo 7. Since L is divisible by 7, the j^2 L^2, k^2 L^2 and jkL^2 contributions to the kernel exponent vanish modulo N. Since a=4 modulo 7 and L=0 modulo 7, direct expansion gives

E_(r,t)(j,k)=gamma_(r,t) w^[(2t+6r+4)j+(t+5r+3)k],

where gamma_(r,t)=E_(r,t)(0,0) is nonzero. Define f_b=(w^(bj))_(j=0)^6. Orthogonality of seventh roots says that E_(r,t) kills every f_b except

b=-t+2r+4 modulo 7,

and sends that exceptional f_b to a nonzero multiple of f_(-2b+3r+5). As t ranges through 0,...,6, these are precisely the seven directed matrix units for the affine permutation

F_r(b)=-2b+3r+5.

F_0 fixes 4. After translating b by 4, it multiplies by -2=5 modulo 7, which has order six on the nonzero residues. Products of its seven individual edge matrix units generate all matrix units within its fixed point and its six-cycle, and none between them. This proves C direct-sum M_6 and dimension 37.

Both F_0 and F_1 are permutations; directed inverse paths are available by traversing their finite cycles. Moreover F_1 composed with F_0 inverse is translation by 3, which is transitive on Z/7. The union directed graph is therefore strongly connected. Products along paths generate every matrix unit, proving M_7. QED.

**Interpretation.** Simply collecting seven independent endpoint directions does not resolve the multiplicity algebra: one terminal row layer retains a genuine 1+6 decomposition. A second terminal row layer removes that obstruction for the *raw transfer blocks*. This is a stronger diagnostic than counting the rank of one 7-by-7 endpoint matrix.

## Why this is not yet the common-commutant theorem

The blocks E_(r,t) are not seven-by-seven compressions known to belong to the algebra generated by actual A_N and M_N with their domains canonically identified. They are transfer matrices between different depth spaces. Independently multiplying them is an auxiliary algebraic construction. Therefore its being M_7 does not imply that an actual common commuter commutes with every E_(r,t).

There is a second obstruction: the FM-008 degeneration uses a depth- and chain-dependent diagonal gauge D. Its blocks become D_(L-1-r)^(-1) E_(r,t) D_t, up to depth scalings. This is not simultaneous similarity of the raw E blocks, so raw block-algebra generation cannot be carried through that gauge without a new argument. At 5|N the valuations of internal bonds vary, adding a genuine filtration issue rather than an omitted finite exceptional case.

Even in the simpler exact surrogate J=J_L tensor I_7, write a general commuter as an upper block-Toeplitz matrix X_(r,s)=C_(s-r) for s>=r, zero otherwise. Then [X,G]=0 gives the **coupled** equations

sum_(d=0)^(L-1-r) C_d G_(r+d,s) = sum_(d=0)^s G_(r,s-d) C_d.

The last-row equation is

C_0 G_(L-1,s)-G_(L-1,s) C_0 = sum_(d=1)^s G_(L-1,s-d) C_d.

The right side need not vanish. Hence the tempting inference that C_0 commutes with all transfer blocks is invalid. The higher Toeplitz coefficients must be controlled, not suppressed by fiat. For the actual A one must additionally justify passage through the degeneration and exclude idempotents lost under a nonintegral specialization.

## Uniform closure for the unweighted Jordan surrogate

This continuation proves the boundary lemma suggested by the computations, then the entire common-commutant assertion for a deliberately identified surrogate. It still does not replace the actual A_N.

**Theorem.** In depth-major order along the actual seven paths, let J=J_L tensor I_7 be the upper nilpotent block shift. For every odd N divisible by49, the complex common commutant of J and the actual raw G_N is scalar.

**Boundary lemma.** In the above Toeplitz equations, just the last two row layers and column layers 0,...,6 force C_0 scalar, independently of the nonzero scalar gamma_(r,t) multiplying each raw transfer block.

**Proof of boundary lemma.** Use the Fourier basis f_b of the multiplicity space. For the last row layer, E_(0,s) is a nonzero multiple of the matrix unit with input b_s=4-s and output a_s=4+2s (all labels modulo7). Its equation is

[C_0,E_(0,s)]=sum_(d=1)^s E_(0,s-d) C_d.

For 0<=s<=6, the right side is supported only on rows a_0,...,a_(s-1), all distinct from a_s. At row a_s and every column other than b_s, the left side is a nonzero multiple of -C_0(b_s,column). Thus row b_s of C_0 has no off-diagonal entries. As s runs over seven values, C_0 is diagonal. At (a_s,b_s), the same equation yields C_0(a_s,a_s)=C_0(b_s,b_s). The affine six-cycle calculation above leaves only two possible diagonal values: one at label4 and one at the other six labels.

Now use the penultimate row equation at column depth s=2:

[C_0,E_(1,2)] + C_1 E_(0,2) = E_(1,1) C_1 + E_(1,0) C_2.

At matrix entry (0,4), E_(1,2) is a nonzero matrix unit. The C_1 E_(0,2) term has its only possible nonzero column at2, while the two right terms have their only possible nonzero rows at5 and3 respectively. All vanish at (0,4). The equation equates the two remaining diagonal values. This proves C_0 scalar. All gamma factors are retained and nonzero, so they cannot affect any support argument. QED.

**Proof of full surrogate theorem for L>=13.** Subtract the scalar C_0 I. If X is nonzero, let d>=1 be the smallest index with C_d nonzero.

If d<=L-7, apply the last-row equation at s=d+t for t=0,...,6. All terms with coefficient index below d vanish. The remaining terms have outputs corresponding to depths t,t-1,...,0, which are seven or fewer distinct Fourier rows. Isolating the row belonging to t gives v_t^T C_d=0, where v_t^T is the raw right Fourier row of E_(0,t). The seven v_t form a basis, so C_d=0, a contradiction.

If d>=7, use rows of depths r=L-d,...,L-1 and column depth s=d. The left side has no coefficient C_e with e>=d because e<=L-1-r<d. On the right only C_d remains after the scalar has been subtracted. Hence G_(r,0) C_d=0 for all these r. Each G_(r,0) is rank one. As r varies through any seven consecutive depths its nonzero right row runs through a full Fourier basis: expanding the kernel, its chain-index exponent changes by c=4^{-1} modulo7 at every step. Again C_d=0.

For L>=13, every positive d satisfies d<=L-7 or d>=7. Since L is an odd positive multiple of7, all L>7 actually have L>=21 and are covered. The only remaining case is L=7, N=49. The exact finite certificate below settles it. QED.

## Exact remaining implication and next handoff

The all-N **actual** target is incomplete. The new uniform result proves that seven-primary interior chains do not obstruct scalar common commutant for the unweighted Jordan surrogate with the actual metaplectic kernel. The 1+6 one-row obstruction and the two-row boundary repair identify exactly why the first endpoint attempt was too weak.

The still-missing bridge is to control the actual coefficient-dependent diagonal gauge and its valuation filtration; one cannot substitute J for A_N while leaving M_N unchanged. This changes the transfer blocks by D_r^(-1) E_(r,t) D_t, not a common similarity on multiplicity space. The Fourier matrix-unit support proof therefore does not automatically survive. Nor does a scalar characteristic-zero surrogate commutant imply scalar commutant of an arbitrary deformation or specialization.

An explicit next object is available. Up to a nonzero factor depending only on t, the normalizing diagonal gauge at depth t and chain j has the form

D_t(j) = w^(jt) / product_(u=1)^t (1+zeta_N^u w^j)^2.

This follows directly from r(q)=-(z^7-1)^2(z+1)^2/(4z^8), since the bond coordinate is z=zeta_N^(u)w^j. All denominators are nonzero for odd N. The omitted factor includes the coefficient -3-4i and the chain-independent z^7-1 factors; these matter for valuations and must be restored there. Thus the next uniform boundary problem is an explicit rational evaluation/Casoratian problem on seventh roots, not an unspecified need for more interior layers. At 5|N, the chain-independent valuation jumps must additionally be tracked. No nonvanishing assertion for that Casoratian is proved here.

## Actual-weight seven-row Casoratian: uniform nonvanishing

The previously open Casoratian paragraph is superseded by this further exact deduction. The leading C_0 boundary and valuation lift remain open.

For q=zeta_N and w=zeta_7, form

V_(t,j)=w^(j(2t+3))/product_(u=1)^t(1+q^u w^j)^2,  0<=t,j<=6.

These are, up to nonzero row scalars, the first seven right rows of the **actual upper-bond-normalizing gauge**. The w^(jt) factor in D_t is essential; dropping it would give a different Casoratian.

**Lemma.** V is invertible for every root of unity q of odd order (in particular every actual odd N divisible by49). No exceptional prime or seven-adic level is omitted.

**Proof.** All denominators are nonzero because q^u w^j has odd order and hence cannot equal -1. Multiply column j by product_(u=1)^6(1+q^u w^j)^2 and remove its nonzero factor w^(3j). The resulting evaluation matrix has rows given by the seven polynomials

p_t(z)=z^(2t) product_(u=t+1)^6(1+q^u z)^2

at z=w^j. Let H(q) be their coefficient matrix modulo z^7-1, so the evaluation matrix is H(q) times the invertible seven-point Fourier matrix. Its determinant P(q) belongs to Z[q].

Reduce modulo2. Each p_t is the square of

h_t(z)=z^t product_(u=t+1)^6(1+q^u z).

The polynomials h_t have degree at most6, minimum degree t, and coefficient1 at degree t. Their coefficient matrix is upper triangular with determinant1. Frobenius squaring squares its entries and permutes coefficient positions by k ->2k modulo7, a permutation because7 is odd. Therefore P(q)=1 modulo2 in F_2[q].

If P(q)=0 at an algebraic-integer q, the identity P=1+2R would imply R(q)=-1/2. But R(q) is an algebraic integer whereas -1/2 is not. Contradiction. Thus P(q) is nonzero at every algebraic integer q, and the stated invertibility follows. QED.

The polynomial and coefficient record are preserved as evidence of the route, not as a substitute for the proof.

**Consequence for the gauge-normalized Jordan surrogate.** If its leading coefficient C_0 is known to be scalar, the minimal-tail proof above now works with G replaced by D^(-1) G D. For d<=L-7, the left column factors differ by one fixed D_(L-1)^(-1), so remain independent, while the seven right rows are exactly the invertible V just proved. For d>=7, the right rows of G_(r,0) retain their Fourier independence because D_0 is a fixed invertible diagonal matrix (equal to identity in the chosen normalization). Thus at L>=21, **C_0 scalar implies the entire gauge-normalized Jordan common commuter is scalar**. This is a conditional exact statement, not yet scalarity of C_0 for the actual gauge or a valid degeneration theorem for actual A.

**A tested stronger shortcut fails.** A possible way to force C_0 scalar would require every overlap v_s^T D_s D_(L-1)^(-1) u_0 to be nonzero. At N49 this is false: s=4,5,6 vanish identically. After extracting chain-independent factors the overlap is the sum over seventh roots of

z^(2s+1) product_(u=s+1)^(L-1)(1+q^u z)^2.

At L=7 and s=4,5,6, its powers lie respectively in [9,13], [11,13], [13,13], containing no multiple of7, so the seventh-root sum is exactly zero. This is a characteristic-zero obstruction, not merely reduction modulo a poorly chosen prime. The first-column shortcut must therefore at least treat N49 separately. Exact modular controls at147,245,343 did not show these zeros; no all-N nonvanishing inference is drawn from that.
