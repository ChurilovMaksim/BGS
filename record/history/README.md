# Historical research record

The living status surface remains intentionally small:
`claim_ledger.md`, `claims.json`, `research_routes.md`,
`retractions.md`, `repository_conventions.md`, and the machine-readable
registries.

Dated reviews in the parent `record/` directory are historical evidence and
do not override the living status surface. They are not physically moved in
this release because many immutable records and manuscript links cite their
existing paths. The curated chronological index is in
`record/history/2026/INDEX.md`.

Future dated operational reviews should normally be created under
`record/history/YYYY/` unless their path is itself part of an established
provenance contract.
