## Evidence levels

- **EXACT** — current algebraic/analytic derivation with explicit hypotheses;
- **EXACT + EXTERNAL-THEOREM** — exact project reduction whose stated input is an identified external theorem;
- **FINITE-CERTIFICATE** — exact finite statement at explicit parameters;
- **FINITE-REGRESSION** — finite implementation/reference agreement;
- **NUMERICAL** — descriptive floating-point evidence only;
- **DRAFT** — route with named unresolved obligations;
- **OPEN** — closure condition not achieved;
- **RETRACTED / SCOPED-HISTORICAL / NOT-PROMOTED** — no current theorem authority.

Repository-wide status authority is the combination of

- `../record/claim_ledger.md` for the maintained repository-wide status,
  including the 20--21 September fixed-map recovery;
- `../record/claim_ledger_addendum_20260907.md` for its explicitly dated
  later/corrected September-7 scopes;

- `../record/verified_state_reconciliation_20260921.md` for the forensic
  disposition of the recovered 14--16 September BGS working dossiers.

The addendum wins only in its stated scope.  Historical dossier labels,
finite verifiers and manuscript prose may not silently strengthen these
authorities.
