# Off/off elliptic pencil: current theorem draft

**Status: DRAFT.** This file records the strongest route that survived the
pair-key repair. It separates present exact reductions from historical
symbolic evidence and from proof obligations that still require
publication-grade closure.

## 1. Where the pencil enters

The corrected fourth-moment distribution is

`B(t)=O(t)+2(p-2)C_H(t)+(p-2)^2 1_{t=0}`.

The mixed term is controlled for fixed audited `K` by
[`cayley_additive_energy.md`](cayley_additive_energy.md). The unresolved
contribution is the centered energy of the off/off term `O(t)`.

After the pair-key and subgroup substitutions, historical symbolic work
organized the relevant fibers as a one-parameter genus-one family and produced
a quartic/Weierstrass candidate, a rational `j`-map, visible reducible fibers
and same-`j` parameter symmetries.

Those calculations are **draft structural evidence**. They are recoverable from
Git history but are not independently regenerated by current CI and are not
promoted here as theorem statements.

## 2. Structural hypotheses suggested by the historical computation

The surviving computation suggests the following proof targets:

1. a generic genus-one fiber after the recorded birational reduction;
2. a nonconstant candidate `j`-invariant, hence expected non-isotriviality on
   the generic locus;
3. finitely many visible degenerate/reducible parameter values;
4. nontrivial same-`j` parameter symmetries that must be quotiented or treated
   as exceptional correlation strata;
5. deleted affine boundary points that must be restored explicitly before
   applying projective trace estimates.

The next proof must re-derive or certify these facts in a current
publication-grade algebraic model. A historical CAS transcript is evidence for
where to look, not a proof object.

## 3. Intended correlation step

The target is a square-root correlation estimate for trace functions of the
family, schematically

`sum_t a_lambda(t) conjugate(a_mu(t)) = O(sqrt(p))`

outside a controlled exceptional relation between `lambda` and `mu`.

A legitimate proof needs more than non-isotriviality. At minimum it must
establish, for the actual sheaves used in the finite-field family:

- a smooth projective model on an explicitly stated open base;
- the complete discriminant/bad-fiber locus, including characteristic
  exceptions;
- purity and lissity on that base;
- geometric irreducibility of the relevant `R^1` sheaf;
- a uniform conductor/ramification bound;
- classification of geometric isomorphisms up to the twists actually present;
- exclusion or explicit accounting of separable and inseparable isogenies;
- treatment of the deleted affine boundary;
- the exact hypotheses of the chosen correlation theorem.

Function-field Tate/isogeny results of Zarhin and quantitative sheaf
technology of Sawin–Forey–Fresán–Kowalski are relevant tools, not automatic
black boxes. Grove–Saad is a useful modern template precisely because the
geometric independence hypotheses are proved before the correlation estimate
is used.

## 4. Conditional target, not theorem

If the correlation step above is established with the required uniformity, the
existing combinatorics lead to the restricted target

`O(K^2 p + K^4 sqrt(p) + K^4)`.

The error is then lower order relative to the intended main scale in the regime

`K=o(p^(1/4))`.

Until the algebraic-geometric and sheaf obligations are closed, both the
estimate and this regime remain conditional theorem-draft conclusions.

## 5. What would still remain

Even a completed off/off estimate would be a moment/fluctuation theorem. A
microscopic BGS/GOE local eigenphase law requires a separate local-law or
comparison mechanism for the structured arithmetic matrix family. The
repository treats that bridge as a different open problem rather than
smuggling it into the elliptic argument.
