# Earlier foundations retained after the reconstruction audit

This file prevents two opposite errors: trusting every historical `PROVEN`
label, and deleting useful earlier work merely because it is not on the newest
proof route. Only the scope stated here is current.

## Finite torus level sets — FINITE-CERTIFICATE

`formalization/Bgs/TorusLevelSets.lean` verifies at `p=199` for
`Q(a,b)=a^2-ab-b^2` and the cyclic action generated by
`A^T=((2,1),(1,1))` that:

- the relevant generator has order `11`;
- every nonzero level has `198` nonzero points;
- the zero cone minus the origin has `396` points;
- every nonzero level has `18` cyclic orbits;
- the action is free on those nonzero levels.

These are fixed finite `native_decide` statements. The analytic Weil
intertwining theorem that historically motivated them is not formalized here.

## Finite branch scale — FINITE-CERTIFICATE

`formalization/Bgs/TorusScaleLaw.lean` checks at `p=199` that the recorded
`a=139` has order `11`, the inverse is `63`, the half-index trace is `123`, and
the branch scales `123` and `76=-123` satisfy

`123^2 = 76^2 = 5 (mod 199)`,

`123*76 = -5 (mod 199)`.

Again this is a finite design certificate, not a general scale theorem.

## Twisted-Jacobi implementation — FINITE-REGRESSION

Maintained scalar-versus-vectorized checks and preserved q-curves validate the
implementation at the recorded designs. Their SHA-256 inputs are pinned in
`computations/verification/twisted_jacobi_regression.py`.

A historical formula omitted the domain condition that the relevant `n1` be
nonzero. On the audited nonzero-q curves that condition is automatic; the
unrestricted historical statement is not retained.

## Antiunitary symmetry — SCOPED-HISTORICAL

The audited anti-linear eigenspace logic is correct. If
`Theta U Theta^-1=U^-1`, `Uv=lambda v`, and `|lambda|=1`, anti-linearity gives

`U Theta v = conjugate(lambda)^-1 Theta v = lambda Theta v`.

Thus the relevant eigenspace is preserved. What is not promoted is a blanket
global GOE-class theorem for every block/chart; that stronger classification
needs a fresh convention-complete treatment if it becomes load-bearing.

## Shape/Catalan and full-group arcsine material — NOT-PROMOTED

Historical exact finite relation counts and moment data motivated useful
Catalan/arcsine conjectures, but the old word-trace algebra had an unresolved
higher rewrite case. Agreement of finitely many moments therefore does not
license an all-orders theorem.

The original relation/shape code and manuscript states remain recoverable from
Git history, in particular the last pre-cleanup archive commit
`d0e414ec26ff7e43ad59e80f77c9d8c679e87c4b`. They are intentionally not
duplicated in the current working tree.

## Higher-rank exploratory diagnostics — NOT-PROMOTED

The historical workspace also contains finite `Sp(4)`/higher-rank
diagonal/coset and kernel diagnostics. They are potentially useful future test
cases, but the associated higher-rank model and global theorem statements were
not re-certified during the present reconstruction. They therefore remain in
Git history as research leads, not as current mathematical results. In
particular, no `Sp(2g)` theorem is inferred from finite diagonal/coset flatness.
