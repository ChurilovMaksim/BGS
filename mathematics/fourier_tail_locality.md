# Fourier-tail coercivity estimate

The full proof of the estimate previously cited under this filename is now
in `manuscript/unified_sections/temporal_resonance.tex`, subsection
"Finite bandwidth and loss of coercivity", equation `eq:temporal-gevrey`.
For fixed hyperbolic A and every Gevrey order s>1 it proves

    0 < c_R(A) <= C_s exp(-c_s R^(1/s)).

The construction uses a separated-support even Gevrey bump, its Fourier
truncations, the Fourier l1 bracket estimate, and finite-Fourier
nondegeneracy. Positivity and deterioration have different quantifiers.
This file repairs the previously dangling proof reference; it makes no new
claim beyond that maintained proof.
