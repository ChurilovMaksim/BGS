# Algebraic spectra, Jacobi minors and cyclicity

These sources analyze the actual Jacobi generator, algebraic spectral separation, Fourier minors and endpoint valuations. They distinguish a generator eigenvector, a product eigenvector and an invariant subspace. Prime-power or bounded-degree certificates retain their precise arithmetic domains; a vanishing leading coefficient of a minor is not a zero of the whole minor. The finite simplicity certificates are evidence for their listed dimensions only.

Qualitative algebraic nonrecurrence does not supply a uniform microscopic spectral gap. The final arithmetic test and its exact valuation calculations are linked from the arithmetic branch. Historical external transcendence inputs retain their named source requirements.
