# Quantum returns, resolvents and dephasing

This branch studies quantum return windows, Weyl overlaps, Schur complements, resolvents and endpoint energy. Exact finite identities are separated from asymptotic decay and from a uniform all-label estimate. A pointwise overlap bound may fail to survive summation over a growing routing family.

Classical intensity contraction and short-time quantum dispersion do not control the balanced signed Heisenberg-scale residual. Countertests below identify which implication fails, rather than claiming every possible dephasing argument is impossible. The full masked endpoint and deterministic microscopic hierarchy remain open.
