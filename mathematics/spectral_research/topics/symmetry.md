# Symmetries and reversals

The symmetry branch treats affine, metaplectic, Clifford and antiunitary candidates separately. Uniform exclusion within a specified classifier is stronger than a finite search but weaker than classifying every possible antiunitary or every product eigenspace. Symmetry removal must preserve the stated modulus, Weyl signs and unitary representation.

These results constrain the relevant symmetry class and failed degeneracy mechanisms. They do not produce signed joint-trace cancellation. Earlier combined pair-commutant results elsewhere in the repository remain scoped to pairs rather than a proof of full propagator simplicity.
