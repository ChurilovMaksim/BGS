# Classical dynamics and trace construction

The analytic route separates the classical generating function, exact plane amplitude, odd-modulus quotient and Gaussian trace-class products. The complete original arguments and author audits are indexed below; import alone is not certification. The 22 October [TRACE-113 reconstruction](../../fixed-map/fixed_kick_uniform_trace_bound.md) now checks the exact comb/amplitude/sector, trace-class continuity and zero-order normalization needed for its all-time upper bound; it does not certify every stronger source claim. Protected fixed points and two-cycles have explicit actions and monodromies. The Round 13 whole-sector removal bounds all exact protected trace terms by $20(83/200)^m$, conditional here on the older amplitude/sector construction; the weighted pair removal then has $O(N^{-1})$ error with every cross term retained. A trace term is not an invariant quantum subspace.

The newly maintained [quartic coordinate-return theorem](../../fixed-map/coordinate_return_quartic.md) is a one-step result. [Inserted traces](../../fixed-map/inserted_trace_coboundary.md) now have a finite-grid proof independent of that all-time construction. [Spectral orbit distance](../../fixed-map/spectral_orbit_distance.md) is proved at the global scale, with an independent upper route via [displacement density](../../fixed-map/displacement_density_trace_bound.md).
