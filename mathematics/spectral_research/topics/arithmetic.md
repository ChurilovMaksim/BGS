# Arithmetic character sectors and routing

Character sectors, canonical oldspaces, seven-chain cuts and prime-power endpoint minors have different invariant objects. In particular, the coordinate Jacobi chains are not sectors of the complete noncommuting product. The final actual-coefficient test proves $v_7(N_r)=s+1$, $v_5(N_r)=t+1$ for $r=3\cdot5^t7^s$; exact hopping zeros remain seven, and nonzero seven-adic valuation does not imply small complex norm.

The last Round 14 source also derives $\|[U,D]\|_{\rm HS}^2=2\|D-\bar dI\|_{\rm HS}^2$ for real seven-chain labels. Rationality of the physical parameter does not create coincident distinct kick phases (qualitative Lindemann–Weierstrass input). None of these statements evaluates the original pair sum.
