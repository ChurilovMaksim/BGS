# Mathematics topic views

The authoritative proof files live under `mathematics/`: at the root and,
since the phase-2 reorganization, in topical home directories (wave by wave;
each wave manifest records the old-to-new path map). These directories
provide the thematic reading organization of a mature research programme
without duplicating sources.

Topic membership is machine-readable in `../../record/mathematics_topics.json`
and checked by the scientific registry audit.

- [Fixed map](fixed-map/README.md), including Lines B–N (29 October – 1 November 2026, TRACE-143–336) in order
- [Trace bridge](trace-bridge/README.md)
- [Cayley arithmetic](cayley-arithmetic/README.md)
- [Returns](returns/README.md)
- [Pell](pell/README.md)
- [Resonance and cohomology](resonance-cohomology/README.md)
- [Locality](locality/README.md)
- [Spectral obstructions](spectral-obstructions/README.md)
- [Information theory](information-theory/README.md)
- [Foundations](foundations/README.md)

The topics follow the phases of the programme only loosely. Phase I (September 2026) is mostly in Cayley arithmetic,
Returns, Pell, Resonance and cohomology, Locality and Information theory; Phases II–V are mostly in Fixed map, Trace bridge
and Spectral obstructions; Phases VI–VII (Lines B–N) are in Fixed map. See [README.md](../../README.md) for the phase table.
