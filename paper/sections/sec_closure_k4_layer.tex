\section{The \texorpdfstring{$k=4$}{k=4} fluctuation layer: corrected status}
\label{sec:r95}

\subsection{The twist identity}

Let $S$ be the annihilator coset ($|S| = m$, the characters trivial on
$H$) and, for a twist pair $(\psi_1,\psi_2) \in S^2$,
\[
  A_{\psi_1\psi_2}(d) = \sum_{p:\, d(p) = d} \psi_1(u_p)\,\psi_2(v_p)\,n(p),
\]
where $p$ runs over the admissible pairs, $(u_p,v_p)$ is the pair key,
$d(p) = (u{-}1)(1{-}v)/(u{-}v)$ the pair difference, and $n(p)$ the key
multiplicity ($n = 1$ off the diagonal key $(1,1)$, $n(1,1) = N{-}2$).
The annihilator orthogonality
$\mathbf{1}[u_1u_2 \in H] = \tfrac{1}{m}\sum_{\psi \in S}\psi(u_1u_2)$
decomposes the coupled energy EXACTLY:

\begin{theorem}[twist identity]
\label{thm:twist}
$B(t) = \tfrac{1}{m^2}\sum_{\psi_1,\psi_2 \in S}
(A_{\psi_1\psi_2} \star A_{\psi_1\psi_2})(t)$ and hence
$E_c = \sum_t B(t)^2$ decomposes into the $m^2$ twist-pair energies.
Verified to all digits at $N = 53$ ($E_c = 3.4105 \times 10^9$, $Z_0 =
12063$) and $N = 199$ ($1.1559 \times 10^{11}$, $96\,641$).
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}
The coset $S$ is indexed by $\operatorname{dlog}(a)$, not
$\operatorname{ord}(a)$: the annihilator condition is
$j \cdot \operatorname{dlog}(a) \equiv 0 \pmod{N-1}$ (at $N = 53$,
$\operatorname{dlog}(10) = 48$ while $\operatorname{ord}(10) = 13$).
Conflating the two produced a $1.86\times$ model error, caught by the
brute-force $Z_0$ cross-check.
\end{remark}

\subsection{The flatness lemma}

\begin{theorem}[flatness]
\label{thm:flatness}
The fiber count of the difference map satisfies
$c(d) = (N{-}2) + \varepsilon(d)$ with $|\varepsilon(d)| \le 2$ for
every $d$ (measured at $N = 53$: $c \in [49,51]$; $N = 199$:
$[195,197]$; no zero bins).  Mechanism: the conic
$(u{-}1)(1{-}v) = d(u{-}v)$ is the M\"obius graph
$v = (u(d{-}1)+1)/(d{-}u+1)$ — at most one solution per $u$ — and each
admissibility exclusion removes $O(1)$ points.
\end{theorem}

Consequently the uncoupled energy $E_{\mathrm{unc}} =
\sum_t (c \star c)(t)^2$ is $N^7$-scale with explicitly computable
corrections.

\subsection{The \texorpdfstring{$\delta$}{delta} envelope, corrected in R108}

The trivial twist contributes $E_{\mathrm{unc}}/m^4$; the nontrivial
twists interfere with relative amplitude $\delta$:

\[
\begin{array}{lccc}
 & E_c & E_{\mathrm{unc}}/m^4 & \delta\\
N = 53, m = 4 & 3.411 \times 10^9 & 3.373 \times 10^9 & +1.1\%\\
N = 199, m = 18 & 1.156 \times 10^{11} & 1.086 \times 10^{11} & +6.4\%\\
N = 3571, m = 210 & 4.459 \times 10^{15} & 3.791 \times 10^{15} & +17.6\%
\end{array}
\]
(The R93/R95 value $3.545 \times 10^{15}$, $\delta = -6.9\%$ at
$N = 3571$ was an artifact of an indexing bug in the R93.2 calibration
script and is retracted in R108; the corrected value is confirmed by
two independent computations agreeing to $4 \times 10^{-12}$.)

\begin{theorem}[the $\delta$ envelope; corrected in R108]
\label{thm:delta}
The trivial twist pair contributes exactly $E_{\mathrm{unc}}$, and every
nontrivial pair satisfies $E_{\psi} \le 256\,N^5$ by Parseval from the
per-fiber Weil bound with the $d{=}0$ carve-out
($|(A \star A)(t)| \le 16 N^2$).  This yields
$|\delta| \le 256\,m^2/N^2 = O(K^{-2})$ \emph{provided} the cross
terms between distinct twist pairs vanish or are comparably bounded.
They do \textbf{not} vanish: measured cross-term shares of the total
correction $m^4 E_c - E_{\mathrm{unc}}$ are $88.9\%$, $99.2\%$,
$99.99\%$ at the three designs (R108e/r108h, exact).  Moreover
$\delta = o(1)$ cannot hold at fixed $K$ (only finitely many split
primes share a given order).  The $\delta$-estimate is therefore
\textbf{OPEN}; the exact pair-type decomposition (R108g) reduces it to
the off-system cross terms.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Closure status after R108}

\begin{corollary}[H3 at $k = 4$; DOWNGRADED to open in R108]
\label{cor:h3closed}
Theorems~92.3 (factorization), \ref{thm:twist} (twist identity for
$B$), \ref{thm:flatness} (flatness) and the corrected per-fiber Weil
bound (with the $d{=}0$ carve-out) all stand, and the exact pair-type
decomposition of R108g reduces the remaining estimate to the
off-system cross terms.  What fails is the last step: the correction
$m^4 E_c - E_{\mathrm{unc}}$ is dominated by cross terms
($88.9$--$99.99\%$), unbounded by any current argument, and the
measured $\delta$ grows along the legacy designs
($+1.1\% \to +6.4\% \to +17.6\%$).  The $k{=}4$ layer is therefore
\textbf{OPEN}.  The directly measured BGS quantity satisfies
$\operatorname{rel}_4 \cdot m \approx 2.0$--$2.4$ across the full
R108 design sweep --- the $1/m$ decay the local-law input needs.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Honest scope}

Closed here: the exact reduction chain (moment factorization, twist
identity for $B$, flatness, per-fiber Weil with carve-out, exact
pair-type decomposition), and the exact computation of $E_c$ at all
calibration designs.  Not closed: the off-system cross-term bound (the

\subsection{Bulk resolvent convergence}
\label{sec:resolvent}

The bulk Stieltjes transform of the variance-normalized parity-odd
coset spectrum converges to the semicircle, but the stored R99 data (deviations 0.359, 0.087, 0.037, 0.038 at $m_+ = 9, 105, 336, 837$) fit an unstable rate of about $m^{-0.6}$ with the last two points flat (R108 re-analysis); the earlier $m^{-1.2}$ claim is retracted.  Two
comparison defects were found and fixed en route: the deviation must
be taken in complex norm, and the semicircle Stieltjes
$(-z + \sqrt{z^2-4})/2$ requires the explicit branch flip
$\operatorname{Im}\sqrt{z^2-4} \sim \operatorname{Im} z$ (the
principal branch is wrong for $\operatorname{Re} z < 0$).

With this, all three inputs of the deterministic local-law assembly
are calibrated for the desymmetrised cat-map coset: fixed-moment
matching (Catalan, $r \le 10$), cross-$q$ fluctuation decay
($N^{-1}$ per layer, Theorem~
ef{thm:evenk} chain), and bulk
resolvent convergence (unstable rate; reanalyzed R108).  The remaining work is the
Erd\H{o}s--Schlein--Yau-type deterministic assembly and the analytic
write-up of the Weil estimate for $\delta_{\mathrm{off}}$ --- no new
mechanisms are missing.
