# BGS research roadmap

This roadmap describes scientific milestones, not promises or deadlines.

## Programme I — fixed-map microscopic universality

**Milestone 1. Heisenberg pair comparison.** Prove the weighted
Heisenberg-scale pair-trace/mixed-resolvent estimate for the correctly
desymmetrized frozen propagator on every compact Fourier support.

**Milestone 2. Higher correlations.** Extend the model-specific estimate to
the signed joint trace products required by the partition-Möbius bridge,
starting with the factorial cubic and all three resonant strips. Retain
the exact finite CUE counterterms. TRACE-010 excludes the simultaneous
unaveraged all-order absolute strengthening; the actual signed estimate
remains open. TRACE-011 deletes the resonant cubic sector once the
actual block mass and local spectral tails are controlled; its
pair-compatible critical clusters exclude a generic pair-only shortcut.
TRACE-012 replaces the sufficient global moment by the smaller projected
square energy. Actual resonant mass, that energy, root allocation and
nonresonant signed cancellation still require model-specific estimates.

**Milestone 3. Point-process closure.** State and verify the exact unfolding,
symmetry class, positivity/tightness, and point-process hypotheses needed to
pass from compact Fourier tests to the desired microscopic law.

**Milestone 4. Independent referee audit.** Obtain line-by-line independent
review of the fixed-map symmetry theorem, Pell crossover, resolvent hierarchy,
and model-specific Heisenberg estimate before a positive BGS claim is promoted.

## Programme II — arithmetic/Cayley closure

Prove a pointwise centered Cayley energy estimate at
`K^(2+o(1))` scale, or an equivalent common-split-divisor theorem strong
enough to close the intended fourth-moment regime. Distinguish pointwise,
density-one, and averaged results at every step.

## Programme III — resonance and cohomology

Resolve whether the nonlinear analytic coboundary equation underlying zero
resonance variance has a nonconstant real-analytic solution. Extend formal
coverage only where it materially reduces proof risk.

## Programme IV — publication and reproducibility

Maintain the consolidated research manuscript as a programme record while
extracting coherent journal papers. Publish immutable research-state releases
with claim registry, dependency map, environment manifest, source archive, and
verification record. Add a DOI only when an external archive actually issues
one.

## Long-horizon extensions

After the fixed-map closure is understood, test which mechanisms survive
outside toral maps and which are arithmetic accidents. The repository should
make negative results and failed proof routes as reusable as positive theorems.
