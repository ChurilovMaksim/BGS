\section{Final consolidation addendum}
\label{sec:final-consolidation-addendum}
This addendum records the repository state after the final recovery pass of
6 September 2026.  It supersedes only delivery-state sentences in the preceding
audit; it does not change the mathematical hypotheses of earlier theorems.
The complete recovery record is
\texttt{record/final\_consolidation\_audit\_20260906.md}.

Three previously omitted arithmetic packets were recovered from their exact
historical Git blobs: the Cayley subgroup-curve route, the generic elliptic
product-row reduction, and the specialization-safe projective Bezout
rank criterion.  The first should be distinguished from the bilinear curve
used in the maintained direct Cayley theorem.  In the recovered route the
polynomial has bidegree $(2,2)$.  Specializing Makarychev--Vyugin Theorem~2
with $m=n=2$ and $g=h=1$ gives the coefficient
\[
 12mng(m+n)=192,
\]
with the stated subgroup-size hypotheses.  This is an auxiliary independent
route and does not replace the sharper maintained bound
$E_+(A)<34K^{8/3}$ obtained from the bilinear specialization
\cite{VM2015,MV2019}.

The projective Bezout criterion is likewise not the retracted two-sided use of
the older dehomogenized $D'/G'$ invariants.  Its matrix nullity equals the
homogeneous gcd degree after specialization, giving an exact determinantal
support description.  What remains open is the growing-$K$ control of the
resulting integral support; no polynomial largest-prime theorem is inferred.
The generic elliptic product-row construction reduces the cross-row problem to
a genus-one fibre product, but a uniform $O(1)$ intersection theorem is also
not claimed.

The maintained build graph was repaired at the same time.  The top-level Lean
module now imports every active \texttt{Bgs.*} file, every maintained C++ source
is a CMake target, and the structural audit treats the new
\texttt{archive/} namespace as provenance rather than active source.  The
canonical manuscript workflow now invokes the fail-closed unified builder in
addition to the legacy arithmetic article.

All divergent historical research branches are retained as exact Git-tree
snapshots.  The original 204-file consolidation ZIP was independently recovered
from the user's delivery library and verified by SHA-256; its byte-level
identity is recorded in \texttt{archive/delivery-20260906/README.md}.  The
binary ZIP itself is intentionally kept as external provenance rather than
re-encoded through a connector path that can truncate long base64 transfers.
This does not promote its historical logs to executions of the final commit.

Finally, the GitHub Actions attempts observed during this finalization did not
receive a runner (no steps executed).  Consequently no cloud-CI success is
claimed here.  This infrastructure fact is kept separate from the mathematical
status.  In particular the positive microscopic BGS/GOE/COE conclusion remains
open: the missing obligation is still the appropriately desymmetrized
Heisenberg-scale joint-trace or local-resolvent control, together with the
high-frequency tail needed to pass from band-limited tests to the full local
law.
