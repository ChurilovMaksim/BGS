\section{Final consolidation addendum}
\label{sec:final-consolidation-addendum}
This addendum records the corrected canonical repository state after the
recovery and cleanup pass of 6 September 2026, followed by the research
continuation above. It supersedes delivery-state sentences in the preceding
audit; it does not silently change the hypotheses of earlier theorems.
The historical repository-shape record is
\texttt{record/final\_consolidation\_audit\_20260906.md}.
Claim status is controlled by \texttt{record/claim\_ledger.md}, with the
explicit proof and correction scopes of the maintained continuation notes.

Three previously omitted arithmetic packets were recovered from exact
historical Git blobs: the Cayley subgroup-curve route, the generic elliptic
product-row reduction, and the specialization-safe projective Bezout rank
criterion. The first is distinct from the bilinear curve used in the
maintained direct Cayley theorem. In the recovered route the polynomial has
bidegree $(2,2)$. Specializing Makarychev--Vyugin Theorem~2 with $m=n=2$ and
$g=h=1$ gives the coefficient
\[
 12mng(m+n)=192,
\]
with the stated subgroup-size hypotheses. This auxiliary route does not
replace the sharper maintained bound $E_+(A)<34K^{8/3}$ from the bilinear
specialization \cite{VM2015,MV2019}.

The projective Bezout criterion is not the retracted two-sided use of the older
dehomogenized $D'/G'$ invariants. Its matrix nullity equals the homogeneous
gcd degree after specialization, giving an exact determinantal support
description. Growing-$K$ control of the resulting integral support remains
open; no polynomial largest-prime theorem is inferred. The generic elliptic
product-row construction reduces the cross-row problem to a genus-one fibre
product, but no uniform $O(1)$ intersection theorem is claimed.

The maintained checkout has one publication surface,
\texttt{manuscript/WoW\_unified.tex}. Obsolete standalone article copies and
the one-shot scripts that regenerated them are absent from canonical
\texttt{main}. The active computational stack is Python/C++/Lean/TeX; Julia is
retired and the structural audit rejects tracked Julia sources or project
environments anywhere in the checkout. Maintained C++ verifiers belong to
the CMake graph and the top-level Lean module is the import surface for
active \texttt{Bgs.*} certificates. Draft Lean sources are not counted as
compiled analytic proofs.

The earlier corrected canonical TeX graph was reconstructed and passed three

The new results distinguish two Fourier variables, retain both oriented
orbits, and separate classical covariance from quantum spectral statistics.
They include a uniform weighted-Fourier synthesis bound, a full translated
resonance-lattice criterion, a two-window phase crossover, sharp polynomial
