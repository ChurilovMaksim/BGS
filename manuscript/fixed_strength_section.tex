\section{Fixed-strength locality and all-orders spectral conjugacy}
Let $\mathcal Cg=g\circ T^{\mathsf T}$ for the fixed cat matrix, and choose a
nonzero real even smooth $g$ with disjoint supports of $g$ and $\mathcal Cg$.
For $f=g-\mathcal Cg$, fixed $\kappa$, and the physical Weyl quantization,
\[
 V_{\kappa,N}=e^{i\kappa N\operatorname{Op}_N(f)}U_N,
 \qquad A_{\kappa,N}=e^{i\kappa N\operatorname{Op}_N(g)},
\]
the companion proof establishes, for every $r\geq1$,
\[
 \|V_{\kappa,N}-A_{\kappa,N}U_NA_{\kappa,N}^*\|_{\rm op}
 \leq \frac{\kappa^2\pi^r}{r!}N^{2-r}
       \mathcal M_r(g)\mathcal M_r(\mathcal Cg).
\]
All local Fourier product coefficients vanish by support separation. The
remaining Taylor integral and a commutator-sensitive Duhamel estimate give
an all-orders operator-norm remainder. No smallness of $\kappa N$ is needed.
The bound survives any fixed polynomial number of iterations, including
the Heisenberg time. An explicit Gevrey-2 example gives the conservative
remainder $O(N^2e^{-cN^{1/3}})$.

On odd Pell spaces, each of the $K$ arithmetic roots retains exactly
$n=(N-1)/(2K)$ eigenvalues in a superpolynomially narrow cluster. The
unfolded gap measure approaches $(1-1/n)\delta_0+(1/n)\delta_n$ with
superpolynomially small transport error. Any fixed positive microscopic
close-pair threshold eventually counts precisely $n-1$ normalized ordered
pairs per level. The random-origin local point process converges to the
empty process while its finite mean intensity remains one. Spacing collapse,
close-pair divergence, and the empty local limit persist under unfoldings
with polynomially bounded derivative.

The classical map is exactly the symplectic conjugate
$\Phi_g^{-\kappa/(2\pi)}T^{\mathsf T}\Phi_g^{\kappa/(2\pi)}$.
It is a fixed nonlinear Anosov map for sufficiently small nonzero $\kappa$,
but it inherits arithmetic structure. This is not a generic BGS theorem,
and not a claim of absent hidden symmetries or world priority. The complete
proof, verification scope, and source audit are in the companion locality
article and \texttt{mathematics/fixed\_strength\_locality.md}.
