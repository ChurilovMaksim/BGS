% DRAFT paragraph 1 (programme framing; mirrors the abstract, adds no results):
This monograph collects the results of a single research programme on
quantum chaos and arithmetic maps, centred on one open question: whether
the microscopic spectra of a fixed deterministic quantum map follow
random-matrix universality at the Heisenberg scale. The question is open;
this volume does not claim it solved.
% DRAFT paragraph 2 (how to read; mirrors front_matter \S Scope):
The chapters are not a linear proof. Each states its own hypotheses,
symmetry class, time scale and spectral object, and the claim index
(Appendix A) records the status of every registered claim separately.
Later chapters do not silently upgrade earlier ones, and chapter
adjacency does not imply a dependency.
