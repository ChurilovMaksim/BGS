\section{Finite-order WKB reduction and its local analytic boundary}
The general near-unitary integer-power and complete partition perturbation
estimate is already proved in the maintained Heisenberg trace-support source,
Section 3. The present contribution instantiates it through the actual
arbitrary-fixed-order WKB construction and explicit signed-table conventions.
For each fixed factorial order $k\geq2$ and fixed compact smooth Fourier
weight, choose a fixed transport order $J\geq k$. The direct finite-shift
transport construction gives $\|U_N-V_{N,J}\|\leq C_JN^{-J-1/2}$.
For either the genuine-inverse Laurent table or the signed-adjoint table,
the complete partition--M\"obius Fourier transform changes by
$O_{k,W,J}(N^{k-J-1/2})$. The estimate retains every partition and zero block.
The proof uses the exact rising-factorial identity
$\sum_\pi|\mu(\pi)|N^{|\pi|}=N(N+1)\cdots(N+k-1)$ and finite-time power
telescoping. The signed comparison to the same ensemble reference is
therefore equivalent at this fixed order. It remains open.

The two tables must not be conflated: negative Laurent powers are inverses,
whereas the adjoint table uses conjugated positive traces after forming each
block sum. Neither table is assigned a positive circle spectral process.
The every-bandwidth positive-measure extension belongs to the actual unitary
spectra only. No fixed finite $J$ covers all orders by this estimate.

Separately, exact finite Gaussian reciprocity represents every finite
$\operatorname{Tr}V_{N,J}^m$, including all branches and winding classes,
without coprimality restrictions. It uses rational matrix inverses and
center-based Abel regularization on a periodic Wiener class. Its unitary
$L^2$ extension is not a point-evaluation or cancellation bound.

The massive local cyclic covariance yields formal connected coefficients
$|\ell_{q,m}|\leq mC^q q!$. For the explicitly defined conditioned rotated
Gaussian box, $h=(2\pi N)^{-1}$ and $mh\leq b$, the actual local theorem is
$Z_{m,h}=\exp(h\ell_{1,m})(1+O_b(\sqrt h))$.
The unconditioned box integral additionally carries its Gaussian box
probability. The separate global rotated-box theorem now controls the
original-trace contour and its summed tail in a specified finite band.
It does not justify absolute summation of local first-loop errors over
exponentially many classes. Signed evaluation, unrestricted bands, TRACE-005
and deterministic BGS remain open.

\paragraph{The unitary polar factor of the principal WKB matrix.}
Let $h=N^{-1}$ and retain the actual fixed coupling and ordinary Weyl
convention.  For the explicitly glued principal four-branch WKB matrix
$W_{0,N}$, the additional amplitude transport and direct discrete Weyl
composition give
\[
 \|W_{0,N}^{*}W_{0,N}-I\|_{\mathrm{op}}=O(h^2),\qquad
 V_N:=W_{0,N}(W_{0,N}^{*}W_{0,N})^{-1/2}
     =\exp\!\bigl(ih\operatorname{Op}_N(b)\bigr)U_N+O_{\mathrm{op}}(h^2),
\]
for a fixed smooth real symbol $b=-\operatorname{Im}(a_1/a_0)$ expressed on
the final canonical graph.  In particular $\|V_N-W_{0,N}\|_{\mathrm{op}}=O(h^2)$.
The smooth symbol is obtained from the exact branch gluing, rather than
inferred from an operator-norm estimate.  The preexisting ergodic
spectral-transport theorem, with the actual logarithmic Egorov and mixing
inputs, supplies a bounded unfolded matching of $V_N$ and $U_N$ with
mean-square displacement $O(1/\log N)$ after a common rotation.  Consequently,
if either unitary endpoint has all compact factorial correlation limits, the
other has the same limits; the all-order reference bounds supply the needed
local occupation moments.  No such limit is evaluated here.  The principal
matrix $W_{0,N}$ may be nonnormal, and its small polar correction is not an
eigenvalue-matching theorem for that matrix or a replacement of the
order-$J$ trace-table reduction by $J=0$.
