\subsection{A finite-grid inserted trace with the required remainder}
Put $h=(2\pi N)^{-1}$ and $t_*=\tau/(2\pi)$. The original cat matrix has
four exact Gauss sheets with initial phase
\[
F_0(Q,s)=-\frac{3Q^2-2Qs+3s^2}{8},\qquad
M_N(x,y)=\frac{e^{3\pi iN/4}}{2\sqrt N}
\sum_{j=0}^3e^{iF_0(x/N,y/N+j)/h}.
\]
For the ordinary-half symbol $a$, solve $F_t=a(Q,F_Q)$. On a fixed
analytic strip and a fixed short time interval the characteristic equation
is a contraction. Its derivative bounds are uniform over the real plane.
The exact conjugated finite Weyl generator has the expansion
$L_h=L_0+hL_1+O(h^2)$, where
\[
L_0b=a_p\partial_Qb+\tfrac12(a_{Qp}+a_{pp}F_{QQ})b.
\]
Solve $\partial_tB_0=L_0B_0$, $B_0(0)=1$, and
$\partial_tB_1=L_0B_1+L_1B_0$, $B_1(0)=0$.
Then $B_0=2\sqrt{F_{Qs}}$ and $B_0+hB_1$ has residual $O(h^2)$.
The exact deck identities
\[
F(Q,s+4)=F(Q,s)+Q-3s-6,\qquad
F(Q+1,s)=F(Q,s-3)-2s+3

pass to both amplitudes. Their four-sheet sum therefore has the exact
wrapped grid action of the original finite matrices.
Its entry residual is $O(h^2/\sqrt N)$, so unitary Duhamel gives
\[

|\operatorname{Tr}(U_N-V_N)|+
|\operatorname{Tr}(A_N(U_N-V_N))|=O(N^{-1}).

Keeping only $B_0$ would not provide this trace error.

Poisson summation with smooth lifted circle cutoffs includes every alias.
The diagonal phases have second derivative $-1+O(\tau)$; their four
stationary classes are the protected fixed points $(q,p)\in\{0,1/2\}^2$.
The kick is stationary there, with values $9/10,9/10,-11/10,-11/10$.
The one-dimensional stationary remainder and the $hB_1$ term each

\subsection{Uniform classical displacement and an independent upper route}

$(x\mapsto\Psi^mx-x)_*dx$ has a continuous density $q_m$ and
\[
\|q_m-1\|_\infty\le\epsilon_Rm\vartheta^{m-1}
\le8\cdot10^{-53},\qquad
\epsilon_R<10^{-56},\quad\vartheta=1-1/8000,
\quad m\ge1.
\]
In Fourier coordinates use the stable/unstable weights
$w_k=\exp(b(|s(k)|-|u(k)|))$, $b=.001$.
The resulting weighted Koopman operator $R$ is trace class and
$\|R-R_0\|_1=\epsilon_R$. Both nonconstant blocks have operator norm
at most $\vartheta$. The linear block has zero diagonal in every
positive power. Hence
\[
\|R_\perp^m-R_{0,\perp}^m\|_1
\le\epsilon_Rm\vartheta^{m-1}.
\]
Finite Fourier compressions telescope the weights exactly; their trace-norm
and unweighted strong limits identify each diagonal with a displacement
Fourier coefficient. This avoids assuming a bounded similarity for the
unbounded weights. Absolute summability of those coefficients proves the
density assertion, also for inverse iterates.

$v_m=\sum_j|b_j-b_j\circ G^m|^2$. The density bound gives

Choosing $\delta=N^{-1/5}$ yields $Ce^{Km}N^{-1/5}$, in particular a
uniform $CN^{-1/10}$ bound for $m\le\log N/(10K)$.
Squared Fej\'er smoothing gives arc discrepancy $O(1/\log N)$ for
