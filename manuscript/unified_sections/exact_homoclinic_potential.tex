\paragraph{An exact stationary potential and homoclinic defect.}
At the literal coupling $\tau=10^{-80}$, the actual real periodic action
correction is a sum of one stationary potential $V_\tau$ along the cat orbit.
Its exact unit-winding limit is the uniformly convergent homoclinic cocycle
\[
 D_\infty(z)=\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}
 \bigl[V_\tau(S^j(z+d))-V_\tau(S^jz)\bigr],
 \qquad d=(1/\sqrt2,-1/2).
\]
With $\alpha=\log(29/5)/\log(3+2\sqrt2)$, the full bound
$\|V_\tau-\tau g/(2\pi)\|_{C^\alpha}\le10^{60}\tau^2$
is explicit at that coupling. The actual isolated Fourier orbit has sum
$|A_{(1,0)}|\ge\tau/1000$. Exact Fourier factorization therefore excludes
an additive $L^2$ translation transfer and shows that the distributional
$q$ derivative of $D_\infty$ is not represented by an $L^1$ function.
The positive derivative measure of $q+D_\infty$ has a nonzero singular part.
These use the project's prior Fourier orbit-sum and constant-tail methods.
They give neither a multiplicative-gauge obstruction nor a decay statement
for the distinct inverse-fibre pushforward measure. Complete quantum weights,
signed trace comparison and BGS remain open.
