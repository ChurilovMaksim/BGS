\subsection{Exact projections and subsequent trace closure}

\[
\inf_B\|A-(B-UBU^*)\|_{\mathrm{HS}}=\|Q\|_{\mathrm{HS}},\qquad
\|Q\|_{\mathrm{HS}}\ge |\operatorname{Tr}(AU)|/\sqrt N.

\|\operatorname{ad}_Q\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2

\subsection{The full weighted sum at the initial parameter}
Let $\phi(\xi)=e^{-1/(1-\xi^2)}\mathbf1_{|\xi|<1}$,
$I=\int\phi$, and $w=(\phi*\phi)^2/(4I^4)$. Define
\[
S_N(t)=N^{-2}\sum_{1\le m<2N}w(m/N)
|\operatorname{Tr}(e^{itNA_N}M_N)^m|^2.
\]
On odd Pell dimensions $N=(\lambda^r-\lambda^{-r})/(4\sqrt2)$,
$\lambda=3+2\sqrt2$, $d=2r$, TRACE-111 proves
\[
S_N(0)=\frac Nd I_w+O(1),\qquad S_N'(0)=O_L(N^{-L}),\qquad
S_N''(0)\le-\frac{289J_2}{80000}\frac{N^5}{d^2},
\]
where $I_w=\int_0^2w$ and $J_2=\int_0^2x^2w(x)\,dx$.
The proof sums all weighted times and every off-clock Hessian term in a
fixed eigenblock basis, without differentiating eigenvalue branches.
Positivity and a uniform third-derivative bound then force positive
curvature at some $t_N=O(d^3/N^2)$. Thus initial concavity cannot persist
to a fixed positive parameter for all sufficiently large Pell dimensions.
This does not place a positive first derivative before that parameter.

The continuation
\[
\mathcal S_N(z)=N^{-2}\sum_mw(m/N)
\operatorname{Tr}U_N(z)^m\operatorname{Tr}U_N(z)^{-m}
\]
is entire. The inverse power, rather than an adjoint at complex $z$, is
essential. If $(d/N)\mathcal S_N$ is bounded by a fixed $M_0$ on a disk
of radius $R_N$, Cauchy's inequality forces
$R_N\le\sqrt{2M_0/c}\sqrt d/N^2$, where $c=289J_2/80000$.
This bounds a uniformly controlled window, not the radius of convergence.
