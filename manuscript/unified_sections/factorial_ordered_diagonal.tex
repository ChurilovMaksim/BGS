\paragraph{Every fixed factorial order under entrywise flatness.}
For canonical positive and negative consecutive cycle presentations, the
Haar-removed ordered-diagonal sector admits a true Gram-inverse compression
even when the mixed two-sided permutation does not preserve its range.
Cutting at every interval endpoint leaves only open strings; zero strings
are exact identity deltas and all repeated ports remain.
For a unitary with $N\max_{a,b}|U_{ab}|^2\le C$, this sector trace is bounded
by $C_{h,C}(1+\log N)^{2h}$ independently of all cycle lengths.
The proof runs as follows (canonical source:
\texttt{mathematics/factorial\_ordered\_diagonal.md}, G1--G5).
The centered Gram operator $H_c$ obeys the same exact Haar moment bound
as in the collective hierarchy, $\|H_c\|\le1/(N+1)$, so the centered
Neumann series for $(I-H_c)^{-1}$ converges absolutely in operator norm.
Writing the sector trace through this series, each summand is a difference
$F_Q(U)-\mathbb E_VF_Q(V)$ over independent Haar products $Q$; the common
scalar $F_J(U)=N^{\mathrm{cycles}(\sigma\pi^{-1})-M}$ is independent of
$U$ and cancels inside each summand. Exact Haar second moments give
$\mathbb E\|Q_j-J\|^2$ decay $(N+1)^{-j}$ up to the dimension factor, so a
fixed cutoff $L=4h+4$ makes the tail $O_h(N^{-3/2})$; the finitely many
initial terms are controlled by maximum-entry Haar moments
$(\mathbb E Y_N^p)^{1/p}\le4\log N+2(p!)^{1/p}$, $Y_N=N\max_{a,b}|V_{ab}|^2$,
which produce the displayed $(1+\log N)^{2h}$ bound. It uses no
position-chain singular gap.

The exact partition--M\"obius formula, including every zero-power stratum,
therefore gives an $O_{k,\widehat\phi,C}((1+\log N)^{2k}/N)$ contribution
for each fixed factorial order $k\ge2$ and bounded compact Fourier weight.
All-zero block powers remain exact constants with no positive replica degree.
The actual-map application inherits only the accepted entrywise flatness.
A separate singular-gap companion gives exponential-in-$N$ estimates for
fixed-many matched long cycles and the two-positive/one-negative long--long
pattern, using shared Haar draws across cycles. Neither estimate controls
the full signed projector complement or proves TRACE-005/BGS.
