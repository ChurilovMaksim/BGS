\section{Uniform physical-kick decay and controlled polynomial replacement}
\label{sec:physical-kick-20261021}

This section records BGS-TRACE-106 and BGS-TRACE-107, with analytic author
proofs and internal self-review on 21 October 2026. The complete maintained
proofs are \path{mathematics/physical_kick_trace_decay.md} and
\path{mathematics/physical_trace_polynomial_approximation.md}.
The imported research snapshot and the limited review coverage are identified
in \path{record/spectral_master_audit_20261021.md}.

For the unchanged modular Weyl quantization $A_N$ and odd $N$, put
$E_N(t)=\exp(itNA_N)$ and $\operatorname{tr}_N=N^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}$.
There is a finite constant $C$, independent of $N,t$, such that
\[
 |\operatorname{tr}_N E_N(t)|\le C(N|t|)^{-1/9},\qquad
 0<|t|\le10^{-20},\quad N|t|\ge1.
\]
The prefactor has not been evaluated numerically. The original
$t=10^{-80}$ is included. This strengthens the imported exponent $1/25$.
For every bounded interval $|t|\le T$ there is also a positive exponent
$c_T$ and a finite prefactor $C_T$.

The ordinary-half Weyl convention is
$\mathsf W_N(k,l)=(-1)^{kl}T_N(l,k)$.
The exact symbol in this convention is
\[
 a(q,p)=\cos14\pi q+\frac{r(q)}{10}
 +\frac{r(q)\sin2\pi q}{100}-\frac1{10}
 +\frac{r(q+1/2)\cos(2\pi p-\alpha_0)}{100}.
\]
In particular, replacing the last coefficient by $r(q)$ would change
the classical symbol and invalidate this proof for the original matrices.

Here is the quantitative localization mechanism. For the flow $\kappa_t$
of $(a_p,-a_q)/(2\pi)$, set $b_1=e^{2\pi iq}$, $b_2=e^{2\pi ip}$,
$d_j=(b_j-b_j\circ\kappa_t)/t$ and $v_t=\sum_j|d_j|^2$, extended
smoothly at zero. Direct derivative estimates give, uniformly on the
displayed small-time interval,
\[
 \operatorname{vol}\{v_t\le\delta\}\le\frac7{34\pi}\sqrt\delta.
\]
Egorov for the two fixed characters has error $O_T(|t|N^{-2})$.
Cyclicity then gives
\[
 |\operatorname{tr}_N(Q_N(ud_j)E_N(t))|
 \le C_T(N|t|)^{-1}|u|_1.
\]
Choose a real smooth cutoff $\eta=\rho(v_t/\delta)$ and use its square.
The symmetric Weyl product cancels its first-order term exactly:
\[
 \|Q_N(\eta)^2-Q_N(\eta^2)\|
 \le C N^{-2}|\eta|_2^2,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{tr}_N Q_N(\eta)^2
 \le\|\eta\|_{L^2}^2+C N^{-2}|\eta|_2^2.
\]
Since $Q_N(\eta)^2$ is positive, its pairing with a unitary is bounded
by its trace. Fourier Cauchy--Schwarz in dimension two requires only
$C^3$ for $|u|_1$ and $C^4$ for $|\eta|_2$.
Writing $R=N|t|$, the resulting estimate is
\[
 |\operatorname{tr}_N E_N(t)|
 \le C_T\bigl(\delta^{1/2}+R^{-1}\delta^{-4}
                         +R^{-2}\delta^{-8}\bigr).
\]
The choice $\delta=R^{-2/9}$ proves the exponent $1/9$.
For arbitrary finite $T$, the stationary circle $q=0$ has nonzero
nilpotent flow linearization. Thus no nonzero time has identity flow;
analytic finite type on the compact time interval supplies a positive
sublevel exponent and the stated bounded-time result.

Consequently, if $0<|t_N|\le T$ and $N|t_N|\to\infty$, the empirical
eigenvalue measure of $E_N(t_N)$ converges to circle Haar measure, and
\[
 \inf_{|z|=1}N^{-1/2}\|E_N(t_N)-zI\|_{\mathrm{HS}}\to\sqrt2,
 \qquad\inf_{|z|=1}\|E_N(t_N)-zI\|_{\mathrm{op}}\to2.
\]
In particular $\inf_{|z|=1}\|U_N-zM_N\|_{\mathrm{op}}\to2$.
This is a matrix comparison, not a comparison of unordered spectra
after arbitrary conjugation.

There is also a controlled polynomial replacement at the actual argument
$N\tau$. Since $\|A_N\|\le a_*=59/50$, a Chebyshev--Bessel polynomial
of degree $2a_*\tau N+O_K(\log N)$ approximates $E_N(\tau)$ with norm
error $N^{-K}$. Telescoping trace products and the partition--M\"obius
formula give an error $O_{k,b}(N^{k-K})$ for each fixed factorial order
$k$ and fixed compact Fourier bandwidth. Negative trace indices of the
nonunitary approximant are defined by adjoint powers. A single degree
$2a_*\tau N+O((\log N)^2)$ works for all fixed orders by choosing
$K=\log N$. No positivity or point-process interpretation of the
approximating trace polynomial is asserted.

On the Pell dimensions $N=xy$, $(1+\sqrt2)^r=x+y\sqrt2$ with odd $r$,
the first nonzero scalar two-copy alias has length exactly $x\asymp\sqrt N$.
Hence the fixed-word two-copy approximation does not extend uniformly
to the degrees needed by the physical exponential. The complete remaining
signed trace sum is not evaluated. BGS-TRACE-005 and BGS-BGS-001 remain open.
