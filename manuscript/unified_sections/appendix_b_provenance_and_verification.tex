\section{Verification, provenance, and the remaining positive problem}
\label{sec:verification}
The mathematical manuscript, its exact certificates, finite matrix checks,
software integration and external literature support are distinct layers.
Agreement of two programs checks implementations of a finite assertion; it
does not prove an asymptotic statement outside its certified range.

\subsection{Repeated checks in this consolidation}
The nondegeneracy package was reconstructed from the delivered archive and
its complete 14-command verification rerun. It rebuilt the C++ interpreter,
regenerated the radius-one, radius-three and radius-eight orbit equations,
replayed the proofs independently, and recomputed the quantitative margins,
Hessian identities and finite quantum variance controls. The radius-eight
proof contains 5704 steps for 1475 orbit equations in 144 complex coefficients,
i.e.\ 288 real Fourier parameters. Source fingerprints were checked across
the run. The small proof language remains an ordinary exact executable
certificate, not a Lean kernel proof.

A further 36-command historical component run completed with zero failures:
eight C++ compilations and their independent executables, finite-field
subresultant and reflection checks, Frobenius amplification, elliptic
spike controls, the exact interval computation of the fourth-moment constant,
Pell Gaussian/Floquet/weighted-echo tests, robust covering inequalities,
sharp-orbit and four-mode checks, and smooth/mixed-kick regressions.
This is a named component suite, not an assertion that every historical
script, every branch and every external theorem has been reverified.
The original sources were not edited to obtain successful outputs. The same
36 commands were then replayed from a hash-indexed frozen input archive in
a new isolated directory. All 36 passed again, with 362 scientific input
files unchanged. This second execution checks portability and reproducibility;
it does not count as 36 new mathematical assertions.

The exact interval computation reconfirms

 \begin{aligned}

 \gamma&\leq5.614220692151198071627965241017794630770616850083857347522849.

The earlier decimal beginning $5.612$ is not the value of the stated series.
That old transcription error had already been recognized; the present run
independently confirms the correction, rather than counting its discovery
again. The two-species constant in the coboundary fourth moment is a different
number and is not substituted for this one.

The new return-word tests have three independent components. Exact integer
C++ code checks the free-word cancellation on eleven anti-palindromic words
and all thirty rational support-separation obligations. A four-site block
model with noncommuting two-by-two matrices verifies the product gauge and
rejects the linear-exponential substitute. Finally physical torus Weyl
matrices constructed by the exact two-grid nodal identity test the actual
single exponential. The latter matrices use floating-point arithmetic.
Their large finite errors at stronger kicks are retained; no decay exponent
is fitted or inferred from them. Classical trajectory comparisons test the
composition order and sign, but do not replace the exact support/flow proof.

The signed-trace arithmetic was separately checked for a family of positive
and negative integer traces and prime periods, together with direct small
matrix spectra on both uniform parity sectors. A deliberately excluded
ramified period is retained as a negative control: $t=-3,K=5$ gives $N=5$
and $\gcd(N,|t|+2)=5$, violating invertibility of $A-I$. An early test
fixture incorrectly included this case among admissible spectra; the
hypothesis check now rejects it. The theorem was not weakened to make the
fixture pass.

\subsection{Proof-review findings and conservative corrections}
Several apparent advances in the historical messages were duplicate versions
of already present lemmas. In particular the regular-gradient analytic
rigidity argument and the Pell-only sharp finite-span extremum are credited
to their first preserved project statements. Their generalizations and new
consequences are separately identified. Competing normalizations of the
classical kick differ by $2\pi$; this manuscript records the conversion
rather than using the same parameter symbol with incompatible meanings.

The new noncommuting construction does not invalidate the proved
nondegeneracy theorems. Its generators have infinite Fourier support,
mixed Fourier signs and open critical sets. It lies outside the radius-eight
certificate, the one-sign criterion, the regular-gradient analytic class,
and the nonzero-Hessian sufficient condition. It does settle a stronger
exceptional mechanism: $\{g,\mathcal Cg\}\ne0$ can coexist with
$\Gamma(g)=0$ and a nonlinear all-orders product gauge. The invalid
implication from a nonzero bracket to a positive orbit variance is never
used in the consolidated theorem chain.

Our proof review included domains, parity restrictions, Weyl sign lifts,
normalizations of the trace and form factor, finite-to-infinite limits,
exact versus approximate conjugacy, and finite-certificate soundness.
External-source-dependent arithmetic statements remain marked by their
named inputs. This is not a claim of complete independent referee acceptance
of every proof in the historical corpus. The accompanying audit records
precisely which statements were rederived, which were checked by finite
regression, and which rely on preserved external inputs.

\subsection{Literature boundary}
Cohen--Conze's classical cohomological variance statements require their
regularity and action hypotheses; their classical CLT is not used in place
of a quantum proof. Fejer--Riesz supplies a spectral factor only after
positivity has been established. Prosen--Znidaric's residual/fidelity
mechanism is a direct precedent, not a theorem about the particular
fixed-strength eigenphase clusters constructed here. Keating--Mezzadri's
nonlinear arithmetic pseudo-symmetries prevent equating mere nonlinearity
with removal of arithmetic structure. General matrix-cocycle Livsic theory
is background; the return-word factorization in this paper is proved
explicitly and does not import a Livsic theorem with unchecked hypotheses.

The current source pass read the scalar factorization statement and its
root-pair proof, the residual commutator formulas, and the classical
orbit/coboundary criterion in the primary texts. The bidegree-$(1,1)$ subgroup-curve input was checked directly against
Vyugin--Makarychev, Theorem~2, including the condition $\deg P(x,0)\geq1$
and the subgroup-size range. Some other older finite-field
sources could not be reacquired in full; their previously recorded input
statements and conditional deductions are preserved, not newly certified.
A title-only retrieval in the old record returned an unrelated paper and
has been excluded. Targeted searches failing to find our exact construction
do not establish its originality. No priority conclusion rests on a missing
search result.

\subsection{The remaining mathematical target}
The finite-field energy/support reductions and the positive spectral
universality problem are separate. For the latter, one needs a fixed
nonarithmetic quantum family, a precise symmetry resolution and unfolding,
and joint trace or equivalent local-resolvent control at the Heisenberg scale.
A global Gaussian observable law, a finite polynomial correction, an
open-dense nondegeneracy statement, and an exceptional all-orders conjugacy
do not supply that result. The noncommuting return-word theorem makes this
separation sharper: even a nonzero principal commutator can conceal an exact
cohomological cancellation and a product gauge. The positive BGS conclusion
is not proved in this manuscript.

For the fixed deterministic family the comparison is a signed weighted
sum. The TRACE-010 continuation excludes its simultaneous unaveraged
all-order absolute CUE strengthening: absolute pair agreement would force
the normalized absolute cubic discrepancy to grow at least as
$\sqrt N/1024$ on a fixed positive Fourier box. The absolute sufficient
implication remains correct, including averages taken before the modulus.
The fixed-map chapter records the viable signed target and the limits
of its reference variance and formal phase controls.
