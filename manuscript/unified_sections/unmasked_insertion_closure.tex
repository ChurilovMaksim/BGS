\paragraph{Three pinches close the complete convex hierarchy.}
For a gap vector $\mathbf n=(n_1,\ldots,n_j)$ with $n_i\ge0$, define
\[
 G_{j,N}(\mathbf n;\theta)
 =\operatorname{Tr}_H\!\bigl[D_\theta T_\theta^{n_1}\cdots
 D_\theta T_\theta^{n_j}\bigr],
\]
where $T_\theta=\operatorname{Ad}(Z_\theta U)$ and $D_\theta$ is the
corresponding centered pinch superoperator, as in the canonical source
\texttt{mathematics/right\_frame\_unmasked\_pinch\_hierarchy.md} and its
exported proof (equations (18)--(23) of
\texttt{evidence/wave3\_checked\_20261007/raw/right\_overlap/}
\texttt{authorized\_exports/right\_overlap\_and\_unmasked\_insertions.md}).
On the ordinary HS traceless space, collective compactness gives, for each
$\varepsilon>0$, a fixed $R$ with $s_{R+1}(D_0)\le\varepsilon$ eventually,
where $s_i$ are the singular values of $D_0$. Splitting the singular values
and using $\|D_0\|\le1$ with the flatness estimate
$\|D_0\|_{S_2}^2\le C_{\rm flat}N$ gives
\[
 \|D_0\|_{S_3}^3
 =\sum_i s_i^3
 \le R+\varepsilon\,\|D_0\|_{S_2}^2
 \le R+\varepsilon C_{\rm flat}N,
 \qquad\text{hence}\qquad
 \eta_N:=N^{-1}\|D_0\|_{S_3}^3\longrightarrow0.
\]
Thus $\|D_0\|_{S_3}^3=o(N)$, with no quantitative rate for $\eta_N$.
Since $V_\theta$ is unitary, $D_\theta$ has the same singular values as
$D_0$ on the traceless space. Schatten--H\"older with three $S_3$ factors
and bounded remaining factors therefore gives, for every $j\ge3$,
\[
 |G_{j,N}(\mathbf n;\theta)-1|\le\|D_0\|_{S_3}^3=N\eta_N,
\]
uniformly in occupation phases, all gaps and insertion count. The identity
contribution $1$ is retained exactly. No pointwise $o(1)$ bound on
$G_{j,N}-1$ is asserted or needed: the closure below is at the normalized
aggregate level. Averaging over the $\binom{m-1}{j-1}$ gap compositions
gives $0\le A_{j,N,m}(\theta)\le1+N\eta_N$ for $j\ge3$. In the expansion of
$\operatorname{Tr}[zT_\theta+(1-z)D_\theta]^m$ the order-$j$ coefficients
are the binomial weights $\binom mj z^{m-j}(1-z)^j$, whose sum over
$j\ge1$ is $1-z^m\le1$. Hence, with $O(N)$ band terms at the $N^{-2}$
normalization, the $j\ge3$ tail contributes at most
$N^{-2}\cdot O(N)\cdot(1+N\eta_N)=O(N^{-1})+O(\eta_N)=o(1)$.
This does not bound the first two orders pointwise by compactness alone.
Their independently controlled complete averages (the preceding paragraph),
combined with the three-pinch tail, close every complete unmasked convex
insertion mixture with mass at most one, uniformly for every
$z_N\in[0,1]$, at the stated $N^{-2}$ band normalization. There is no
quantitative decay rate, growing-$r$ stationary phase estimate or
effective startup.

\paragraph{Sharp unsigned logarithmic obstruction.}
The actual whole-centered contraction, with $\kappa=1-\rho>0$, gives
$F_m(z)=1+o(1)$ uniformly on $aN\le m\le bN$ when
$1-z=A_0\log N/N$ and $A_0>2/(\kappa a)$. The unweighted complete positive
interval sum satisfies
\[
 N^{-2}\sum_{m=\lceil aN\rceil}^{\lfloor bN\rfloor}
 z^{-m}F_m(z)
 =(1+o(1))\frac{N^{bA_0-1}}{A_0\log N}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
This is an actual unsigned obstruction. At finite size the pinch correction's
common scalar part is $1-z^m$; it cancels exactly after signed occupation
subtraction and is excluded by the relevant projection. The divergence does
not refute T16. A signed proof must retain this cancellation before absolute
bounds. Hamming/high-switch extraction, source derivatives and rates, signed
centered deconvolution, undamped zero-insertion comparison, microscopic
