\section{Rank-two four-cut classification and its bounded certificate}
For two distinct reduced four-block decompositions with pairwise distinct
port values, a specified rank-two relative position permutation between
cyclically aligned equal outputs is a product of two transpositions on four
consecutive alternating source vertices. Its cut sets are exactly
$\{s,s+1,s+4,z\}$ and $\{s,s+3,s+4,z\}$, where $z$ lies outside the cluster
and its endpoint. A three-cycle cannot occur. The canonical mathematical
proof in \texttt{mathematics/four\_cut\_rank\_two\_classification.md}
retains the support, its immediate successors, and the first cut set,
reducing to at most twelve or ten vertices and preserving genuine cuts.
This contraction and the endpoint-preserving lift were independently reviewed.

The compiled \texttt{Bgs.FourCutKernel} module certifies only the executable
finite predicates: 3858 pair configurations with 560 directed local matches
for sizes five through twelve, and 650 oriented triple configurations with
no matches for sizes five through ten. These use \texttt{native\_decide}
and its declared native-evaluation trust. The size-four exclusions use
ordinary \texttt{decide}. The abstract compression, cyclic-word correspondence,
and analytic cancellation are not Lean theorems here. This bounded certificate
alone asserts neither complete four-block unmarking nor BGS universality.
