\section{Position-chain mixing and two scoped path contributions}
\label{sec:actual-path-theorems}
Keep the unchanged odd-dimensional quantization
$U_N=\exp(i\tau N A_N)M_N$ at the fixed coupling $\tau=10^{-80}$.
All limits in this section are through odd $N$. These are subsidiary
project results with independent internal review, not a deterministic BGS
claim, a priority claim, or an external referee certificate.

\subsection{Position-chain mixing and the cyclic contribution}
Put $P_N(x,y)=|U_N(x,y)|^2$ and $J_N(x,y)=1/N$. The position-chain
estimate is
\[
 \limsup_{N\to\infty}\|P_N-J_N\|\le10^{-64}.
\]
Consequently, for all sufficiently large odd $N$, simultaneously for every
integer $L\ge0$,
\[
 \|P_N^L-J_N\|\le2^{-L}.
\]
Indeed, $P_N$ is doubly stochastic, so $P_NJ_N=J_NP_N=J_N$ and hence
$(P_N-J_N)^L=P_N^L-J_N$; the displayed estimate
$\limsup\|P_N-J_N\|\le10^{-64}<1/2$ then gives
$\|P_N^L-J_N\|=\|(P_N-J_N)^L\|\le\|P_N-J_N\|^L\le2^{-L}$.
The threshold is not effective. This is a singular-norm estimate and does
not assume that $P_N$ is normal.

Here is the proof mechanism, with complete details in the canonical source.
An exact four-term quadratic Gauss identity, followed by two finite-difference
transport equations and unitary Duhamel, gives a four-branch kernel with
entry error $O(N^{-3/2})$. Its four branch-diagonal squared amplitudes yield
$N^{-1}d(x,y)$ with $|d-1|<10^{-64}$. For every distinct branch pair, the
mixed derivative of the difference phase has a Fourier coefficient whose
linear term is
\[
 -\frac{\pi\tau}{128}e^{-i\alpha}
   \bigl(e^{\pi i r/2}-e^{\pi i s/2}\bigr),
\]
and whose remainder is bounded by $10^{50}\tau^2$. It is nonzero at the
specified coupling. The mixed derivatives also have absolute value less
than $1/4$. A local discrete $TT^*$ summation-by-parts argument therefore
excludes all lattice aliases and gives squared norm $O(\log N/N)$ away
from the analytic zero set. A small-area cutoff is controlled in
unnormalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm. Hence the twelve off-diagonal branch
kernels are $o(1)$ in operator norm. Double stochasticity gives the stated
simultaneous power estimate.

For a cyclic coordinate word $x$ of length $m$, let $a(x)$ be its path
amplitude and $d(x)$ its least cyclic period. The exact cyclic contribution
is $D_m=\sum_xd(x)|a(x)|^2$. Writing $q_N=\max_{x,y}|U_N(x,y)|^2=O(N^{-1})$,
\[
 |\operatorname{Tr}P_N^m-1|\le N2^{-m},\qquad
 0\le m\operatorname{Tr}P_N^m-D_m\le m^2Nq_N^{m/2}.
\]
Thus $D_m/m\to1$ uniformly on each fixed band $aN\le m\le bN$,
$0<a<b<\infty$.

\subsection{A precisely marked three-link contraction}
With cyclic indices modulo three, define
\[
 E_{\rm dist}(L)=
 \sum_{\substack{a_1,a_2,a_3\ \mathrm{distinct}\\b_1,b_2,b_3}}
 \prod_{i=1}^3(P_N^{L_i})_{a_i b_i}
 \prod_{i=1}^3 U_{b_i a_{i+1}}
                  \overline{U_{b_i a_{i-1}}},\qquad L_i\ge0.
\]
Replacing any one link by $J_N$ gives zero exactly, by distinct-column
orthogonality. Replacing the longest link by $P_N^{L_i}-J_N$ then gives
\[
 |E_{\rm dist}(L)|\le C^3N\,2^{-\max_iL_i},
 \qquad q_N\le C/N.
\]
This includes zero-length links. For $m\ge3$, summing all
$L_1+L_2+L_3=m-3$ and multiplying by $m^2$ costs at most
\[
 C^3m^2\binom{m-1}{2}N\,2^{-(m-3)/3}.
\]
Its normalized smooth compact positive Heisenberg-band sums vanish.
This is a marked expression only: finite primitive path pairs have different
representation multiplicities, and canonical masks can destroy the free
endpoint contraction. No once-counted unmarked family is estimated here.

\subsection{The reversed-coordinate-path family}
Let $Q_N(x,y)=U_N(x,y)\overline{U_N(y,x)}$. The separate reversal theorem
states $\|Q_N\|\to0$. Its sixteen phases use
$S_r(x,y)-S_s(y,x)$, whose mixed coefficient has magnitude
$\pi\tau/128$ with remainder at most $10^{50}\tau^2$.
The same shared discrete norm lemma applies.
Let $R_m^{\rm rev}$ sum ordered pairs related by reverse cyclic rotation,
excluding all cyclic pairs. Exact periodic-word and reflection counts give
\[
 |R_m^{\rm rev}-m\operatorname{Tr}Q_N^m|
 \le2m^2Nq_N^{\lfloor m/2\rfloor}.
\]
For the even edge-axis reflection there are $m/2-1$ paired edges and two
endpoint loops; both loop factors are retained. It follows that
$R_m^{\rm rev}/m\to0$ uniformly on every fixed positive Heisenberg band.

\subsection{The signed boundary remains open}
For every $w\in C_c^\infty((0,\infty))$,
\[
 N^{-2}\sum_m w(m/N)D_m\longrightarrow\int_0^\infty \xi w(\xi)\,d\xi.
\]
Consequently the still-unestimated other-path remainder
$R_m^{\rm other}=|\operatorname{Tr}U_N^m|^2-D_m-R_m^{\rm rev}$ must satisfy
\[
 N^{-2}\sum_m w(m/N)R_m^{\rm other}
 \longrightarrow\int_0^\infty(\min(\xi,1)-\xi)w(\xi)\,d\xi
\]
for the pair CUE target. The correction is negative above $\xi=1$.
Neither the marked estimate nor position-chain mixing proves this signed
limit. The higher factorial hierarchy, TRACE-005 and deterministic BGS
remain open. Exact finite verification supports only its stated algebraic
coverage; no analytic theorem here has been formally certified.
