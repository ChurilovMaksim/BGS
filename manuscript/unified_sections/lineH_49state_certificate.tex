\begin{theorem}[Kappa lemma and 49-state lifted identity, BGS-TRACE-207, proved]
For every odd \(m\) with \(3\nmid m\) and \(s=\pm1\), \(\kappa=-k u y'\bmod 8\) is odd, so \(c_b=8^{-1}\sum_{t\bmod 8}e((\kappa t^2-bt)/8)\) has \(\lvert c_b\rvert=\tfrac12\) for even \(b\) and \(c_b=0\) for odd \(b\). The lifted operators \(V_b,E_b\) of BGS-TRACE-197 with real shifts \(x=n/7\) tensored with the monomial matrices \(U_n\) on \(\mathbb C^{49}\) of the BGS-TRACE-194 law are trace class, and \(Z_m(\nu_{7,s})=(1-\lambda^{-m})\sum_{b\in\{0,2,4,6\}}c_b(\operatorname{Tr}V_b^m-\operatorname{Tr}E_b^m)\) for the full alphabet.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Certified spectrum for the Line B kick, BGS-TRACE-208, proved (computer-assisted)]
At \(\eta=2\), \(s=-1\): \(V_0\) has exactly one eigenvalue \(\mu^*\) in \(\lvert z\rvert>1.5\); it is algebraically simple and \(\lvert\mu^*\rvert\in[1.846834,1.946835]\), above \(\lambda^{1/4}\approx1.5538\). The rest of \(\sigma(V_0)\) lies in \(\lvert z\rvert<1.5\), \(\sigma(V_b)\subset\{\lvert z\rvert<1.45\}\) for \(b=2,4,6\), \(\sigma(E_b)\subset\{\lvert z\rvert<1\}\) for \(b=0,2,4,6\), and \(\lVert V_b\rVert_1\le10672.622\), \(\lVert E_b\rVert_1\le1106.332\). The \(V_0\) certificate of record is the referee's independent re-certification.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[All-m growth for the Line B kick, BGS-TRACE-209, proved (computer-assisted)]

\begin{corollary}[Conjecture R as stated is false; BGS-TRACE-179, refuted]
For every compact range \([\eta_0,\eta_1]\ni2\), \(\max_{\eta\in[\eta_0,\eta_1]}\lvert\tilde W^{\mathrm{rest}}_m(\eta)\rvert\) is not \(o(m)\) along odd \(m\). Hence Conjecture R (BGS-TRACE-179) as stated is false. R\(_{\mathrm{NSW}}\), formerly part of BGS-TRACE-179, is registered as BGS-TRACE-210 (open): it is violated at every \(m\ge47\) with \(h_m\) prime, and whether there are infinitely many such \(m\) is unknown. R restricted to compact ranges avoiding \(2\) is open. The conclusion of BGS-TRACE-196 for \(s=-1\) holds on the lifted space; \(H_{\infty,7}\) as stated there is neither proved nor needed, and BGS-TRACE-196 stays conditional.
\end{corollary}
