\noindent\textbf{Scope note.} This conclusion
summarises only what the preceding chapters and the claim registry
already record, and it marks every open obligation as open. It is not
a claim of completion.

\subsection{What this volume establishes}
The chapters fall into distinct regimes, and their results do not
combine into a single universality claim. The finite-field and Cayley
chapters give exact combinatorial reductions (pair keys, Cayley energy,
fixed-degree row polynomials, scalar supports) together with
range-restricted or conditional arithmetic estimates. The return-law
chapters give asymptotic laws on explicitly declared arithmetic
subsequences, regularity scales, and observables. The resonance and
cohomology chapters give sharp coercivity and rigidity results,
counterexamples in distinct smooth, analytic, linear-transport, and
nonlinear regimes, and an explicit noncommuting return-word exception.
The fixed-map chapters give exact trace and resolvent identities,
selected interference sectors, and conditional bridges toward the
microscopic target. The tomography and minimax chapters are a separate
branch, with their own dynamics and source and measurement assumptions,
and are not results about the unchanged deterministic fixed-strength
spectrum.

Two reductions deserve explicit mention because later chapters build
on them. The fixed-map position-chain singular mixing, cyclic ramp,
and marked three-block results are registered as proved, and the
phase-model transfer built on them is proved in the repo-model reading
of the transfer while remaining conditional in the literal reading of
the external transfer text; the written dictionary between the two
readings has since landed as mathematics/fixed-map/r1\_dictionary.md
(90c3b18), leaving only the R-L6 residual at \(R \le 3\). No chapter in this
volume silently upgrades a conditional bridge into a proved theorem.

\subsection{What is conditional, pending, or unresolved}
As registered on 5 November 2026, the claim registry contains 438 claims: 353
proved, 17 verification-pending, 25 conditional, 42 open, and 1 refuted. The
19 claims of 4--5 November (BGS-TRACE-337..355; 15 proved, 4 conditional)
narrow the open inputs H5--H7$^*$ of Theorem~R$^\sharp$-mod~v5 without
closing any of them; H5--H8 stay open and the theorem stays conditional. (On
29 October, before Lines B--N, it contained 207 claims: 181 proved, 12
verification-pending, 9 conditional, and 5 open.) Conditional claims are
labelled as such at their statements, and verification-pending claims
are not presented as proved. Among the latter, the radius-eight
resonance nondegeneracy result stays verification-pending; its
promotion is a separate gated decision, not a consequence of this
chapter. External results used in the finite-field and spectral
chapters retain their named external hypotheses, and the bibliography
collects uncited entries separately under Further reading rather than
attaching them to points of use.

The reported proofs have not received a blanket independent external
referee or novelty certification. That is a statement about the
present status of the volume, not a disclaimer about any individual
proof: individual results carry their own evidence, finite
certificates, or replay records, as recorded in Appendix~B.

The operator-resolution continuation supplies a pair-test transfer through
$O(N^{-2})$ approximants and a complementary short-trace information barrier.
It does not supply an approximant with a proved CUE limit.
The kick-blind spike theorem records an explicit arithmetic family on which
the fixed kick keeps the Abel pair statistic a distance greater than \(1/2\)
from the CUE value, through the 77-digit dimension \(u_{201}\). The
quadrature argument at \(u_{203}\) moves the same explicit threshold one
odd Pell step further, to 78 digits. The positive-time expansion at
\(u_{205}\) moves it again, to 79 digits. At \(n=207\) the pure
second-order center still satisfies \((1-\varepsilon_2)^2<3/4\), so that
disk cannot prove the gap. The order of \(C\) modulo \(N\) splits the
same statistic: a short return keeps the pure cat map above \(3/4\), while
an odd prime whose return is at least half the dimension lies within
distance \(1/2\) of the CUE value, and the fixed kick preserves both sides
through explicit digit windows. The same order is a trichotomy: every odd
prime is short, long, or split-half, the prime \(p=7\) lies above \(3/4\),
and every odd prime power is decided by the lifting depth. That
classification is not a generic limit.
None of these estimates closes the signed hierarchy.

Lines B--H (29--30 October) turn to a fixed diagonal kick. The exact
Pell-norm trace formula gives the short-time law $B_m=2m$ for $m\le8$,
and a computer-assisted 49-state certificate shows that Conjecture~R
(BGS-TRACE-179) fails at $\eta=2$; it is registered refuted. Lines I--N
(31 October--1 November) study the corrected mean law $R'$
(BGS-TRACE-206) along $m=3p$. They prove an exact reduction of $R'$, the
growth of the $d=7$ contribution (Theorem~J), Theorems K3, W, W$^+$, N3
and C$'$ at their stated scope, and end in Theorem~R$^\sharp$-mod~v5
(BGS-TRACE-325), which is conditional on the open inputs H0, H1 and
H5--H8. $R'$ is open and is not refuted. These lines test one ingredient
of the trace hierarchy; they do not address BGS-TRACE-005 directly.

\subsection{The open target}
The terminal open obligation is the signed Heisenberg-scale
desymmetrized joint-trace estimate for the fixed map (BGS-TRACE-005),
and with it the fixed-strength microscopic universality claim
(BGS-BGS-001). Both are registered open, and this chapter does not
close them. For the fixed
deterministic family the comparison is a signed weighted sum: the
necessary pair-scale plateau correction is negative, classical
intensity mixing cannot supply the cancellation by itself, and a
global Gaussian observable law, a finite polynomial correction, an
open-dense nondegeneracy statement, or an exceptional all-orders
conjugacy does not supply the missing comparison. The absolute
sufficient implication remains correct, including averages taken
before the modulus; the simultaneous unaveraged all-order absolute
strengthening is excluded.

\subsection{Outlook}
The next obligations, in order, are: a uniform-in-word-length asymptotic for
the second model, aimed at the pair-scale plateau correction; and
then the higher factorial orders. On the Lines I--N side, the next
obligation is the re-scoped hypothesis H1 (BGS-TRACE-336), the
bad-cluster joint second moment, together with H5--H8. In parallel, the remaining
verification programme is the re-basing of the replay notes that
could not be pinned during the restructure, the continuation of the
formalization (whose current modules cover finite and algebraic kernels and selected Mathlib scalar/reference
limits, with the open target formalized as a statement only), and the
splitting of this compendium into focused articles, each with its
own claim manifest and publication-review requirements. Until those
articles pass external review, the correct description of this volume
is the one its abstract already gives: a continuing research
monograph of exact results and an open frontier, not a proof of
universality.
