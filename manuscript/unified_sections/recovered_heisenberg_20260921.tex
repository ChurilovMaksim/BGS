\section{Recovered Heisenberg-scale structural results}
\label{sec:recovered-heisenberg-20260921}

A forensic reread of the 14--16 September working dossiers recovered several
exact results that had not survived into the consolidated repository. They
are restated only after reconciliation with the corrected raw modular Weyl
convention. None evaluates the complete CUE form factor.

\subsection{Raw-Weyl path escape}
For the frozen operator, raw Weyl multiplication differs from ordinary
midpoint multiplication by a sign, but the commutator magnitude remains
\[
2\left|\sin{\pi\sigma(r,n)\over N}\right|.
\]
For the frozen finite Fourier generator,
\[
 A_1={2\sqrt{101}\over25}+{1307\over200}<8,\qquad R_0=9.
\]
The exact Dyson expansion and the stable/unstable Green function give
\[
 \gamma=3.39318149\ldots,\qquad Z_\gamma=0.14391550\ldots<0.145.
\]
If $\mathcal T^{\rm near}_{N,m}$ is the exact path contribution with every
Weyl state satisfying $\|n_j\|_\infty\le N/32$, then
\[
 |\mathcal T^{\rm near}_{N,m}-1|\le Z_\gamma^m.
\]
With $R=N^\epsilon$, long nonzero near runs contribute
$O(N^6e^{-8N^\epsilon})$. Hence, uniformly for
$m\in[\tau_0N,\tau_1N]$, after deleting an $o(1)$ sub-sum every
surviving nonidentity path has at least
\[
 {\tau_0\over26}N^{1-\epsilon}
\]
bulk visits. This is Heisenberg-time structure, not smallness of the bulk
contribution. For the encounter kernel $B_P=P^TA_mP-A_m$, if $s(P)$ is the
number of nonzero rows, an induced matching gives
\[
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F}B_P\ge s(P)/25
\]
over every field.

\subsection{The long-period subsequence $N=19^k$}
Exact lifting gives
\[
 \operatorname{ord}_{19^k}(S)=20\,19^{k-1}={20\over19}N>N.
\]
Every vector primitive modulo 19 has the full orbit period. For distinct
times below the period,
\[
 \sum_{\xi\in(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^2}
 \left|\sum_{j\in J}a_je_N(\xi\cdot S^jr)\right|^2
 =N^2\sum_{j\in J}|a_j|^2.
\]
A p-adic finite-type analysis together with Cluckers' analytic
non-archimedean van der Corput lemma gives, for primitive $r,\xi$ and
every interval $J$ of length at most $N$,
\[
 \left|\sum_{j\in J}e_N(\xi\cdot S^jr)\right|
 \le C N^{2/3}\log N.
\]
This is deterministic cancellation at Heisenberg length on the stated
subsequence, but it is not yet inserted into every constrained variable of
the full quantum tensor sum.

\subsection{One-shear no-go and the signed frontier}
If the upper-right cat entry is invertible modulo odd $N$, the metaplectic
kernel is diagonal--Fourier--diagonal. Multiplying it by any $q$-only
diagonal kick remains unitarily equivalent to a symmetric unitary and has
an exact antiunitary reversor. The project cat has upper-right entry $4$,
so every one-position-shear fallback is excluded as a CUE route.

The forensic audit also retracted two historical shortcuts. The v9
split-prime two-frequency Bourgain estimate was not supplied by the cited
source, and the phrase ``CUE contraction sector'' had not been accompanied
by an explicit evaluation of that deterministic sub-sum as $\min(m,N)$.
The remaining problem is signed: insert the available bulk cancellation
into the complete path expansion and simultaneously derive the triangular
CUE main term. Absolute suppression of every nonidentity path would be the
wrong target.
