\subsection{From the complete signed hierarchy to a local process}
For a nonnegative band-limited Schwartz function $b$ with $\sup b<1$, let
$M_{k,N}(b)$ be the origin-averaged elementary factorial coefficient of
$b_N(t)=\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb Z}b(N(t+\ell))$, and put
$\Delta_{k,N}=M_{k,N}^U-M_{k,N}^{\rm CUE}$.
TRACE-086 proves the implication
\[
 \Delta_{k,N}(b)\longrightarrow0\quad
 \hbox{for every fixed $k$ and every such $b$}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \Xi_N\Rightarrow\mathrm{Sine}_2.
\]
It also supplies uniform integrability and convergence of every fixed local
count moment. No separate UI premise is needed under this complete
all-order hypothesis. For $h=1-e^{-f}$, $f\ge0$ smooth and compactly supported,
positive band-limited approximants $b$ give the finite certificate
\[
 |L_N^U(f)-L_N^{\rm CUE}(f)|
 \le 2\|h-b\|_1+\sum_{k=2}^{J}|\Delta_{k,N}(b)|
       +\frac{(\int b)^J}{J!},\qquad J\text{ odd}.
\]
First choose the approximation and finite $J$, then take $N$ to infinity.
The actual signed coefficients are not estimated by this implication.

\subsection{Parameter bandwidth and the missing averaged input}
For $U_N(t)=e^{itNA_N}M_N$, $\|A_N\|\le C$, and a fixed real
$w\in C_c^\infty((0,B))$, define
\[
 J_{N,w}(t)=\frac1{N^2}\sum_{m\ge1}w(m/N)
       \bigl(|\operatorname{Tr}U_N(t)^m|^2-\min(m,N)\bigr).
\]
TRACE-087 gives exact angular parameter bandwidth at most $2CBN^2$ and
global bound $O_w(N)$. A reproducing-kernel argument yields, for
$K\Subset I$, $q>1$ and every fixed $A>0$,
\[
 \sup_K|J_{N,w}|
 \le C_{I,K,w,q}N^{2/q}\|J_{N,w}\|_{L^q(I)}
       +O_{I,K,w,A}(N^{1-2A}).
\]
Thus a physical sixth moment $\int_I|J_{N,w}|^6=O(N^{-3})$ would imply
$O(N^{-1/6})$ fixed-coupling decay. It is unproved. The corresponding
Haar-CUE moment is only a reference statement. The exact CUE covariance
(CUE-002), valid for all positive $m,n$, is
\[
 \operatorname{Cov}(|p_m|^2,|p_n|^2)
 =\delta_{mn}\min(m,N)^2+2(N-\max(m,n))_+
 -(N-m-n)_+-(N-|m-n|)_+.

Gaussian trace moment identities are used only in their weighted-degree
stable range.

\subsection{Equal-edge multiplicities and their limited cancellation}
For a rooted closed word let $\nu_{ab}$ be its directed edge counts,
$r_a=\sum_b\nu_{ab}$, and $t(\nu)$ the directed tree cofactor on the
active support. TRACE-088 proves the rooted multiplicity
\[
 c(\nu)=\frac{m\,t(\nu)\prod_{a:r_a>0}(r_a-1)!}
                   {\prod_{a,b}\nu_{ab}!}.
\]
For $P_{ab}=|U_{ab}|^2$, set
$\mathcal B_{N,m}=\sum_\nu c(\nu)^2\prod_{a,b}P_{ab}^{\nu_{ab}}$.
Assume $P_{ab}\le C/N$ and $\|P-\mathbf J\|_{2\to2}\le\rho<1$,
with fixed $C,\rho$ and $\mathbf J_{ab}=1/N$. At $m/N\to\xi>0$,
\[
 \liminf\frac1N\log\mathcal B_{N,m}
 \ge \log2\,[\xi-2+(\xi+2)e^{-\xi}]>0.
\]
Unitarity forces the complementary contribution to equal
$-\mathcal B_{N,m}+O(N^2)$, which does not identify a CUE limit.
At the different scale $m\to\infty$, $m/\sqrt N\to c<\infty$,
TRACE-089 gives
\[
 \frac{\mathcal B_{N,m}}m\longrightarrow
 \frac{\sinh(c^2/2)}{c^2/2},
\]

of triple visits and the classical Harer--Zagier chord identity. Substituting
$c=\sqrt N$ is outside the theorem. The later reported Gaussian comparison

\subsection{Contractive ratios and exact positive Hardy remainders}
Let $U$ be unitary of size $N$, $p(z)=\det(I-zU)$, $0<s<t$,
$a=e^{-s/N}$, $b=e^{-t/N}$, $x=e^{-s}$, $y=e^{-t}$.
TRACE-090 uses the full bounded observable
\[
 \mathcal F_{N;s,t}(\alpha)=e^{-(t-s)}
 \frac{|p(ae^{-2\pi i\alpha})|^2}{|p(be^{-2\pi i\alpha})|^2}
 \in(0,1].
\]
All mixed shifted moments characterize the weak local process, but do not
alone supply its unbounded correlation moments. The CUE mean is exactly
\[
 e^{-(t-s)}\left[1+\frac{(a-b)^2}{1-b^2}
                         \frac{1-a^{2N}}{1-a^2}\right].
\]
The sampled Toeplitz contraction argument requires an angular grid of
at least $N$ points; it is not valid under arbitrary undersampling.

Write $p(z)=\sum c_kz^k$, $q(z)=p(bz)$,
$\mathscr B(z)=z^N\overline{q(1/\bar z)}/q(z)$, and
\[
 u=\frac{1-xy}{1-y^2},\qquad v=\frac{x-y}{1-y^2},\qquad
 E(z)=\frac{p(az)}{p(bz)}-u-c_Nv\mathscr B(z).
\]
TRACE-091 proves the exact orthogonal decomposition
\[
 Z_U(s,t)=e^{-(t-s)}\left[1+\frac{(x-y)^2}{1-y^2}
                                      +\|E\|_{H^2}^2\right].
\]
The minimum is attained exactly by a rotated regular $N$-clock. All roots
of $p$ are required to lie on the unit circle; self-inversiveness alone
does not suffice. For $r=e^{-s/N}$, $q_r=p(rz)$ and
$\mathscr B_r=q_r^*/q_r$, put
\[
 Y(z)=\frac zN\frac{q_r'(z)}{q_r(z)}
       +\frac{e^{-2s}}{1-e^{-2s}}
       -\frac{c_Ne^{-s}}{1-e^{-2s}}\mathscr B_r(z).
\]
Then the complete Abel energy satisfies
\[
 L_N(s)=\frac1{N^2}\sum_{m\ge1}e^{-2sm/N}
                  |\operatorname{Tr}U^m|^2
       =\frac1{e^{2s}-1}+\|Y\|_{H^2}^2.
\]
The required CUE excess is positive:
\[
 \|Y\|_{H^2}^2\ \longrightarrow\
 \frac{1-e^{-2s}}{4s^2}-\frac1{e^{2s}-1}.
\]
This limit is unproved for the frozen family. Deleted-clock spectra have
strictly positive finite excess of order $N^{-1}$ and bounded fixed traces,
so neither observation determines the desired full Hardy norm.
