\begin{theorem}[Uniform Pell-orbit overlap, BGS-TRACE-125]
\label{thm:uniform-orbit-overlap}
Let \(n\ge5\) be an odd integer and let \(N=u_n\). The overlap \(\gamma(r)\) of
the fixed \(32\)-mode symbol under \(C\) vanishes for \(1\le r\le n-1\) except
at \(r=1\) and \(r=n-1\), where
\[
\gamma(1)=\gamma(n-1)=-\frac7{2000000}.
\]
The quadratic trace coefficient of the transported product is
\[
\frac{\operatorname{Tr}(S^2)}{N}=n\cdot\frac{1650797}{4000000}.
\]
This is \(f''(0)/(-\theta^2 N)\) for
\(f(t)=\operatorname{Tr}\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}\exp(ti\theta A_j)\).

The index \(n=5\) is a finite scan of the middle lags modulo \(41\). Every
odd \(n\ge7\) is the size, archimedean, and quadratic-form argument. The
identity does not bound the third-order remainder, does not give
\(L_N(U_N;1)>3/4\) at any new dimension, and does not estimate the signed
off-diagonal sum at times of order \(N\). The pure cat map is already away
from the CUE Abel value by BGS-TRACE-117. BGS-TRACE-005 and BGS-BGS-001
remain open.
\end{theorem}
