\paragraph{A distinct ordered-diagonal companion.}
Removing full Haar invariants from the entire ordered diagonal algebra gives
an orthogonal projector $P_{\rm ord}$ of rank $N^m-1$. Only full traceless
slot support survives its cyclic trace. An exact matrix-product Neumann
identity and the independently reconstructed Haar tail show, for each fixed
$a>0$, uniformly over integers $m\ge aN$ and sufficiently large odd $N$,
\[
 |\operatorname{Tr}(R_mP_{\rm ord}C_U)|
 \le12N^3e^{-c_aN},\qquad
 c_a=\min\{a\log2,-a\log\sqrt{159/160},1/160\}>0.
\]
This bound is exponential in $N$, not in $m$ at fixed $N$; the exact
$N=2$ even-degree control has a $-1/m$ tail. Different cycle cancellations
mean this theorem does not imply the collective bound above.
The exact residual $Q_{\rm ord}=I-P_H-P_{\rm ord}$ still contains remaining
balanced and off-charge ranges. No bound on that full signed trace or BGS
conclusion follows. Only the inherited actual FM-021 singular gap is used.
