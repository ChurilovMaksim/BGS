\section{Short-time limits of the diagonal-phase form factor}
\label{sec:diagonal-phase-short-time}
The phases below are independent at the \(N\) sites of the position basis.
They are not the sixteen mode-pair phases, and the propagator is not the fixed kick.
The canonical proof is
\path{mathematics/fixed-map/diagonal_phase_short_time.md}.
Integer comparisons are certified by

Python and C++ check the occupation sums in floating point. No external refereeing is asserted.

\begin{theorem}[Short-time diagonal-phase limits, BGS-TRACE-128]
\label{thm:diagonal-phase-short-time}
Let \(N\ge 3\) be odd, let \(M_N\) be the symmetric cat-map quantization, and let
\(D\) be diagonal with independent uniform phases. For \(U_N=DM_N\),
\(\mathbb E\lvert\operatorname{Tr} U_N\rvert^2=1\) and
\(\mathbb E\lvert\operatorname{Tr} U_N^2\rvert^2=(2N-1)/N\). For every integer
\(m\ge 3\), with \(P(N,m)\) the falling factorial,
\[
\Bigl\lvert\mathbb E\lvert\operatorname{Tr} U_N^m\rvert^2
-2m\frac{P(N,m)}{N^m}\Bigr\rvert
\le\frac{m!(1+m(m-1))}{N}.
\]
The limit at fixed \(m\ge 3\) is \(2m\). The same bound reaches every sequence
\(m\le c\log N/\log\log N\) with a fixed \(c<1\), and it does not reach
\(m\le N^{\alpha}\).

Let \(p\) be an odd prime and let \(W_p\) be \(M_p\) with rows permuted by
\(x\mapsto x^{-1}\), with \(0\) fixed. At \(p=3\) this permutation is the
identity. For \(V_p=DW_p\) and every \(m\ge 3\),
\[
\Bigl\lvert\mathbb E\lvert\operatorname{Tr} V_p^m\rvert^2
-m\frac{P(p,m)}{p^m}\Bigr\rvert
\le m!\Bigl(3^{1/4}p^{-1/4}+\frac{m(m-1)+2}{p}\Bigr).
\]
The limit at fixed \(m\) is \(m\). The logarithmic window has \(c<1/4\) and
does not reach \(m\le p^{\alpha}\). For \(p\ge 5\) the matrix \(W_p\) is not
symmetric. A second independent diagonal does not break the reversal:
\(D_1M_ND_2\) is similar to a single diagonal times \(M_N\).

The limit \(2m\) is the short-time orthogonal value and the limit \(m\) is the
short-time unitary value. Neither ensemble is Haar measure on the
corresponding circular ensemble, neither is the sixteen-phase kick, and
neither is the fixed propagator. The constants are not sharp. No
Heisenberg-scale statement is proved. The verifiers include \(N=3,5,7\). At
time four the symmetric value is about \(7.527892\) at \(N=49\), against a
main term about \(7.056652\), toward \(8\).
\end{theorem}
