\section{Chinese product for the cat-map trace square}
\label{sec:crt-composite-abel}
The trace square of the pure cat map multiplies over the prime-power
factors of every odd modulus. Every product of two odd primes then lies
strictly above \(3/4\) or strictly below \(5/7\). The canonical proof is
\path{mathematics/fixed-map/crt_composite_abel.md}.
The integer comparisons are certified by
\path{formalization/Bgs/CrtCompositeRational.lean} and recomputed by the
Python and C++ verifiers named there. No external refereeing is asserted.
BGS-TRACE-005 and BGS-BGS-001 remain open.

\begin{theorem}[Chinese product and the semiprime dichotomy, BGS-TRACE-122]
\label{thm:crt-composite-abel}
Let \(N=\prod_ip_i^{k_i}\) be odd. For every integer \(m\ge0\),
\[
\lvert\operatorname{Tr}M_N^m\rvert^2
=\prod_i\lvert\operatorname{Tr}M_{p_i^{k_i}}^m\rvert^2,
\]
and the order \(t(N)\) is the least common multiple of the orders
\(t(p_i^{k_i})\). If \(N\) is square-free, \(L_N(M_N;1)\) is the sum,
over subsets of the prime factors, of a positive coefficient times the
geometric series at the least common multiple of the corresponding orders.

Let \(N=pq\) with odd primes \(p<q\) and let \(T=t(N)\). If \(12T\le5N\),
or if \(7\) divides \(N\) and the product is not short, or if \(p=3\) and
\(q\) is a short prime whose product is not short, then
\(L_N(M_N;1)>3/4\), so the distance to \((1-e^{-2})/4\) is greater than
\(1/2\). Every remaining semiprime satisfies \(L_N(M_N;1)<5/7\), so that
distance is less than \(1/2\).

For the fixed kick \(\tau=10^{-80}\) and every such \(N\le10^{26}\), the
gap above \(3/4\) survives when \(N=21\), when \(N=7q\) with
\(q\equiv1\) or \(7\pmod8\), \(t(q)=q-1\) and \(3\nmid(q-1)\), and when
\(N=3q\) is a non-short product of \(3\) with a short prime of odd order.
No such short prime is asserted to exist. The kick is not transferred on
the side below \(5/7\), nor on the inert full-order multiples of \(7\).
The modulus \(N=195\) is not short, and no bound is claimed for three or
more prime factors. No density is proved. The kick at the \(79\)-digit
dimension \(u_{207}\), the signed off-diagonal sum, the limit, and every
\(k>2\) stay open.
\end{theorem}
