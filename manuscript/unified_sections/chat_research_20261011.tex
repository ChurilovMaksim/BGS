\subsection{Exact Haar feedback and entropy-stable clocks (TRACE-076)}
\label{sec:haar-feedback-20261012}
Take an initial Haar unitary $U_N$ independent of the fresh feedback
phases and uniform coordinate order, with $F_N(z)=zU_N$ and initial
incoming and outgoing delays equal to the identity. Retain a
predetermined set of $u$ coordinates and put $q=N-u$. Absorb any fixed
feedback phases in the eliminated rows:
$\widehat U=\operatorname{diag}(I_u,W_E)U_N$. Conditional on the independent
phases and order, this matrix is still Haar. Define
\[
 X(z)=(I_q-z\widehat U_{EE})^{-1}z\widehat U_{ER},\qquad
 F_u(z)=z\widehat U_{RR}+z\widehat U_{RE}X(z).
\]
Sequential and block feedback solve the same linear system in the
unit disk and agree at regular boundary points. All required inverses
exist almost surely at $z=1$: a fixed vector of the eliminated principal
block would, by unitarity and the norm identity, extend to a fixed
vector of $\widehat U$, an event of Haar measure zero.

Set $S=F_u(1)$, $T=S^*F_u'(1)$ and $Q=STS^*$.
The exact joint law is
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 S&\sim\mathsf H_u,\qquad S\ \hbox{independent of }T,\qquad
 T=I_u+X^*X,\\
 d\mathbb P(X)&\propto\det(I_u+X^*X)^{-(N+u)}\,dX,\qquad
 X\in\mathbb C^{q\times u}.
 \end{aligned}                                           \tag{HF.1}
\]
The $X$ law is invariant under right unitaries, so $Q$ has the same law
as $T$ and is separately independent of $S$. The pair $(T,Q)$ need not
be independent of $S$, and no independence across sizes is asserted.
At $q=0$, $T=Q=I_u$.

\paragraph{Derivative and complete block factorization.}
On $z=e^{i\theta}$, the unitary colligation identity is
\[
 z\widehat U\begin{pmatrix}I_u\\X(z)\end{pmatrix}
       =\begin{pmatrix}F_u(z)\\X(z)\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Differentiate in $\theta$ and left multiply by $(F_u(z)^*,X(z)^*)$.
The terms $X(z)^*\dot X(z)$ cancel, giving
$-iF_u(z)^*\dot F_u(z)=I_u+X(z)^*X(z)$. At $\theta=0$ this is the
delay identity in (HF.1).

For the distribution, use the Hermitian Cayley variable
\[
 C=i(I+\widehat U)(I-\widehat U)^{-1}
      =\begin{pmatrix}A&B^*\\B&D\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 \widehat U=(C-iI)(C+iI)^{-1}.
\]
The differential
$-i\widehat U^*d\widehat U=2(C-iI)^{-1}dC(C+iI)^{-1}$ gives Haar
density proportional to $\det(I+C^2)^{-N}dC$. Here Hermitian
congruence in dimension $n$ by $L$ has real Jacobian
$|\det L|^{2n}$: singular values $s_j$ scale the real diagonal
coordinates by $s_j^2$ and each complex off-diagonal coordinate by
$s_js_k$, whose real two-dimensional contribution is $s_j^2s_k^2$.

The block colligation gives $S=(A-iI)(A+iI)^{-1}$ and
$X=B(A+iI)^{-1}$. With $G=I_q+XX^*$ make the change
\[
 B=X(A+iI),\qquad D=XAX^*+G^{1/2}YG^{1/2},\qquad Y=Y^* .
\]
Its real Jacobian is $\det(I_u+A^2)^q\det(G)^q$.
The full derivative is block triangular in the order $(A,X,Y)$;
the derivatives of the square root contribute no extra factor.
Since
$B(I+iA)^{-1}B^*=X(I-iA)X^*$, the Schur determinant gives
\[
 \det(I+iC)=\det(I+iA)\det G\det(I+iY).
\]
Consequently the transformed density is proportional to
\[
 \det(I_u+A^2)^{-u}\,
 \det(G)^{-(N+u)}\,
 \det(I_q+Y^2)^{-N}\,dA\,dX\,dY.                     \tag{HF.2}
\]
The original mass is finite and positive; Tonelli therefore makes
each independent factor finite and positive. The $A$ factor is the
Cayley image of Haar in dimension $u$, proving (HF.1).
Haar-compatible Schur projections and Cauchy ensembles are prior
objects; see \cite{BNN2011Haar}. Rectangular matrix-beta integrals and
their singular-value reductions are prior as well
\cite{Neretin2015Beta}. The displayed factorization and its delay
coupling are derived here; no world-priority claim is made.

\paragraph{Inverse-Jacobi spectrum, atom and moments.}

 X\stackrel{d}{=}G_0W^{-1/2}.
\]

\]
This is a spectral identity, not an equality of differently ordered

The power of $y_\ell$ is $N+u-|q-u|-2r=u$.

The necessity and sufficiency follow from the density near $y=0$;

$b=P_{ie}P_{ek}$, $d=P_{ke}P_{ej}$ and $r_e=P_{ie}P_{ej}$.
With all following sums over $k\ne e$, define
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 A_e&=\sum(y\alpha+x\gamma),&
 B_e&=\sum(xd+yb+2\operatorname{Re}(\alpha\bar\gamma)),\\

 D_e&=\sum(\alpha d+\gamma b),\qquad F_e=\sum bd.
 \end{aligned}
\]
The exact real increment, $q_e=w_e/(1-S_{ee}w_e)$, is
\[
 \Delta_e=-r_e+2\operatorname{Re}(q_eA_e)+|q_e|^2B_e

\]

 \sum|A_e|^2&\le10\delta^2K_{ij},\\
 \sum B_e^2&\le6\delta^2(P^3)_{ij}+12\delta^2K_{ij},&

 \sum|D_e|^2&\le4\delta^3(P^3)_{ij},&

 \end{aligned}                                                   \tag{SV.3}
\]
