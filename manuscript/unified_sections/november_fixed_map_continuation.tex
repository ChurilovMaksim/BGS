\section{November continuation at the fixed map}
\label{sec:november-fixed-map-continuation}

The continuation uses the original \(U_N=\exp(i\tau N A_N)M_N\) with
\(\tau=10^{-80}\) unless otherwise stated. All comparisons below use the
same mean-spacing unfolding. Exact finite witnesses, conditional
implications, and shrinking-coupling results are kept separate.

\paragraph{The pair problem after the alignment reduction.}
For a fixed \(d>1/2\), consider the signed sum over pairs of closed paths
having at least \(dm\) coordinate matches in some direct or reversed
cyclic alignment, counting each pair once. The proposed control
(BGS-TRACE-356, verification pending) removes intersections by folding
short relative orders into a bounded number of threads and by bounding
long relative orders by a count of independent labels. Above half
overlap, a multiple intersection enforces a positive density of triple
contacts. The estimates depend on the contact chapter's fixed-power
semiclassical comparison and summable compression bound. Taking them
as inputs, the signed sum equals \(m+O_d(e^{-c_d m})\) for
\(m\ge\lceil\log N\rceil\), and the noncyclic part of that class has
exponentially small signed mass. This is not a statement about the
complete boundary \(d=1/2\).

Write \(R_{m,N}(d)\) for the sum over the excluded class, which reaches
the threshold at no alignment. For the fixed band-two pair weight,
the remaining task on this route is
\[
  N^{-2}\sum_{\lceil\log N\rceil\le m<2N}w(m/N)R_{m,N}(d)
  \longrightarrow -\int_1^2(t-1)w(t)\,dt .
\]
The right-hand side is strictly negative. Neither pointwise unsigned
estimates nor exact short-history orthogonality evaluate this sum.

\paragraph{Finite and arithmetic controls.}
The finite band-two comparison at \(h_{207}\) has a strengthened lower
bound \(0.024484\) (BGS-TRACE-362). A different finite certificate at
\(h_{209}\) gives a normalized return trace larger than
\(1.8\cdot10^{-5}\) at \(m=209\) and a discrepancy from Sine\(_2\) for a
bounded local test (BGS-TRACE-363, verification pending). It is not
a band-two witness at that dimension and cannot supply an infinite
positive lower bound on the same Pell return: BGS-TRACE-113 bounds
those normalized traces by a quantity tending to zero.

The scoped arithmetic notes treat single-step transfer between
torsion sectors, simple phases and independent phase monomials on
\(7^k\), and an effective but extremely small lower bound on nonzero
gaps (BGS-TRACE-358--361, verification pending). An exact nonresonance
or a fixed-dimension long-time average does not control close
resonances for \(m\asymp N\).

\paragraph{Transfer and weak versus moment limits.}
For the specified positive autocorrelation weight, \(o(1/N)\) in
operator norm transfers the pair test in both directions; \(o(1)\) in
trace norm transfers a weak local law. At \(O(1/N)\), auxiliary
determinant-preserving rounded spectra can retain prescribed short
traces and have a different weak law (BGS-TRACE-364). This is a
limitation of the approximation, not a counterexample for \(U_N\).
Weak Sine\(_2\) convergence forces a lower bound for every nonnegative
pair test by truncation; a persistent negative defect would therefore
refute weak convergence without assuming convergence of second
moments. Weak convergence plus the specified pair test implies the
other pair tests, while weak convergence alone does not (BGS-TRACE-365).
