\begin{theorem}[Fixed-time limits, BGS-TRACE-132]
\label{thm:hyperbolic-short-time-limit}
Let \(S\in\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb Z)\) be hyperbolic and let \(N\) be odd and coprime
to the upper-right entry. Write \(P(N,m)=N(N-1)\cdots(N-m+1)\) for \(m\le N\),
and \(P(N,m)=0\) for \(m>N\). For the Haar diagonal ensemble \(U=DM_N(S)\) and
every \(m\ge 3\),
\[
\bigl\lvert\mathbb E\lvert\operatorname{Tr} U^m\rvert^2-2m P(N,m)/N^m\bigr\rvert
\le m!(1+m(m-1))/N.
\]
The fixed-time limit is \(2m\). The antiunitary \(\Theta'=DEK_0\) of
BGS-TRACE-131 reverses \(DM_N\) for every such \(S\). At times one and two the
limits are \(1\) and \(2\). A finite cosine in the window
\(\eta=o(N^{-2/3})\), along odd \(N\) with \(\gcd(N,\beta(2-u_m))=1\), has
limit \(1\), not \(2m\). The same conclusion fails when that gcd exceeds \(1\):
the model kernel at time three has order \(49\) for \(N=35,77,91,707\). If the
frequencies \(\{1,\ldots,m\}\) are present, sending \(\eta\to\infty\) before
\(N\to\infty\) recovers \(2m\). A second diagonal factor does not replace \(2m\)
by \(m\). Row inversion on an odd prime not dividing the upper-right entry has
limit \(m\). A momentum sine takes the propagator out of this class, and its
form factor is not identified. The orthogonal-class name remains a heuristic
for \(\Theta'\). The factor \(2m\) is the count of Tanner and of
Pako\'nski--\.Zyczkowski--Ku\'s; the extension to a general hyperbolic \(S\)
is the error term above.
\end{theorem}
