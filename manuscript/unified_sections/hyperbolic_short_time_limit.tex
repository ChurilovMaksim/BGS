\begin{theorem}[Fixed-time limits, BGS-TRACE-132]
\label{thm:hyperbolic-short-time-limit}
Let \(S\in\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb Z)\) be hyperbolic and let \(N\) be odd and coprime
to the upper-right entry. For the Haar diagonal ensemble \(U=DM_N(S)\) and every
\(m\ge 3\),
\[
\bigl\lvert\mathbb E\lvert\operatorname{Tr} U^m\rvert^2-2m P(N,m)/N^m\bigr\rvert
\le m!(1+m(m-1))/N.
\]
The fixed-time limit is \(2m\). It does not use equality of the diagonal entries
of \(S\). At times one and two the limits are \(1\) and \(2\). A finite cosine
kick in the window \(\eta=o(N^{-2/3})\) has limit \(1\), not \(2m\). If the
frequencies \(\{1,\ldots,m\}\) are present, sending \(\eta\to\infty\) before
\(N\to\infty\) recovers \(2m\). A second diagonal factor does not replace \(2m\)
by \(m\). Row inversion on an odd prime not dividing the upper-right entry has
limit \(m\). A momentum sine takes the propagator out of this class, and its
form factor is not identified. The orthogonal-class name remains a heuristic.
\end{theorem}
