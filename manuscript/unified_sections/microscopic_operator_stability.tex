\section{Operator resolution and the short-trace information barrier}
\label{sec:microscopic-operator-stability}
The global trace estimate of TRACE-113 has a useful microscopic consequence:
a sufficiently accurate matrix approximation preserves the prescribed pair
trace test. This is a transfer theorem, not a determination of the test's limit.
The complementary construction below shows why much coarser quantum information
and polynomially many short traces cannot determine a local law.
The proofs have author self-review; no external refereeing or formal certification
is asserted. Canonical proofs are in
\path{mathematics/trace-bridge/microscopic_unitary_stability_20261027.md}
and \path{mathematics/fixed-map/balanced_rounding_research_20261027.md}.

For unitary $U$ of dimension $N$, define
\[
 R_U(s)=1+\frac{2}{N^2}\sum_{m\ge1}e^{-sm/N}|\operatorname{Tr}U^m|^2,
 \qquad T_U(w)=\frac1{N^2}\sum_{m\ge1}w(m/N)|\operatorname{Tr}U^m|^2.
\]
The circle point process is unfolded by $N$ and rooted with one uniform common
shift. Its Laplace functional is denoted $\mathscr L_U(f)$.

\begin{theorem}[Microscopic unitary stability, BGS-TRACE-115]
\label{thm:microscopic-unitary-stability}
If $U,V$ are unitary, $\epsilon=\|V-U\|<2$, and
$\eta=2\arcsin(\epsilon/2)$, then for nonnegative $f\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)$,
\[
 |\mathscr L_V(f)-\mathscr L_U(f)|\le\frac{N\eta}{2\pi}\|f'\|_1,
 \qquad e^{-2\eta/\sinh(s/N)}\le\frac{R_V(s)}{R_U(s)}
 \le e^{2\eta/\sinh(s/N)}.
\]
For real even $w\in C_c^2(\mathbb R)$, set
$C_w=4\pi\|tw\|_1+\pi^{-1}\|(tw)''\|_1$. Then
\[
 |T_V(w)-T_U(w)|\le\frac{C_wN\eta}{2}
 e^{2\eta/\sinh(2\pi/N)}R_U(2\pi).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $V=e^{iH}U$, $\|H\|\le\eta$. Differentiating along $e^{itH}U$
and averaging the periodized $f$ over the common shift proves the first bound.
On Hilbert--Schmidt matrices the generator is $\operatorname{ad}_H$, of norm
at most $2\eta$. For the Poisson kernel $P_r$,
$|P_r'|\le P_r/\sinh(s/N)$ when $r=e^{-s/N}$; its square certificate is
\[
 (1+r^2-2rc)^2-(1-r^2)^2(1-c^2)=[2r-(1+r^2)c]^2.
\]
Trace differentiation gives the relative bound. The inverse Fourier transform
$\phi$ of $w$ obeys $|\phi'(x)|\le C_w/(1+x^2)$. Periodizing it gives a
Poisson majorant for the differentiated trace weight, hence the final bound.
All arguments allow repeated eigenvalues.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original-family pair transfer, BGS-TRACE-116]
\label{thm:original-pair-transfer}
For the unchanged fixed-$\tau$ family along all sufficiently large odd $N$,
if $V_N$ is unitary and $\|V_N-U_N\|\le K/N^2$ with fixed $K$, then
$|T_{V_N}(w)-T_{U_N}(w)|=O_{w,K}(1/\log N)$.
For arbitrary matrices $B_N$ with that norm error, the finite trace polynomials
instead differ by $O_{w,K}(1/\sqrt{\log N}+1/N)$.
No phase point process is assigned to a nonunitary $B_N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
TRACE-113 supplies $|\operatorname{Tr}U_N^m|\le Ca^m$ with one threshold
for all $m$. If $|\operatorname{Tr}U^m|^2/N^2\le\delta$ for $1\le m<L$,
expanding a geometric block in the Poisson kernel gives
\[
 R_U(s)\le(1-\delta)\coth\frac{sL}{2N}+\delta\coth\frac{s}{2N}.
\]
Take $L=\lfloor\log N/(2\log a)\rfloor$ and $\delta=C^2/N$ eventually.
Thus $R_{U_N}(s)=O_s(N/\log N)$, and the preceding theorem proves the
unitary assertion. For $B_N$, telescoping powers gives
$|\operatorname{Tr}(B_N^m-U_N^m)|\le Nm\epsilon_N e^{RN\epsilon_N}$
on the support of $w(m/N)$. Its weighted square sum is
$O_w(\epsilon_N^2N^3e^{2RN\epsilon_N})$.
Cauchy--Schwarz with $T_{U_N}(|w|)=O_w(N/\log N)$ proves the second bound.
The inherited TRACE-113 constants remain noneffective.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Short-trace barrier, BGS-TRACE-114]
\label{thm:balanced-spectral-rounding}
Let $U_N$ be any unitaries and $0<\gamma<1/2$, $\gamma+1/2<\alpha<1$.
There are unitaries $V_N$ sharing their eigenbasis, with
$\|V_N-U_N\|=O(N^{-\alpha})$ and
\[
 \max_{1\le m\le\lfloor N^\gamma\rfloor}
 |\operatorname{Tr}V_N^m-\operatorname{Tr}U_N^m|=o(1),
\]
whose uniformly rooted microscopic processes converge to the empty process.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Round each eigenphase between its two neighboring points of a grid of size
$K=\lceil N^\alpha\rceil$, spacing $h=2\pi/K$.
Linear interpolation has moment error at most $Nm^2h^2/2$.
A real kernel direction in the $2M$ moment constraints, $M=\lfloor N^\gamma\rfloor$,
moves all but at most $2M$ interpolation weights to endpoints without changing
the interpolated moments. Rounding the remainder adds at most $Mmh$.
The uniform bound is $NM^2h^2/2+M^2h=o(1)$.
An optional phase split of total size $N^{-4}$ adds at most $MN^{-4}$.
All eigenphases lie in $K$ intervals of that width; a fixed unfolded window
meets them with probability $O(K/N+KN^{-4})=o(1)$.
\end{proof}
This auxiliary family changes the exact quantum kick. Compared with TRACE-025
it preserves stronger absolute short-trace data at a coarser operator scale;
it does not preserve all of that theorem's charge estimates.
The original signed Heisenberg pair limit and higher factorial hierarchy remain
open. Weak process convergence alone does not imply convergence of unbounded
pair moments.
