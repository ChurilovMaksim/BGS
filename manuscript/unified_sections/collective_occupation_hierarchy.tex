\section{The centered collective occupation hierarchy}
Work in the full ordered $\operatorname{End}((\mathbb C^N)^{\otimes m})$
with ordinary Hilbert--Schmidt metric and the two-sided slot cycle.
For traceless diagonal coefficients of degree $q$, full Haar removal has
Gram matrix $G_{q,N}=I-H_{q,N}$, where
$H_{q,N}=\mathbb E_{V\text{ Haar}}\operatorname{Sym}^q(|V|^2-J)$.
The exact collective trace is
\[
 S_{m,N}(U)=\sum_{q=1}^m
 \operatorname{Tr}\!\left[G_{q,N}^{-1}
 \bigl(\operatorname{Sym}^q(T_U)-H_{q,N}\bigr)\right],
 \qquad T_U=(|U|^2-J)|_{\mathbf1^\perp}.
\]
An all-degree Gram bound and a finite $q\le bN$ Haar-tail argument,
together with the explicitly inherited actual FM-021 gap and flatness
estimates, give $S_{m,N}(U_N)=O_b(1)$ through fixed positive Heisenberg bands.
The original $N^{-2}$ compact-time aggregate is therefore $O_w(N^{-1})$.
The $N=2$ unbounded-degree failure control prevents replacing this finite
band theorem by an infinite-degree determinant expectation.

With $P_H$ the full invariant projector and $P_{\rm coll}$ the centered
collective projector, the exact remaining space is
\[
 Q=I-P_H-P_{\rm coll}
   =(\Pi-P_H-P_{\rm coll})+(I-\Pi).
\]
The full pair trace is $\min(m,N)+S_{m,N}(U_N)+\operatorname{Tr}(R_mQC_U)$.
Some vectors in $Q$ are cycle-fixed. This is not a decomposition into only
nontrivial permutation sectors, nor a bound on the whole balanced trace.
The separately reviewed quadratic and slow-source examples retain their
single-sector or single-vector scopes. No identification with WKB stationary
masks, complete signed residual estimate, plateau mechanism or BGS follows.
