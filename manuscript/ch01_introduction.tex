\section*{Introduction and guide}
\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Introduction and guide}
The Bohigas--Giannoni--Schmit conjecture, in the unitary-class form
studied here, concerns the local statistics of desymmetrized quantum
chaotic spectra and their comparison with the $\mathrm{Sine}_2$
process. This programme studies that question for fixed arithmetic
quantum maps, where exact finite-dimensional computations are available
but Heisenberg-scale control is not.

The terminal open obligation is the signed Heisenberg-scale
desymmetrized joint-trace estimate for the fixed map (BGS-TRACE-005).
Everything else in this volume is either an exact reduction toward it,
a conditional bridge, an obstruction, or a result in a separate regime.
Neither BGS for the fixed map (BGS-BGS-001) nor BGS-TRACE-005 is proved.

\paragraph{How the work developed.} The programme ran in seven phases
(September to November 2026); the claim registry
\path{record/claims.json} is the status authority for all of them.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Phase I (September):} pair-key and Cayley-energy reductions,
Pell and Floquet return laws, resonance cohomology and fixed-strength
locality (Chapters~2--11).
\item \emph{Phase II (20 September--7 October):} the structure of the
frozen map, the exact trace-to-statistics bridge, intensity mixing,
path and sector reductions, and noisy Dyson comparisons (Chapter~12 and
the consolidation addendum).
\item \emph{Phase III (8--19 October):} phase channels, clock and
collision laws and spectral obstructions; several of these results are
verification-pending.
\item \emph{Phase IV (21--27 October):} a uniform power-trace bound for the
original kick family and operator-stability transfer (Chapter~14).
\item \emph{Phase V (28--29 October):} kick-blind arithmetic spikes
(Section~\ref{sec:kick-blind-arithmetic-spike}) and exact random-phase
form factors (Chapter~14).
\item \emph{Phase VI (29--30 October), Lines B--H:} random-phase pairs,
band-limited kicks and the fixed diagonal kick
(Sections~\ref{sec:random-phase-encounter}--\ref{sec:lineH-49state});
Conjecture~R is refuted at $\eta=2$ by a computer-assisted certificate.
\item \emph{Phase VII (31 October--1 November), Lines I--N:} the corrected
mean law $R'$ along $m=3p$ (Section~\ref{sec:lines-I-N}); $R'$ is open and
not refuted, and Theorem~R$^\sharp$-mod~v5 is conditional.
\end{itemize}
Chapter~15 summarises what is proved, conditional and open, and
Appendix~A lists the registered claims.
