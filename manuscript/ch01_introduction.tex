\section*{Introduction and guide}
\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Introduction and guide}
% DRAFT: field overview (one paragraph; to be expanded by the author):
The Bohigas--Giannoni--Schmit conjecture, in the unitary-class form
studied here, concerns the local statistics of desymmetrized quantum
chaotic spectra and their comparison with the $\mathrm{Sine}_2$
process. This programme studies that question for fixed arithmetic
quantum maps, where exact finite-dimensional computations are available
but Heisenberg-scale control is not.
