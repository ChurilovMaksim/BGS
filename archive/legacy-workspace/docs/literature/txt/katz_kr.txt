Summary We prove equidistribution results for certain exponential sums that arise in the work of Kurlberg-Rudnick on "cat maps". We show (Theorems 1 and 2) that suitable normalizations of these sums behave like the traces of random matrices in SU(2). We also show that as a suitable parameter varies, the corresponding sums are statistically independent (Theorems 3 and 4). The main tools are Deligne's Equidistribution Theorem, the Feit-Thompson Theorem, the GoursatKolchin-Ribet Theorem, and Laumon's Theorem of Stationary Phase.
