# Literature scope, checked 18 October 2026

No priority claim is made. New proofs use character orthogonality, Gaussian
polynomial norms, finite-field rank, principal-minor completion and Pfaffian
expansion. Search is a contextual check, not proof of novelty or completeness.

1. G. Tanner, *Unitary stochastic matrix ensembles and spectral statistics*,
   arXiv:nlin/0104014v1, https://arxiv.org/pdf/nlin/0104014.
   Read §II.B, §III.A and conjecture (20) in §III.B. The gap criterion is
   proposed as a conjecture. Its diagonal approximation supplies no uniform
   growing-degree remainder theorem needed here. No theorem is imported.
2. G. Cipolloni, L. Erdos, D. Schroder, *On the Spectral Form Factor for Random
   Matrices*, arXiv:2109.06712v2, 7 March 2023,
   https://arxiv.org/html/2109.06712v2. Read §2 and its Wigner and
   monoparametric ensemble hypotheses. The Hermitian Wigner data, including
   fixed typical independent Wigner pairs in the latter model, do not give
   a theorem for a random diagonal times this deterministic Fourier matrix.
   A transfer requires additional proof, absent here.

Searches for random-phase Fourier traces, characteristic polynomials of
unitary-stochastic ensembles and symplectic Fourier random trace limits
did not establish the literature status of the exact present statements.
Unsuccessful search is not evidence of a first result.
