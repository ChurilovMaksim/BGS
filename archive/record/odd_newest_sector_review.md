## 1. Raw cocycles and the packet construction

Use omega(u,v)=u_a v_b-u_b v_a. Direct multiplication gives

    T_n(u) T_n(v) = q^(omega(u,v)/2) T_n(u+v).

The prescribed quadratic kernel gives M_n T_n(y)=T_n(Sy) M_n, with S=(3,4;2,3), rather than S inverse. Substituting its exponents verifies this identity before any reduction. The independent raw-entry test checks every x,y at n=5 and n=125 for all u in {0,...,4}².

Let J=J_l:H5 -> H_n. The accepted exact formulas are M_n J=J M5 and J* T_n(v)J=0 unless h divides both coordinates of v, in which case the compression is T5(v/h). Hence E_y=T_n(y)JH5, y modulo h, are mutually orthogonal dimension-five spaces whose dimensions sum to n.

If v=r+hd with r the chosen representative modulo h, then

    T_n(v)J = q^(-omega(r,v)/2) T_n(r) J T5(d).

Thus changes of representative introduce exactly a five-power scalar and a fiber Weyl operator. There is no suppressed real normalization. Direct exponent controls independently check 393,750 such carried entries at h=5,25. The raw oldform intertwining is also checked by exact cyclotomic exponent sums in 16,250 entries at those h.

Nesting gives J1 J_(l-1)=J_l. Applying J1 to the lower packet indexed by z gives the ambient packet indexed by 5z. Therefore W1 is precisely the span of packets whose two labels are divisible by five. This includes l=1, where it is the single packet E_0 tensor H7. ## 2. Primitive circles, holonomy, and all character conductors
