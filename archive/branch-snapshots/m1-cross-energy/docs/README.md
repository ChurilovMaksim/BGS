# Documentation map

The documentation tree intentionally keeps current planning together with the full research record.  Root `STATUS.md` always wins when historical documents disagree about whether something is proved.

## Current planning and proof boundary

- root `STATUS.md`, `THEORY.md`, `HISTORY.md`, `HANDBOOK.md` — canonical present-tense state;
- `programme_status.md` — short programme summary subordinate to `STATUS.md`;
- `research_goals.md`, `roadmap.md` — planning material; old estimates/status words are not theorem certificates;
- root `research/README.md`, `research/M1.md` — current non-error continuation.

## Research corpus

- `literature/` — literature notes and source reconnaissance;
- `review/` — referee/red-team/model-review material and prior repository audits;
- `ideas.md` — incubator notebook; its historical probabilities and `PROVEN` wording are snapshots only;
- `cycle2_falsifier.md` — additive toy calculation preserved as exploratory evidence, not a certificate for multiplicative H3;
- `trace_fluctuation_lemma.md` — **invalid/superseded proof route as stated** because its Step 1 uses a scalar binomial expansion without a commutation theorem;
- `sp4_project_scope.md` — rank-2/Sp4 scope notes;
- `REVIEW_FINDINGS.md` — later review findings; consult before the superseded/refined `REVIEW_ERRORS.md`.

## Detailed historical record

- `THEORY_FULL_PRE_REBUILD.md` — detailed pre-rebuild theory notebook with valuable derivations and historical status labels;
- `HISTORY_FULL_PRE_REBUILD.md` — surfaced full old ledger with known legacy mojibake preserved; root `HISTORY.md` is the readable current chronology.  The verified repair method/checksums are recorded in the audit directory;
- `archive/` — old handoffs, ledger entries, snapshots, scripts, and papers at their historical granularity.

## Current audit records

- `audit/full_repository_sync_2026-09-03.md` — whole-repository semantic/structural audit;
- `audit/FILE_INVENTORY_SUMMARY.json` — compact inventory summary; the full path-level inventory is regenerated on demand;
- `audit/PRODUCER_PATH_MAP.md` — historical producer-path resolution;
- `audit/CLAIM_STATUS_MAP.md` — compact claim-level status map;
- `audit/HISTORY_ENCODING_REPAIR.json` — before/after checksums and method for the verified historical-ledger encoding repair.

Do not discard an old document simply because a claim was superseded: the derivation, counterexample, numerical design, or literature trail may be exactly what the next proof attempt needs.
