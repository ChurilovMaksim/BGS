# Coboundary resonance: source audit and priority boundary

Date: 2026-09-05. This is a targeted primary-source review, not an exhaustive
priority certification.

## Direct precedent: residual perturbations and fidelity freeze

Tomaz Prosen and Marko Znidaric, *Quantum Freeze of Fidelity Decay for Chaotic
Dynamics*, Physical Review Letters 94 (2005), 044101,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.94.044101),
[primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0401142).

The publisher's record gives 31 January 2005. The preprint identifier and
version are from 2004; an unrelated later typesetting date in the PDF header
must not be used as the publication date. The full five-page preprint was
retrieved through alphaXiv; pages 2 and 3 were also visually checked in the
primary arXiv PDF.

Equation (4) writes a residual perturbation as a discrete coboundary.
Equations (6)--(7) telescope its first-order time sum and express its
second-order term through a commutator, with an endpoint commutator.
Equation (12) and the accompanying paragraph replace the perturbation strength
by its square divided by two in the effective description. The subsequent
fidelity laws use semiclassical mixing and factorization, with separate
exponential and Gaussian time regimes. These are substantial direct
precedents, not merely vaguely related citations.

What our proof does not claim to invent: coboundary cancellation, the
second-order commutator mechanism, residual fidelity freeze, or the occurrence
of fourth powers of a perturbation parameter in fidelity exponents.

What must be compared separately: a norm remainder valid for growing quantum
exponents; the exact two-species Pell collision classification; a deterministic
wrapped-normal spectral limit of an actual cat-map propagator at arithmetic
recurrences; and compact-test pair-statistics obstructions. The cited paper's
fidelity approximation is not used as a theorem proving any of those claims.

## Standard matrix-analysis machinery

Andrew M. Childs, Yuan Su, Minh C. Tran, Nathan Wiebe, Shuchen Zhu,
*Theory of Trotter Error with Commutator Scaling*, Physical Review X 11
(2021), 011020, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.11.011020).

The two-factor Duhamel estimate used for the generated observable is standard.
The project supplies an explicit proof for its specialized three-factor loop
and does not characterize commutator-sensitive product bounds as a new field
or a new universal Trotter theorem.

Greg Kuperberg, *A tracial quantum central limit theorem*, Transactions of the
AMS 357 (2005), 459--471,
[primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0202035).
The deterministic two-species array here is handled by direct word counting;
we do not assume an i.i.d. tensor-product hypothesis for it.

## Search limits and negative findings

Web searches for cat maps, coboundaries, residual perturbations, and Pell
recurrences returned no source establishing precisely the new statement, but
many results were irrelevant. This absence is weak evidence and is not a
priority argument. A broad alphaXiv discovery call also surfaced recent
quantum-cat papers, mostly about delocalization, Ehrenfest propagation, or
correlations, without resolving the priority question. Their abstracts were
not promoted to imported theorems.

The maintained conclusion is therefore a project proof with independently
checked finite components, not a first-ever or prize-level claim. Fixed
classical perturbations and positive BGS universality remain outside its scope.
