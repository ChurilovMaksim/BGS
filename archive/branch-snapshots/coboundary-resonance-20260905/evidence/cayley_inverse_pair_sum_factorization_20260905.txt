A. Generic polynomial red-team

For exact monic integer polynomials of degrees m=2,3,4,5,6, the prototype independently formed

  R(Z)=Res_X(F(X),F(Z-X)),
  D(Z)=2^m F(Z/2),

factored R(Z)/D(Z), and compared its monic square root S(Z) with

  Res_Y(E_Z(Y),O_Z(Y)),
  F(X+Z/2)=E_Z(X^2)+X O_Z(X^2).

The exact quotient between the translated parity resultant and monic S(Z) was:
  m=2: +1
  m=3: +1
  m=4: -1
  m=5: -1
  m=6: -1

Thus every tested generic case satisfies the theorem that the two polynomials differ only by a convention-dependent global sign.

B. Full cyclotomic pair-sum reference path

The direct route constructs the degree-m^2 inverse shift resultant, divides the diagonal factor, recovers the integral monic square root in Q(tau), and contracts (S(0),S'(0)). Observed exact contractions:

  K= 7: Theta=7,       Theta'=1
  K=11: Theta=121,     Theta'=1
  K=13: Theta=10985,   Theta'=5
  K=17: Theta=83521,   Theta'=1

Factorizations:
  10985 = 5 * 13^3
  83521 = 17^4

Representative wall-clock times in the same environment:
  K= 7: about 0.26 s
  K=11: about 2.51 s
  K=13: about 6.83 s
  K=17: about 50.85 s

The K=17 timing is the reason the full degree-m^2 route is not a default CI gate beyond small anchors.

C. Translated half-degree resultant route

Using only first-order translation

  E_eps = E + eps*(O+2Y O')/2,
  O_eps = O + eps*E',

and extracting rho_0,rho_1 from Res_Y(E_eps,O_eps), the exact Smith contractions were:

  K= 7: Theta=7,               Theta'=1
  K=11: Theta=121,             Theta'=1
  K=13: Theta=10985,           Theta'=5
  K=17: Theta=83521,           Theta'=1
  K=19: Theta=4821877,         Theta'=37
  K=23: Theta=44800545195737,  Theta'=302633

Factorizations:
  7 = 7
  121 = 11^2
  10985 = 5 * 13^3
  83521 = 17^4
  4821877 = 37 * 19^4
  44800545195737 = 23^6 * 47^2 * 137
  302633 = 47^2 * 137

Representative wall-clock times for the translated route:
  K= 7: about 0.030 s
  K=11: about 0.084 s
  K=13: about 0.167 s
  K=17: about 0.580 s
  K=19: about 1.36 s
  K=23: about 6.13 s

This is a large practical improvement over the full shift-resultant construction. At K=23 the translated resultant itself remains fast; the Smith contraction is already the dominant exact-algebra cost.

E. Negative performance control

A later attempt to push the translated-resultant Smith contraction to K=29/31 was deliberately interrupted when SymPy's integer Smith normal form became the bottleneck. No mathematical conclusion is drawn from that interruption. The next scalable implementation target is fraction-free / modular Smith or determinant arithmetic over Z[tau], not a larger symbolic full resultant.
