# Sp4 / rank-2 scope

The rank-2/Sp4 corpus is an active research direction, not a completed continuation theorem.

Use the stored `N=53` diagonal/coset/period-4 identities as exact finite-design calibration. The negative product-kernel result is also valuable: it rules out one naive factorised mechanism. Numerical `G0`, edge-mass, and trace-fluctuation probes should be used to design/falsify candidate uniform estimates.

Do not claim a general rank-2 flatness theorem, higher-genus Weil bound, M4d, or general BGS from these finite computations. Any promotion must state the exact family/parameters, prove the required uniform character-sum/representation estimate, and be independent of the invalid historical M1 and R86 promotions unless those are repaired separately.
