## 4. New mathematical observation found by the audit

Substituting `h' = h − u` reduces the two collision equations to the single
quadratic `h² − u·h + u/v = 0` in `h` alone; hence for every `v ≠ 0`

> **C(u,v) ≤ 2**, uniformly — strictly stronger than the paper's C(u,v) ≤ 4,

matching the red-team-C2 remark ("U2 actually sharpens"). Formalized in Lean
(`BgsAudit.collision_card_le_two`); the paper's constant 4 remains valid via
`collision_card_le_four`. Downstream uses need no change.
